## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, primary amenorrhea is defined as the absence of menarche by age 15 in girls with otherwise normal growth and secondary sexual characteristics, or by age 13 with no signs of puberty. It is investigated for genetic, hormonal, or structural causes, including ovarian failure, hypothalamic-pituitary disorders, and anatomical abnormalities of the reproductive tract. Diagnosis typically involves blood tests for hormone levels, imaging such as pelvic ultrasound, and sometimes genetic testing.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Treatment depends on the underlying cause. Hormone therapy with estrogen and progestin is often used to induce puberty and menstruation in cases of ovarian insufficiency. Surgical correction may be needed for structural anomalies like an imperforate hymen or Müllerian agenesis. For functional hypothalamic amenorrhea, addressing stress, nutritional deficiencies, or excessive exercise is the primary approach. Underlying conditions such as thyroid disease or prolactinoma are managed with specific medications.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM sees menstruation as a monthly expression of the body's deepest resources-Kidney Essence, Qi, and Blood. The first period depends on the arrival of Tian Gui, a reproductive essence stored in the Kidneys that initiates the menstrual cycle. When Kidney Essence is insufficient from birth or early life, the body simply isn't ready for menstruation, and puberty may be delayed. The tongue is often pale and the pulse deep and weak.
The Spleen is equally important because it produces the Qi and Blood that fill the uterus's vessels-the Chong and Ren channels. If the Spleen is weak due to poor diet, overwork, or constitutional factors, it can't generate enough Blood, so the uterus remains undernourished and empty. A pale, puffy tongue and a soft, weak pulse often accompany this pattern.
Even when essence and blood are sufficient, the Liver must ensure a smooth downward flow of Qi to move blood into the uterus. Emotional stress, frustration, or a tendency to Liver Qi stagnation can block this flow, preventing the period. Over time, stagnation can lead to blood stasis-a physical obstruction in the channels. And when Spleen weakness causes dampness to accumulate into phlegm, that sticky phlegm can lodge in the uterus and block menstruation. Each of these patterns requires a different treatment strategy.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the young woman’s overall development, not just the missing period. Kidney Essence is the root of reproductive growth, so signs like delayed breast development, a small or underdeveloped uterus, or a family history of late menarche point strongly to Kidney Essence Deficiency. The tongue is often pale and the pulse deep and weak, confirming that the body’s foundational reserves are simply not yet ready to trigger menstruation.
If instead the picture is one of general depletion - pale complexion, dizziness, poor memory, and a thin, pale tongue with a thready, weak pulse - the practitioner considers Qi and Blood Deficiency. Here the Chong and Ren vessels are under‑nourished because the raw materials (Qi and Blood) are insufficient. The woman may also report chronic fatigue, weak digestion, or a history of poor diet, which helps separate this pattern from a purely Kidney‑based problem.
When digestive symptoms dominate - bloating, loose stools, poor appetite, and a heavy tiredness after eating - Spleen Qi Deficiency is the likely driver. The tongue may be pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse feels weak, especially on the right wrist. The Spleen fails to transform food into enough Blood, and sometimes generates dampness that further obstructs the uterus, delaying the first period.
In contrast, a woman who experiences lower abdominal pain that feels fixed or stabbing, even without a period, and whose tongue is dark purple with red spots, suggests Blood Stagnation in the Chong and Ren vessels. The pulse is often wiry or choppy. This pattern is less common but important because it points to a physical blockage rather than a simple deficiency, and it often follows emotional stress or exposure to cold.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Kidney Essence Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-essence-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Delayed puberty with little breast development, Weak, sore lower back and knees, Dizziness and poor memory, Fatigue and lack of vitality, Pale, thin body with little or no coating
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Late nights and sleep deprivation, Excessive physical exercise, Poor diet lacking nourishment, Excessive worry
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and adequate sleep, Warm, nourishing meals, Gentle exercise (walking, yoga, etc.), Warmth and moxibustion
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Kidney Essence is the deepest foundational substance, governing growth, development, and the arrival of Tian Gui-the reproductive essence that triggers menstruation. When it is deficient from birth or depleted early in life, the Chong and Ren vessels remain empty and the uterus never receives the signal to begin its cycles, so the first period never comes.
This often shows as delayed puberty with little breast development and a thin, pale body, because the essence that should fuel maturation is simply not there.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, thin body with very little or no coating, possible fine cracks. Pulse: deep, fine (thin), especially weak at the Chi (rear) positions.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Essence is the body's deepest reserve, governing growth, development, and reproduction. Overwork, insufficient sleep, and excessive physical strain drain this reserve, delaying the arrival of Tian Gui and menstruation. Emotional stress further taxes the Kidneys, as fear and anxiety are said to damage Kidney Qi. Rest and sleep allow the body to conserve and rebuild essence. Nourishing foods like bone broth and black sesame directly supplement the essence, while gentle exercise promotes Qi flow without further depletion, supporting the gradual maturation of the reproductive system.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Prioritize deeply nourishing foods like bone broth, black sesame, walnuts, and goji berries. Avoid overwork, late nights, and excessive physical strain, which deplete Kidney Essence. Gentle exercises like Tai Chi or walking support vitality without draining reserves.

### Qi and Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: No period by age 16, Persistent fatigue and weakness, Pale or sallow complexion, Dizziness or lightheadedness, Heart palpitations
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Skipping meals or irregular eating, Raw, cold foods and iced drinks, Excessive worry, Irregular lifestyle
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and adequate sleep, Warm, nourishing meals, Gentle exercise (walking, yoga, etc.), Regular meal times, Stress reduction and relaxation
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Qi and Blood are the raw materials the body uses to build menstrual blood. When the Spleen is weak or nutrition is poor, the body cannot produce enough of these essentials. The Chong and Ren vessels remain undernourished, and the uterus stays empty, so the first period never arrives.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body, thin white coating, may be thin or have teeth marks. Pulse: fine (thin) and weak, especially weak in the right Guan position (Spleen).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Qi and Blood deficiency stems from a weak Spleen and insufficient nourishment. Rest and proper nutrition support the Spleen’s ability to produce Qi and Blood, while overwork, skipping meals, and cold foods further deplete or damage the Spleen, worsening the deficiency. Gentle movement helps Qi circulate without draining energy, and stress reduction prevents further consumption of Qi.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like soups, stews, and congees that are easy to digest and rich in iron and protein. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that weaken the Spleen. Gentle exercise such as walking or yoga can help Qi flow without depleting energy. Ensure adequate sleep and avoid overwork.

### Spleen Qi Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-qi-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Poor appetite or reduced food intake, Abdominal bloating, worse after eating, Loose or soft stools, Fatigue and lack of energy, Dull, pale or yellowish complexion, Heaviness or weakness in the limbs
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Skipping meals or irregular eating, Raw, cold foods and iced drinks, Overwork and chronic stress, Damp, cold environments
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, nourishing meals, Small, frequent meals, Rest and adequate sleep, Gentle exercise (walking, yoga, etc.)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is your body's main factory for Qi and Blood after birth. When Spleen Qi is weak, it cannot transform food into enough nourishing Blood to fill the uterus and the Chong and Ren vessels. This emptiness means there is simply not enough Blood to form a menstrual flow, so the first period never arrives.
At the same time, a sluggish Spleen often creates Dampness, which can further block the pathways that should carry Blood to the uterus. So the problem is both a lack of raw material and a blocked delivery route.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, and tender with tooth marks along the edges; thin white coating. Pulse: overall deficient and soft, especially weak at the right middle (Spleen/Stomach) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Spleen Qi is easily depleted by irregular eating, cold raw foods, and overwork. Warm, cooked foods require less digestive effort and directly support the Spleen's transformative function. Rest and gentle movement help preserve Qi and encourage its smooth flow, while damp and cold further stagnate a system already struggling to produce energy and blood.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat regular, warm, cooked meals - like soups, stews, and congees - that are easy to digest. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods, which further weaken the Spleen. Gentle daily movement like walking supports Qi flow without exhausting you, and prioritize rest to conserve energy.

### Liver Qi Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-qi-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Irritability, mood swings, or depression, Breast tenderness or swelling, Rib-side distension or pain, Frequent sighing, Worse with emotional stress
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Emotional stress and anger, Sedentary lifestyle, Alcohol and caffeine, Suppressing feelings
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Stress reduction and relaxation, Gentle exercise (walking, yoga, etc.), Peppermint or chrysanthemum tea, Deep breathing and sighing
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver is responsible for the smooth, free flow of Qi throughout the body, including the Chong and Ren vessels that govern menstruation. When emotional stress, frustration, or a constitutional tendency cause the Liver Qi to become stuck, this vital flow is obstructed. The Qi that should move blood downward to the uterus gets trapped, preventing the very first period from occurring. Over time, this stagnation can also lead to blood stasis, further blocking the menstrual pathway.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: normal or slightly dark body, red or slightly purple sides, thin white coat. Pulse: wiry (弦 xián), like a guitar string.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Liver Qi Stagnation is directly caused by emotional stress and a sedentary lifestyle, which constrict the free flow of Qi. Activities that calm the mind, move the body, and express emotions help unblock the Liver channel, allowing Qi to flow smoothly to the uterus. Peppermint and chrysanthemum have a gentle soothing effect on the Liver, while alcohol and greasy foods create heat and dampness that further obstruct Qi.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Prioritize stress management through meditation, yoga, or gentle walks. Avoid excessive alcohol, caffeine, and spicy or greasy foods which can aggravate Liver stagnation. Regular aerobic exercise helps move Qi. Create a calming evening routine to unwind before sleep.

### Blood Stagnation in the Directing and Penetrating Vessels
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation-in-the-directing-and-penetrating-vessels)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing lower abdominal pain, Pain that worsens with pressure, Breast tenderness or distension before an expected cycle, Dark purple or dusky tongue with stasis spots, Palpable lumps or masses in the lower abdomen
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold exposure or cold foods, Stress, frustration, and emotional suppression, Sedentary lifestyle, Greasy, heavy, or raw foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth on the lower abdomen, Gentle exercise (walking, yoga, etc.), Stress reduction and relaxation, Warming foods and spices (ginger, cinnamon)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  In this pattern, static blood physically blocks the Chong and Ren vessels-the two channels most responsible for menstruation. The obstruction prevents menstrual blood from descending, so the first period never arrives. This stasis often develops from long-standing Liver Qi stagnation, emotional stress, or exposure to cold, all of which can congeal blood and trap it in the lower abdomen.
Because the blockage is solid and fixed, any pain that does appear is sharp, stabbing, and located in one spot. The body’s signs-a dark purple tongue, engorged veins under the tongue, and a choppy pulse-all point to blood that is stuck rather than flowing smoothly.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple or dusky body, often with purplish stasis spots; engorged, tortuous sublingual veins; thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (rough, uneven, like a knife scraping bamboo) and often wiry, especially at the left middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold causes blood to congeal and stagnate, so warmth and movement are essential to dissolve the blockage. Emotional stress knots the Liver Qi, which is the leading cause of blood stasis; relaxation and expression help the Qi flow smoothly again. Gentle exercise physically pumps blood through the pelvis, while a sedentary habit lets stasis settle and harden.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the lower abdomen warm; avoid cold, raw foods and icy drinks. Gentle daily movement-walking, yoga, or stretching-helps keep blood moving. Add warming spices like ginger, cinnamon, and turmeric to meals. Prioritize stress management and emotional release, as held-in feelings can worsen stasis.

### Damp-Phlegm in the Uterus
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-phlegm-in-the-uterus)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Overweight or obesity, Heavy, full sensation in the lower abdomen, Copious thick white vaginal discharge, Poor appetite and loose, sticky stools, Chest stuffiness and nausea, Fatigue and drowsiness
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Greasy, fried, or sweet foods, Damp or humid weather, Sedentary lifestyle, Overeating or irregular meals
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Light, warm meals, Gentle exercise (walking, yoga, etc.), Dry, warm weather, Avoiding dairy and sweets
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen is weak and fails to transform fluids, dampness builds up and gradually condenses into phlegm. This sticky phlegm-dampness settles in the uterus, physically blocking the Chong and Ren vessels and preventing the menstrual blood from flowing. The result is that the first period never arrives, often in a young woman who also struggles with weight gain and a heavy, bloated sensation in the lower abdomen.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Pale, swollen body with teeth marks and a thick, white, greasy coating (especially at the root). Pulse: Slippery (滑 huá), deep (沉 chén), and possibly soft.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Phlegm-dampness arises from Spleen weakness and the accumulation of fluids. Greasy, sweet, and heavy foods further burden the Spleen and generate more dampness, while a sedentary lifestyle slows Qi movement and fluid metabolism. Light, warm foods and regular exercise support the Spleen's transforming function and help dry and move dampness, clearing the uterine channels.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, fried, sweet, and dairy foods that create dampness. Eat warm, lightly cooked meals with plenty of vegetables and moderate amounts of whole grains. Gentle, regular exercise like walking or yoga helps the Spleen transform fluids.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture and herbs really bring on my first period?
Yes, when the underlying pattern is correctly identified and addressed. TCM doesn't just force a bleed-it gently rebuilds the body's own capacity to cycle. Many young women who have never had a period experience their first natural menstruation after several months of consistent treatment. The key is patience: deep deficiency takes time to fill, and blockages need to be carefully cleared.

### How long does TCM treatment take for primary amenorrhea?
It varies by pattern. Deficiency patterns (Kidney Essence, Qi and Blood) usually need 3 to 6 months of daily herbs and weekly acupuncture. Excess patterns (stagnation, phlegm) often improve within 1 to 3 months. Consistency is crucial-missing doses or skipping sessions can slow progress. Once a regular cycle is established, treatment may be tapered and eventually stopped.

### Is it safe to combine TCM with hormone therapy?
Generally, yes, but it's essential to inform both your doctor and TCM practitioner about all treatments. Some herbs, like Dang Gui (Angelica sinensis) and Chuan Xiong (Ligusticum), have mild blood-moving effects and should be used carefully with anticoagulants. Your TCM practitioner can adjust the formula to avoid interactions. Never stop prescribed hormones abruptly without your doctor's guidance.

### What can I do at home to support treatment?
Diet is a powerful ally. Avoid cold, raw foods and icy drinks-they weaken the Spleen and constrict the uterus. Instead, eat warm, cooked meals like soups, stews, and congee. Include blood-nourishing foods such as dark leafy greens, eggs, and small amounts of red meat. Gentle exercise like walking or yoga helps move Qi without depleting reserves. Managing stress through deep breathing or meditation is also important, especially if Liver Qi stagnation is a factor.

### My daughter is 16 and hasn't started her period-should I be worried?
It's worth investigating, but not necessarily alarming. A TCM evaluation can often identify a pattern even when standard tests show no clear cause. Many girls with constitutional weakness or delayed development respond well to gentle herbal and dietary support. Combined with a Western medical check-up to rule out structural or chromosomal issues, TCM offers a safe, holistic way to encourage the body's natural timing.

### Will I need to take herbs forever?
No. The goal is to restore your body's own rhythm so that it cycles naturally without ongoing treatment. Once a regular period is established for a few months, herbs are gradually reduced and then stopped. Some women benefit from occasional tune-ups, but long-term daily herbs are rarely necessary for this condition.

### Can TCM help if I have a diagnosed condition like Turner syndrome?
TCM cannot change chromosomal abnormalities, but it can support overall vitality and may help induce some bleeding when combined with hormone therapy. Realistic expectations are important-treatment focuses on improving energy, digestion, and general well-being rather than achieving a fully natural cycle. Your practitioner will work with your medical team to provide safe, complementary care.
