## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Vaginitis is an inflammation of the vagina, often caused by an imbalance in the normal vaginal flora, an infection (bacterial, yeast, or trichomoniasis), or irritation from chemicals or hygiene products. Common symptoms include abnormal discharge, itching, burning, and discomfort. Diagnosis typically involves a pelvic exam, pH test, and microscopic examination of the discharge to identify the cause.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Treatment depends on the cause: antifungal creams or oral tablets for yeast infections, antibiotics (oral or vaginal) for bacterial vaginosis, and specific anti-parasitic medication for trichomoniasis. Over-the-counter remedies are available for some yeast infections, but recurrent or complicated cases require prescription medication.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM sees vaginitis as a sign of dampness sinking downward into the lower burner, the area below the navel. The Ren (Conception) and Dai (Belt) meridians are supposed to hold and guide vaginal fluids, but when dampness overwhelms them, they lose control and discharge pours out. Dampness can come from outside - like wearing wet clothes or sitting on damp ground - but far more often it is generated internally by weak digestion or a sluggish metabolism.
The Spleen and Kidney are the two organs most central to this condition. The Spleen transforms fluids; if it is weak, dampness builds up. The Kidney provides the body's warming fire; if it is low, cold-dampness forms and leaks downward. Sometimes dampness combines with heat, creating a yellow, thick, foul-smelling discharge with intense itching - the Damp-Heat pattern. Other times the discharge is thin, white, and odorless, pointing to Spleen or Kidney deficiency.
This is why TCM never treats vaginitis as just a local infection. The discharge is a mirror of the whole body's balance. A woman with a yellow, itchy discharge and a red tongue is not the same as a woman with a clear, watery discharge and a pale, puffy tongue, even though both have vaginitis. Each needs a different strategy - clearing heat, draining dampness, strengthening the Spleen, or warming the Kidney - to not only stop the discharge but also correct the terrain so it does not return.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner starts by looking closely at the vaginal discharge itself-its color, consistency, smell, and amount-because these qualities point directly to the underlying imbalance. They also ask about accompanying sensations like itching, burning, or coldness, as well as your energy, digestion, and any pelvic pain. The tongue and pulse provide crucial confirmation of what the discharge is already suggesting.
If the discharge is yellow, thick, and has a strong, unpleasant odor, with intense itching and a burning sensation, the pattern is likely Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner. This is an excess pattern where dampness and heat have accumulated and are pouring downward. The tongue will often be red with a yellow, greasy coating, and the pulse feels slippery and rapid, like a rushing stream.
When the discharge is profuse but pale white or slightly yellow, thin, and odorless, and you feel generally tired with poor appetite and loose stools, the root is Spleen Deficiency with Dampness. The Spleen’s ability to transform and transport fluids has weakened, allowing dampness to collect. The tongue is pale and puffy, often with a white, moist coating, and the pulse is soft and weak, especially on the right side.
A clear, watery discharge that feels cold to the body, accompanied by a deep, achy lower back, cold limbs, and frequent nighttime urination points to Kidney Yang Deficiency. The Kidney’s warming and transforming function is too weak to hold fluids, so they leak downward. The tongue is pale, possibly with teeth marks on the edges, and the pulse is deep and thin, like a thread that is hard to find.
In some cases, the discharge is scanty, yellow or tinged with blood, and thicker, with a sensation of heat and dryness in the vagina. This is often worse at night, alongside night sweats, a dry mouth, and a feeling of heat in the palms and soles. The tongue is red with little or no coating, and the pulse is thin and rapid, indicating Kidney Yin Deficiency with empty heat.
When an infection becomes severe, the discharge turns yellow-green like pus, smells foul, and is accompanied by pelvic pain and sometimes fever. This Toxic-Heat pattern is a dangerous progression where dampness and heat have congealed into toxicity. The tongue is red with a yellow, dry coating, and the pulse is rapid and forceful. This requires immediate professional care.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Yellow or greenish, thick, foul-smelling vaginal discharge, Intense vulvar itching and burning, Lower abdominal heaviness and distension, Burning or stinging during urination, Thirst with no desire to drink much, Feeling of heaviness in the legs
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot and humid weather, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol and caffeine, Prolonged sitting, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool sitz baths, Eating mung bean soup or barley water, Wearing breathable, loose clothing, Keeping the area dry and clean, Gentle exercise
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When dampness and heat accumulate in the lower body, they pour downward into the genital region, overwhelming the Ren (Conception) and Dai (Belt) meridians. These vessels normally govern the vaginal environment, but the damp-heat disrupts their function, causing a profuse, yellow, foul-smelling discharge with itching and burning. The heat irritates the tissues, while the dampness creates heaviness and a sensation of fullness in the lower abdomen. This is the classic acute vaginitis presentation in TCM.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a yellow greasy coating, thickest at the root. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and rapid (数 shuò), more pronounced at the rear position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-heat thrives on heat and moisture, so hot, humid weather and damp environments worsen the condition by adding external damp-heat to the internal imbalance. Spicy, greasy, and sweet foods directly generate internal dampness and heat, while alcohol is pure heat that fuels the fire. Emotional stress stagnates Liver Qi, which can transform into heat and combine with dampness. Cooling sitz baths, drying clothing, and damp-resolving foods help drain the excess damp-heat, while gentle movement keeps Qi flowing so dampness doesn't settle in the lower burner.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid rich, greasy, spicy, and sweet foods that create dampness and heat. Instead, eat cooling, damp-resolving foods like mung beans, barley, bitter melon, and celery. Keep the genital area clean and dry; wear loose, breathable cotton underwear and change it promptly after sweating. Avoid prolonged sitting, especially on damp surfaces, and consider a cool sitz bath with a mild herbal decoction such as Huang Bai to soothe itching.

### Spleen Deficiency with Dampness
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-deficiency-with-dampness)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Profuse, thin, white or pale, odorless vaginal discharge, Fatigue and lack of energy, especially after meals, Loose stools or diarrhoea, Poor appetite with abdominal bloating and fullness, Heaviness of the body and limbs, head feels muzzy
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, raw foods and iced drinks, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Overwork and exhaustion, Hot and humid weather, Prolonged sitting
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Eating warm, cooked foods, Ginger or cinnamon tea, Rest and adequate sleep, Gentle exercise, Dry, warm environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is responsible for transforming and transporting fluids in the body. When Spleen Qi is weak, it can no longer manage this job effectively, and unused fluids accumulate as dampness. Dampness is heavy and naturally sinks downward, settling in the lower burner and damaging the Ren and Dai meridians, which regulate the vaginal area. This leads to a chronic, profuse, thin, white or pale discharge that is typically odorless, because there is no heat involved.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks, white greasy coating, moist surface. Pulse: soggy (Ru) and slow (Huan), soft and lacking force.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Spleen Qi is responsible for transforming fluids. Cold and raw foods damage Spleen Yang, worsening dampness and discharge. Warmth, rest, and easily digestible foods support Spleen Qi, helping it to manage fluids. Damp environments add external dampness, overloading the already weak Spleen. Gentle movement encourages Qi flow, preventing stagnation, while overwork depletes Qi further.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked meals like soups, stews, and congees. Avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods that create dampness. Include ginger, cinnamon, and cardamom to warm the Spleen. Gentle exercise like walking helps Qi flow. Keep the lower body warm and dry, and avoid sitting on cold or damp surfaces.

### Kidney Yang Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-yang-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Clear, watery, cold-feeling discharge, Soreness and cold pain in the lower back and knees, Frequent urination, especially waking at night, Feeling cold all over, worse in the lower body and abdomen
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, raw foods and iced drinks, Cold, damp weather, Overwork and exhaustion, Excessive sexual activity, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth on the lower abdomen and back, Eating warm, cooked foods, Rest and adequate sleep, Moxibustion on the lower back
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Kidney Yang is weak, the body's ability to warm and transform fluids declines. Cold-dampness pools in the lower burner because there is not enough internal fire to vaporize and move it. This excess cold fluid overflows downward, producing a clear, watery, cold-feeling vaginal discharge. The lack of warmth also leads to the characteristic lower back and knee soreness and frequent nighttime urination.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, and tender with teeth marks and a wet white coating. Pulse: deep, slow, and weak, especially at the rear (Chi) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Yang is the body's internal fire. Cold and dampness directly extinguish it, while warmth and moxibustion add fuel. Rest conserves Yang, and overwork or excessive sexual activity depletes it. Eating warm foods and avoiding raw cold items supports the digestive fire, which in turn helps the Kidneys transform fluids and stop the watery discharge.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the lower back and abdomen warm at all times. Eat warming, cooked foods like lamb stew, ginger, and cinnamon; avoid raw, cold, and frozen items completely. Gentle daily movement such as walking or tai chi helps circulate Yang without exhausting it.

Go to bed before 11 p.m. - late nights directly drain Kidney Yin - and build in short, calming rests during the day.

### Toxic-Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/toxic-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Yellow-green, pus-like discharge, Foul, fishy odor, Pelvic pain and lower back soreness, Fever or feeling of heat, Restlessness and intense thirst
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol and caffeine, Hot and humid weather, Prolonged sitting
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cooling foods and adequate hydration, Rest and adequate sleep, Wearing breathable, loose clothing
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When dampness and heat accumulate in the lower burner without resolution, they can intensify into a toxic state. This toxic heat damages the delicate tissues of the vagina and cervix, causing pus formation, a foul odor, and pelvic pain. It is like a severe, smoldering infection that the body struggles to contain, often arising from untreated or recurrent damp-heat conditions.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with dry yellow coating, possibly prickly. Pulse: rapid and forceful, often slippery in the lower burner.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Spicy and greasy foods generate internal Heat and Dampness, which are the very fuels that feed Toxic-Heat in the lower burner. Alcohol and caffeine further dry out fluids and aggravate Heat. Cooling foods and hydration help to clear Heat and support the body's detoxification. Rest allows Qi to focus on fighting the infection, while loose clothing and gentle hygiene prevent external irritation and dampness from worsening the condition.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol and caffeine, which generate internal heat and dampness. Eat plenty of cooling, water-rich foods like cucumber, watermelon, and mung beans. Keep the genital area clean and dry, wear loose cotton underwear, and avoid douching or harsh soaps that can disrupt the natural balance.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture really help with vaginal itching and discharge?
Yes. Acupuncture is used to drain dampness, clear heat, and strengthen the organs that control fluids. Points along the lower abdomen and legs help regulate the Ren and Dai meridians, while points on the back and feet support the Spleen and Kidney. Many women notice a reduction in itching and discharge after just a few sessions, especially when combined with herbs.

### How long will it take for the herbs to work?
Most women begin to feel relief within 1-3 weeks. Acute infections with yellow discharge and heat signs tend to respond faster. Chronic, thin, watery discharge from a deficiency pattern may take longer, because the herbs are rebuilding the body's energy, not just clearing an infection. Consistency is key - take the formula as prescribed and return for follow-up adjustments.

### Can I use TCM alongside antibiotics or antifungals?
Yes, TCM can be safely combined with conventional medications. Herbal formulas that clear heat and drain dampness rarely interact with antibiotics or antifungals, but always tell both your TCM practitioner and your doctor what you are taking. If you are using a topical cream, coordinate timing with any external herbal wash your practitioner may recommend.

### Will the discharge come back after TCM treatment?
Because TCM aims to correct the underlying imbalance - not just kill the pathogen - recurrence is less likely. However, if the same dietary or lifestyle habits that created the dampness return, the discharge may return as well. Your practitioner will guide you on how to maintain a healthy internal environment through diet and self-care.

### What foods should I avoid if I have vaginitis?
In general, avoid foods that create dampness and heat: dairy products, greasy or fried foods, spicy dishes, alcohol, and excessive sugar. Cold, raw foods can also weaken the Spleen and promote dampness. Instead, eat warm, cooked meals with plenty of vegetables and moderate amounts of whole grains. Your practitioner may give you more specific advice based on your pattern.

### Is TCM safe for vaginitis during pregnancy?
Yes, but you must tell your practitioner you are pregnant. Many acupuncture points and herbs are contraindicated during pregnancy, so your treatment will be adjusted. A qualified TCM practitioner will choose only pregnancy-safe points and gentle herbal formulas. Always also consult your obstetrician.
