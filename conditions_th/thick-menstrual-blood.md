## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, thick or sticky menstrual blood is often noted as a variation of normal menstrual flow, but it can be a sign of underlying conditions such as uterine fibroids, endometriosis, adenomyosis, or hormonal imbalances that affect the consistency of the uterine lining.
The thickness may be due to a higher concentration of blood clots, tissue fragments, or changes in the viscosity of the menstrual fluid. Diagnosis typically involves a pelvic exam, ultrasound, and sometimes blood tests to rule out clotting disorders or thyroid issues.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatments for thick menstrual blood depend on the underlying cause. Hormonal birth control pills, progestin-only therapies, or the levonorgestrel-releasing IUD can regulate the menstrual cycle and reduce heavy or clotted flow.
Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) like ibuprofen can ease cramping and may reduce blood loss. If structural issues like fibroids or polyps are present, surgical options such as hysteroscopy or myomectomy may be recommended.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM views menstrual blood as a mirror of the body's internal environment. The uterus relies on the smooth flow of Qi and Blood, which is governed by the Liver, Spleen, and Kidneys through the Chong and Ren channels. When these organ systems are in harmony, menstrual blood flows freely with a healthy consistency.
But when heat, stagnation, or dampness disrupts that balance, the blood's quality changes - becoming thick, sticky, clotted, or stringy - and the body sends other signals like thirst, irritability, or bloating.
Heat is the most common culprit. Whether it's excess heat from emotional stress, spicy food, or an external pathogen, or a deficiency heat from depleted Yin, heat scorches the blood. This makes the menstrual flow thick, dark red or purple, and often heavy.
A woman with this pattern might feel hot, thirsty for cold drinks, and notice a red tongue with a yellow coating. The heat forces the period to arrive early and can make the flow difficult to ignore.
When Qi doesn't move freely - often due to chronic stress or frustration - blood stagnates. This stagnation causes menstrual blood to pool and congeal, producing a thick, dark, and clotted discharge with a fixed, stabbing pain that eases once clots pass. The tongue may show purple spots, and the pulse feels wiry or choppy.
Meanwhile, a weak Spleen that fails to transform fluids allows Dampness and Phlegm to accumulate in the uterus, mixing with the blood to create a gluey, stringy consistency accompanied by a heavy, bloated sensation and a greasy tongue coating.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner first looks at the colour and consistency of the menstrual blood, then asks about pain, flow volume, and whole-body signs. Thick blood points toward heat, stasis, or phlegm-dampness, but each underlying pattern has its own fingerprint.
If the blood is dark red or purple, sticky, and the flow is heavy with a feeling of heat and thirst, the pattern is likely Heat in Uterus Blood. The tongue appears red with a yellow coat, and the pulse feels rapid and slippery.
When the blood is dark purple with many clots and is accompanied by sharp, stabbing lower abdominal pain, Blood Stagnation is the key. The tongue may show purple spots, and the pulse is often wiry or choppy.
Emotional stress that triggers irritability, breast distension, and a dark red thick flow points to Liver Qi Stagnation that transforms into Heat. The tongue is red with a thin yellow coating, and the pulse is wiry and rapid.
A scanty but thick and bright-red flow, together with night sweats, a dry mouth, and a feeling of heat in the palms and soles, suggests Empty-Heat caused by Yin Deficiency. The tongue is red with little coating, and the pulse is thready and rapid.
If the blood is thick, gluey, or stringy, and the person feels heavy or has a greasy tongue coating and a slippery pulse, Damp-Phlegm in the Uterus is the likely pattern.

### Heat in Uterus Blood
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-in-uterus-blood)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Heavy menstrual flow with dark red, sticky blood, Periods arrive early, often by a week or more, Feeling of heat in the lower abdomen, Intense thirst for cold drinks, Flushed face and sensation of internal heat, Restlessness and irritability
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy and greasy foods, Alcohol and coffee, Stress and anger, Hot weather, Overexertion
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cooling foods and drinks, Rest and relaxation, Cool environment, Gentle exercise like walking
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When excess heat builds up in the uterus, it scorches the blood, causing it to become thick, sticky, and dark red or purplish. This internal heat also forces the period to arrive early and can make the flow heavy and difficult to ignore. The same heat rises to the face, stirs up irritability, and creates an intense thirst for cold drinks.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a redder tip, possible prickles, thin yellow coating that tends to be dry. Pulse: rapid (Shu) and wiry (Xian), may be slippery (Hua).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat is the driving force behind this pattern-it scorches the blood and makes it thick and sticky. Spicy foods, alcohol, stress, and hot environments all add heat to the body, worsening the condition. Cooling foods, rest, and a calm environment help drain that excess heat, allowing the blood to return to a normal consistency and flow. Gentle movement prevents stagnation without generating more internal fire.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and deep-fried foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which add fuel to the internal fire. Instead, reach for cooling foods like cucumber, watermelon, mung beans, and leafy greens. Stay well hydrated with room-temperature or cool water. Gentle walking or stretching helps Qi flow, but avoid intense exercise that can overheat the body. A calm, cool environment and stress management are especially important.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing lower abdominal pain, Dark purple or blackish blood with large clots, Pain worse with pressure and at night, Dark circles under the eyes or purplish nails
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold exposure and iced drinks, Emotional stress and frustration, Prolonged sitting or inactivity
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Applying warmth to the lower abdomen, Gentle exercise, Stress reduction
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Qi does not move freely, blood cannot flow smoothly. In the uterus, this stagnation causes menstrual blood to pool and congeal, producing a thick, dark, and clotted discharge. The obstruction creates a characteristic stabbing pain that feels fixed in one place.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body with stasis spots, distended dark sublingual veins. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè) or wiry (弦 xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold causes blood vessels to constrict and Qi to slow, making stasis worse; warmth and movement encourage circulation and break up clots. Emotional stress knots the Liver Qi, which is responsible for moving blood, so calming the mind directly eases the obstruction and pain.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the lower abdomen warm and avoid iced drinks or raw foods, which can congeal blood. Gentle daily movement like walking or yoga helps keep Qi and blood flowing. Manage stress with regular relaxation, as emotional tension directly tightens the Liver Qi and worsens stasis.

### Liver Qi Stagnation that transforms into Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-qi-stagnation-that-transforms-into-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Irritability and explosive anger before period, Distending pain or tenderness in the ribcage and breasts, Bitter taste in the mouth, Thick, sticky menstrual blood, dark red or purplish
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Anger and frustration, Spicy, greasy foods, Alcohol and coffee, Prolonged stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Stress reduction, Cooling foods (cucumber, mint), Gentle exercise
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Emotional stress and frustration cause the Liver Qi to stagnate. Over time, this stuck Qi generates heat, which then enters the blood. The heat scorches the menstrual blood, making it thick, sticky, and dark red or purplish. The flow may also be heavy or contain clots, and it is typically preceded by a build-up of irritability, breast tenderness, and a bitter taste in the mouth.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with redder sides and a yellow coating. Pulse: wiry and rapid (弦数, xián shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Emotional stress and anger directly fuel Liver Qi stagnation, which then generates heat-exactly the mechanism that thickens menstrual blood. Cooling foods and calming habits soothe the Liver and clear the heat, while gentle movement keeps Qi flowing smoothly so it does not get stuck and turn into fire.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which add heat and stir up Liver Qi. Incorporate cooling, calming foods like celery, cucumber, mint tea, and chrysanthemum tea. Build a daily wind-down routine-gentle yoga, walking, or meditation-to release emotional tension before it stagnates.

### Empty-Heat caused by Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/empty-heat-caused-by-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Scanty but thick and sticky menstrual blood, bright red, Night sweats or heat sensation in palms, soles, and chest (five-palm heat), Dry mouth and throat, worse at night, Flushed cheekbones, especially in the afternoon or evening, Tidal low-grade fever or feeling of heat in the afternoon, Restlessness and irritability, difficulty sleeping
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy or fried foods, Late nights and overwork, Emotional stress, Hot weather or saunas
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Getting enough sleep, Cooling foods and drinks, Gentle, non-sweaty exercise, Quiet, dark environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the body's Yin is depleted-often from overwork, chronic stress, or insufficient rest-it fails to anchor fluids and cool the blood. An empty, low-grade heat arises that gently scorches the menstrual blood, making it thick and sticky. Because Yin and Blood are both deficient, the flow is typically scanty and bright red, and is accompanied by classic signs of Yin emptiness like night sweats and a dry mouth.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin, dry body with cracks, little or no coating. Pulse: fine (thin) and rapid.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Yin deficiency means the body’s coolant and moisture are depleted. Spicy foods, late nights, and emotional stress further consume Yin and stir up empty heat, which thickens the menstrual blood. Rest, cooling foods, and gentle activity help replenish Yin and calm the internal heat, restoring a normal flow.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Prioritize rest and avoid staying up late, which further drains Yin. Incorporate cooling, moistening foods like pear, black sesame, goji berries, and cucumber. Steer clear of spicy, fried, and overly heating foods. Gentle exercise like yoga or walking can help, but avoid intense workouts that cause excessive sweating.

### Damp-Phlegm in the Uterus
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-phlegm-in-the-uterus)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Gluey or stringy menstrual blood, Scanty or delayed periods, Profuse thick white vaginal discharge, Lower abdominal heaviness and fullness, Overweight or weight gain, Fatigue and drowsiness, Poor appetite and loose stools
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold and raw foods, Damp or humid weather, Sedentary lifestyle, Overeating or heavy meals, Excessive worry or overthinking
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, cooked meals, Gentle daily movement, Dry, warm environment, Light, non-greasy foods, Applying warmth to lower abdomen
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen is weak, it fails to transform and transport body fluids. These fluids accumulate into Dampness and then condense into Phlegm, which settles in the uterus. The menstrual blood mixes with this thick, turbid Phlegm-Dampness, giving it a sticky, gluey, or stringy consistency. Because Phlegm obstructs the free flow of Qi and blood, periods may also be scanty or delayed.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks and a white greasy coating thicker at the root. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and deep (沉 chén), possibly slow or soft.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold and raw foods damage the Spleen's yang energy, leading to more Dampness and Phlegm. Damp weather and a sedentary lifestyle both allow fluids to stagnate and condense. Overeating and worry further weaken the Spleen's ability to transform and transport. In contrast, warmth and gentle movement stimulate the Spleen and help it dry up Dampness. Warm, cooked meals are easy to digest and do not produce phlegm, while light, non-greasy foods prevent the accumulation of turbid fluids.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods that burden the Spleen and generate Dampness. Favor warm, cooked meals like soups and stews with mild spices such as ginger and pepper. Gentle daily movement-like walking or yoga-helps Qi circulate and transform fluids. Keep the lower abdomen warm and avoid sitting on cold surfaces.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture and herbs really change the thickness of my period blood?
Yes. By cooling heat, moving stagnation, or transforming dampness, TCM works to restore the quality of your menstrual blood. Many women notice a lighter, less clotted flow within one to two cycles of consistent treatment.

### Is thick menstrual blood a sign of endometriosis?
It can be. In TCM, endometriosis often involves blood stagnation, which can cause thick, dark, clotted menstrual blood. However, thick blood can also arise from heat or dampness. A TCM practitioner will assess your full pattern, not just the blood quality, to determine the root cause.

### Will I need to change my diet?
Dietary adjustments can greatly support your treatment. In general, avoid spicy, greasy, and rich foods that create heat and dampness. Favour cooling, blood-nourishing foods like cucumber, spinach, and mung beans. Your practitioner may give more specific advice tailored to your pattern.

### How soon will I see results?
Excess patterns like Heat in Uterus Blood often respond quickly - improvement may be seen in the first or second cycle. Blood Stagnation can take two to three cycles. Deficiency patterns (Yin deficiency or Spleen weakness) typically require three to six months of consistent herbs and acupuncture to rebuild reserves and change the blood's consistency.

### Can I use TCM alongside birth control pills?
Yes. TCM herbs generally do not interfere with contraceptive effectiveness. However, always inform both your TCM practitioner and your doctor about all medications you're taking, especially if you use anticoagulants, as some blood-moving herbs may interact.

### Does thick blood mean I have a blood stagnation problem?
Not always. While blood stagnation is a common cause, thick blood can also be due to heat scorching the blood or damp-phlegm mixing with the flow. The colour, accompanying pain, and overall symptoms help differentiate the pattern.

### Are there any warning signs I should watch for?
If your thick menstrual blood is accompanied by severe pelvic pain, fever, soaking through a pad every hour, or large clots, seek urgent medical care. For a full list of red flags, see our Safety section.

## การวิจัยทางวิทยาศาสตร์สมัยใหม่ (Modern Scientific Research)
1. **Chinese herbal medicine for primary dysmenorrhoea** ([ลิงก์](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD005288.pub2))
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhu X, Proctor M, Bensoussan A, Wu E, Smith CA. Chinese herbal medicine for primary dysmenorrhoea. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2007, Issue 4. Art. No.: CD005288.*
   This Cochrane systematic review evaluated 39 randomized controlled trials involving 3,475 women. It found that Chinese herbal medicine reduced menstrual pain more effectively than placebo, no treatment, or NSAIDs. Many of the studied formulas contained blood-moving and heat-clearing herbs like Dang Gui, Chuan Xiong, and Mu Dan Pi, which address the stasis and heat patterns underlying thick menstrual blood.

2. **Acupuncture for primary dysmenorrhoea: a systematic review** ([ลิงก์](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1471-0528.2011.02973.x))
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Smith CA, Armour M, Zhu X, Li X, Lu ZY, Song J. Acupuncture for primary dysmenorrhoea: a systematic review. BJOG 2011;118(8):943-955.*
   This systematic review of 27 RCTs concluded that acupuncture significantly reduced menstrual pain compared to sham acupuncture or no treatment. Improvements in pain were accompanied by better menstrual blood quality in several studies, suggesting that acupuncture may help normalize thick, clotted flow by resolving blood stasis and regulating Qi.
