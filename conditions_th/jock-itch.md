## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Jock itch (tinea cruris) is a common fungal infection of the groin, inner thighs, and buttocks. It's caused by dermatophytes - the same fungi that cause athlete's foot - and thrives in warm, moist environments. The classic symptoms are a red, itchy, often ring-shaped rash with a scaly border, and it's more common in men, athletes, and people who sweat heavily. Diagnosis is usually straightforward based on appearance, though a skin scraping can confirm it under a microscope.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment relies on topical antifungal creams, such as clotrimazole or terbinafine, which clear most cases within a few weeks. More stubborn infections may need prescription-strength creams or oral antifungal pills. Patients are also advised to keep the area clean and dry, wear loose cotton underwear, and avoid sharing towels.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the groin is a unique crossroads. The Liver channel runs along the inner thigh, and the Spleen's energy governs the flesh and muscles.
This area is naturally prone to dampness - a heavy, sinking energy that can pool in the lower body, especially when the Spleen's digestive power is weak or when external humidity invades. When dampness combines with heat (from spicy food, alcohol, or emotional stress), it creates the hot, wet, intensely itchy rash of acute jock itch. This is the Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner pattern, where the body's drainage system is overwhelmed and the groin becomes inflamed and weepy.
When the condition drags on or is treated repeatedly with drying medications, the body's blood and fluids can become depleted. The skin in the groin loses its nourishment and becomes dry, scaly, and fragile. TCM calls this Blood Deficiency, and the resulting dryness generates an internal 'Wind' - a restless, moving force that causes itching that shifts and intensifies, especially at night.
This is why some people have a chronic, dry itch that never seems to fully go away, even after the fungus is gone.
In a minority of cases, long-standing damp-heat can congeal the blood, leading to Blood Stagnation with Heat. The patches turn dark red or purplish, and the itch feels fixed and stabbing. This pattern reflects deeper, more stubborn blockage in the local circulation and requires a different approach - moving the blood and clearing heat, rather than simply drying dampness or nourishing blood.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner starts by asking what the rash looks and feels like, when the itching is worst, and whether there are any whole-body clues like thirst, urine color, or sleep changes. The tongue and pulse then confirm which pattern is driving the jock itch.
If the groin is red, swollen, and oozing yellow fluid with a fierce, burning itch, the picture is Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner. The tongue is red with a thick, yellow, greasy coating, and the pulse feels slippery and rapid. You may also notice a bitter taste in the mouth and dark, scanty urine.
When the condition has dragged on and the skin becomes dry, thickened, and scaly, with itching that worsens at night, it points to Blood Deficiency with External Wind. The tongue is pale or light red with a thin white coat, and the pulse is thin. This pattern often appears in people who feel tired or have had repeated flare-ups.
If the patches look dark red or purplish, with visible tiny broken veins or bleeding points, Blood Stagnation with Heat is likely. The tongue may show dark spots or a purplish tint, and the pulse feels wiry and choppy. This pattern develops when long-standing damp-heat congeals the blood.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Red, moist rash with oozing blisters, Intense itching, worse with heat and sweating, Groin and inner thigh, Heavy, dragging sensation in the legs, Thick yellow greasy coating at the back of the tongue
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot, humid weather, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol, Tight, non-breathable clothing, Prolonged sitting, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, dry weather, Loose, breathable clothing, Cooling, damp-draining foods, Gentle movement like walking, Keeping the area clean and dry
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Dampness and Heat are heavy, sinking energies that naturally settle in the lower body. When they pour into the groin, they create a hot, wet environment that inflames the skin, producing the red, oozing rash and fierce itching of acute jock itch. This pattern often flares up in humid weather or after eating rich, spicy foods that generate internal Damp-Heat.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, slightly swollen, with a thick yellow greasy coating that is heaviest at the root. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and rapid (数 shuò), often more pronounced at the rear (chi) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat thrives in hot, humid conditions and is fed by greasy, spicy foods and alcohol. Cooling and drying measures - like a dry environment, breathable clothing, and damp-draining foods - directly counteract this. Gentle movement helps Qi circulate and prevents stagnation, while stress fuels Liver Qi stagnation that can generate more Heat, so calming the mind also cools the skin.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and sugary foods that create internal Damp-Heat. Cut back on alcohol and seafood. Eat cooling, damp-draining foods like mung beans, adzuki beans, coix seed (Job's tears), and winter melon. Keep the groin clean and dry, wear loose cotton underwear, and change out of sweaty clothes promptly.

### Blood Deficiency with External Wind
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-deficiency-with-external-wind)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dry, scaly, thickened patches in the groin, Itching that worsens at night, Itching that shifts location or comes and goes, Pale or sallow complexion, Aversion to wind or drafts
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Dry, windy weather, Fatigue and overwork, Spicy and drying foods, Exposure to drafts
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and adequate sleep, Warm, slightly humid environment, Nourishing, blood-building foods, Gentle moisturizing of the skin
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the body’s blood is insufficient, the skin in the groin loses its nourishment and becomes dry, fragile, and unable to resist external influences. This internal dryness generates an invisible “wind” - a restless, moving force - that triggers itching that shifts and intensifies, especially at night when blood naturally retreats inward. The result is the chronic, scaly, and thickened patches typical of recurrent jock itch rather than the red, oozing rash of the acute stage.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, thin body with a thin white and slightly dry coat. Pulse: fine (thin), wiry on the left middle position, and floating on the right front position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood deficiency creates an internal dryness that easily stirs up wind, so anything that dries the body further-like spicy foods, dry air, or overwork-intensifies the itching. Rest, nourishing foods, and a stable, humid environment help replenish blood and moisture, calming the internal wind and allowing the skin to heal.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Build blood with gently cooked, nourishing foods like bone broths, black sesame, spinach, and small amounts of organ meats. Protect the groin from drafts and dry air; wear soft, breathable cotton and pat the area dry after washing. Avoid late nights and overwork, which drain blood and worsen the itch.

### Blood Stagnation with Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation-with-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dark red or purplish skin patches in the groin, Fixed, stabbing pain or sting in the rash, Itching worse at night or with pressure, Tiny bleeding points or crusting on the lesions, Irritability and a sensation of internal heat, Thirst with a desire to rinse the mouth but not swallow
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot, humid weather, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol, Emotional stress, Tight, non-breathable clothing
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Loose, breathable clothing, Gentle cleansing with lukewarm water, Avoiding spicy food and alcohol, Rest and adequate sleep
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When damp-heat sits in the groin for too long, it can thicken and congeal the blood, creating a stuck, hot pattern. The stagnant blood blocks the tiny vessels in the skin, turning patches dark red or purplish and causing a fixed, stabbing itch that gets worse at night. The trapped heat makes you feel restless, thirsty, and uncomfortable inside.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: reddish-purple body with purple stasis spots, distended veins underneath, and a thin dry yellow coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè) and rapid (数 shuò), often with a wiry (弦 xián) quality.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat and stagnation are the root of this pattern, so anything that adds heat (spicy food, alcohol, hot weather, emotional upset) will worsen the stasis and itching. Cooling the body, avoiding congestion, and keeping the area dry help the blood move more freely, which reduces the dark patches and the stabbing sensation.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat cooling, blood-moving foods like mung beans, eggplant, hawthorn, and small amounts of vinegar. Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol, which add heat and thicken the blood. Wear loose, breathable underwear and keep the area dry; gentle washing with lukewarm water is best. Try not to scratch, as it further damages the skin and traps more heat.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture really stop the itching?
Yes, acupuncture can be very effective for relieving itching and reducing inflammation. Points on the legs and lower abdomen are used to drain damp-heat, nourish blood, or move stagnation, depending on your pattern. Many people feel a reduction in itch intensity after just a few sessions.

### Do I need to change my diet?
Diet plays a big role in TCM treatment. Foods that create dampness and heat - like greasy, spicy, or sugary foods, and alcohol - can fuel the rash. Adding cooling, damp-draining foods like cucumber, mung beans, and barley can help. Your practitioner will give you specific advice based on your pattern.

### Can I use TCM herbs alongside my antifungal cream?
Generally yes. Topical creams work on the surface, while internal herbs address the root imbalance. Just be sure to inform both your TCM practitioner and your doctor about all treatments you're using. If you're taking oral antifungal pills, your herbal formula may need to be adjusted to avoid any interactions.

### Why does my jock itch keep coming back?
From a TCM perspective, recurrence means the internal environment - dampness and heat - hasn't been fully cleared. Even if the fungus is temporarily wiped out, the body's terrain still invites it back. TCM aims to dry up that internal dampness and strengthen the Spleen so the problem doesn't return.

### Is jock itch the same as athlete's foot in TCM?
They are related because both involve damp-heat, but the location matters. Athlete's foot often involves more external dampness invading the feet, while jock itch is more about internal damp-heat sinking to the groin. The treatment principles are similar, but the specific herbs and points may differ because the affected channels are different.

### How long does TCM treatment take?
Acute cases may improve in 1-2 weeks. Chronic or recurrent cases often need 4-8 weeks of consistent herbs and weekly acupuncture to fully clear the rash and prevent relapse. After the skin heals, your practitioner may continue treatment for a few more weeks to consolidate the results and strengthen your constitution.

### Is TCM safe during pregnancy?
Many TCM herbs are contraindicated in pregnancy, so you must tell your practitioner if you are pregnant or trying to conceive. Acupuncture can be safe, but points that stimulate the lower abdomen are avoided. Always work with a qualified practitioner who has experience with pregnancy.
