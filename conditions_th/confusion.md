## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, confusion is a state of impaired consciousness and cognition, ranging from mild disorientation and inattention to full-blown delirium. It can be caused by infections (like urinary tract infections or pneumonia), metabolic imbalances (low sodium, high calcium, liver or kidney failure), stroke, dementia, medication side effects, or substance withdrawal. Diagnosis typically involves a mental status examination, blood tests, and sometimes brain imaging to identify the underlying trigger.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment focuses on identifying and correcting the root cause: antibiotics for infections, electrolyte replacement for imbalances, stopping offending medications, or providing supportive care for dementia. In acute delirium, reorientation strategies, hydration, and a calm environment are first-line, while antipsychotic medications may be used cautiously for severe agitation or hallucinations.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the mind's clarity depends on the Shen - the spirit that resides in the Heart. When the Shen is calm and well-nourished, thinking is bright and orientation is intact. Confusion occurs when something disturbs or clouds the Shen.
That disturbance can come from several directions: excessive heat that agitates the spirit, sticky phlegm that mists the mind's orifices like a fog, or a collapse of the Heart's warming Yang that leaves the Shen unanchored and adrift.
Heat patterns are often sudden and dramatic. An external pathogen can penetrate deep into the body and directly invade the Pericardium - the protective wrap around the Heart - causing high fever, delirium, and a crimson tongue.
Internal fire, generated by long-standing emotional stress or dietary excess, can combine with phlegm to create a restless, agitated confusion with a thick yellow tongue coating. These patterns are what TCM calls excess conditions: something unwanted has arrived and must be cleared out.
Phlegm patterns produce a different quality of confusion - a heavy, dull mental fog rather than fiery agitation. When the Spleen is weakened by poor diet or worry, it fails to transform fluids, and dampness congeals into turbid phlegm. This phlegm rises to mist the Heart, making thinking sluggish and unclear.
The tongue becomes swollen with a thick, greasy coating, and the pulse feels slippery. This kind of confusion often lingers after a stroke or in chronic illness.
At the far end of the spectrum is Heart Yang Collapse - a deficiency emergency where the body's warming fire goes out. This can follow severe blood loss, extreme cold exposure, or the final stages of a devastating illness. The Shen loses its anchor entirely, causing mental cloudiness while the body turns cold and the pulse becomes barely perceptible.
This pattern requires immediate rescue of Yang, not just clearing or dissolving.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the onset and quality of the confusion. Is it a sudden, dramatic loss of clarity with high fever, or a gradual mental dullness that has crept in over time? The presence of fever, restlessness, phlegm sounds, and the appearance of the tongue and pulse quickly narrow the possibilities.
If the confusion is sudden, severe, and accompanied by high fever and delirium, Heat in the Pericardium is the leading candidate. The tongue is typically crimson red with a yellow coating, and the pulse is rapid and forceful. This is an acute excess pattern seen in serious febrile illnesses.
When confusion comes with marked restlessness, agitation, a heavy sensation in the chest, and a thick yellow greasy tongue coating, Phlegm-Fire harassing the Heart is likely. The pulse feels rapid and slippery, reflecting the combination of phlegm and fire disturbing the mind.
A pattern of Phlegm Misting the Heart produces mental clouding and dullness rather than agitation. The confusion is often chronic, the tongue coating is white and greasy, and the pulse is slippery. It frequently appears after a stroke or in long-standing illness.
Heat in the Ying Level presents with confusion, insomnia, and thirst, but the fever may be less dramatic than in Pericardium invasion. The tongue is dry and red with little coating, and the pulse is rapid and thready. This signals heat disturbing the spirit at a deeper, nutritive level.
When the confusion is profound or the person is barely responsive, Turbid Phlegm Blocks Orifices should be considered. The tongue coating is extremely thick and greasy, and the pulse is slippery. This reflects severe phlegm obstruction of the sensory orifices.
Heart Yang Collapsing is a critical deficiency pattern. The person loses consciousness suddenly, with cold limbs, a pale face, and a deep, weak, or barely perceptible pulse. This is a life-threatening collapse that demands immediate emergency care.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Heat in Pericardium
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-in-pericardium)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: High fever that worsens at night, Delirium with incoherent speech, Cold hands and feet despite burning body heat, Burning hot skin to the touch, Deep red (crimson) tongue, stiff or shortened, Severe thirst or dry mouth
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot weather or heat exposure, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Emotional stress or agitation
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, quiet environment, Cooling fluids (e.g., water, pear juice)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  In TCM, the Pericardium is the outer wrap of the Heart and protects the spirit (Shen). When an intense external Heat pathogen penetrates deep into the body, it can bypass the normal defenses and directly invade the Pericardium. This Heat then agitates and disturbs the Shen, causing sudden, severe confusion and delirium. The high fever and crimson tongue reflect the deep, Nutritive-level (Ying) Heat that is cooking the body's fluids and blood, while the cold hands and feet happen because the extreme internal Heat blocks the Yang Qi from reaching the limbs.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: crimson (deep red) body, stiff or shortened, with a yellow dry coating or little coating. Pulse: fine (细, xì) and rapid (数, shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat in the Pericardium is a deep, excess Heat pattern that directly disturbs the spirit. Any external or dietary heat intensifies the fire and further agitates the Shen, while emotional upset stirs up internal Yang. Cooling the environment and giving light, cooling fluids help drain the pathogenic Heat, calm the Pericardium, and protect the precious Yin fluids that are being damaged.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: This is a medical emergency that requires immediate hospital care. During recovery, keep the environment cool and quiet. Sips of cooling fluids like mung bean soup or pear juice can gently clear Heat and replenish fluids, but only when the person is able to swallow safely. Avoid any warming or spicy foods, alcohol, and greasy dishes that add Heat.

### Phlegm-Fire harassing the Heart
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-fire-harassing-the-heart)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Mental restlessness and agitation, Copious thick yellow sticky phlegm, Chest oppression and fullness, Flushed red face and red eyes
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol and coffee, Emotional stress or agitation, Hot weather or heat exposure, Worry and overthinking
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Light, cooling diet, Cool, quiet environment, Calming activities, Gentle exercise or movement
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Phlegm and Fire combine inside the body, they rise to harass the Heart, which houses the mind (Shen). The Fire agitates the mind, causing restlessness and confusion, while the sticky Phlegm clouds the Heart’s orifices, making thinking foggy and unclear. This dual assault produces a state of agitated mental clouding, often with a sensation of fullness in the chest and copious thick yellow phlegm.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, swollen with red prickly tip, thick yellow greasy coating. Pulse: slippery and rapid, full and overflowing at the left Cun (Heart) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Greasy and spicy foods create Phlegm and Fire, which directly fuel this pattern. Stress and anger stir up Heart Fire, while overthinking knots Qi and generates Phlegm. Cooling foods and environments help clear Heat, and calming activities settle the agitated mind. Gentle exercise promotes Qi flow, preventing Phlegm from accumulating and Fire from stagnating.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, fried, and spicy foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which generate Phlegm and Heat. Eat light, cooling meals with plenty of vegetables. Gentle daily exercise like walking helps move Qi and clear Phlegm. Practice stress management techniques such as meditation or deep breathing to calm the Heart and mind.

### Phlegm Misting the Heart
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-misting-the-heart)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Mental dullness rather than agitation, Rattling or gurgling sound in the throat, Thick white greasy tongue coating, Heavy-headedness and dizziness, Chest stuffiness and nausea
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, raw, or greasy foods, Damp or humid weather, Sedentary lifestyle or prolonged sitting, Worry and overthinking
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, light meals, Gentle exercise or movement, Mental engagement, Warm, dry environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen is weakened by poor diet, worry, or chronic illness, it fails to transform fluids. These fluids accumulate as Dampness, which eventually congeals into turbid Phlegm. This Phlegm rises to the Heart, misting the mind’s orifices like a fog, causing mental dullness, confusion, and a heavy, sluggish feeling.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale swollen body with a thick, white, greasy coating, often stiff or sluggish. Pulse: slippery (滑 Huá), like beads rolling on a string, with moderate strength.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Phlegm arises when the Spleen is too weak to transform fluids. Cold and raw foods further damage the Spleen's Yang, creating more Dampness that congeals into Phlegm. Damp weather adds external Dampness, worsening the internal fog. Physical movement keeps Qi moving, which helps the Spleen transform and transport fluids. Warm foods support Spleen Yang and dry some internal Dampness. Mental engagement stimulates the Shen, preventing it from being completely obscured by Phlegm.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods that create Dampness and Phlegm. Favour warm, cooked meals like soups and congees. Gentle daily movement such as walking helps Qi circulate and transform Phlegm. Keep the mind gently engaged with simple conversation or puzzles to stimulate the Shen.

### Heat in the Ying Level
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-in-the-ying-level)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fever that worsens at night, Confused or delirious speech, Mental restlessness and insomnia, Deep crimson tongue with little or no coating, Faint reddish skin rashes
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot weather or heat exposure, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Emotional stress or agitation, Overexertion
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, quiet environment, Rest and sleep, Cooling fluids (e.g., water, pear juice), Yin-nourishing foods
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Heat in the Ying (nutritive) level represents a deeper stage of febrile disease where pathogenic heat has moved from the Qi level into the blood-nourishing layer. This heat consumes the Yin fluids that anchor the mind, causing the Heart spirit to become restless and unmoored. The result is confusion, insomnia, and delirious speech, accompanied by a characteristic deep crimson tongue and fine rapid pulse.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: deep crimson (绛 jiàng) body, dry, with little or no coating; red prickles may appear on the tip. Pulse: fine (细 xì) and rapid (数 shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat in the Ying level consumes Yin fluids and disturbs the Heart spirit. Cooling and rest help preserve Yin and calm the mind, while heat, spicy foods, and stress add more heat and further deplete Yin, worsening confusion.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Focus on cooling, Yin-nourishing foods like mung bean soup, pear, and cucumber. Avoid alcohol, spicy foods, and late nights that consume Yin. Gentle rest in a cool, quiet room helps settle the spirit.

### Turbid Phlegm Blocks Orifices
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/turbid-phlegm-blocks-orifices)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Mental confusion or foggy thinking, Heavy-headedness, feeling the head is wrapped, Gurgling phlegm sound in the throat, Slurred speech or difficulty speaking, Chest tightness or feeling of oppression
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Damp or humid weather, Cold, raw, or greasy foods, Sedentary lifestyle or prolonged sitting, Overeating or irregular meals
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, dry environment, Light, cooked meals, Gentle exercise or movement
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen is weak, it fails to transform fluids, allowing dampness to collect and eventually thicken into turbid phlegm. This heavy, sticky phlegm rises to mist the upper orifices and physically obstructs the pathways that keep the mind clear. The result is a dense, foggy confusion that feels like the head is wrapped in a wet cloth, often accompanied by a gurgling throat and slurred speech.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks and a thick white greasy-slippery coat. Pulse: slippery (滑, hua) like beads rolling, often also wiry (弦, xian) at the right Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Turbid phlegm is the product of a weak Spleen that cannot manage fluids, so anything that adds dampness (cold, raw, greasy foods, humid air) thickens the phlegm and worsens the confusion. Warmth, dryness, and gentle movement help the Spleen transform and transport fluids, while light cooked meals reduce the burden on digestion. As the phlegm is cleared and fresh Yang rises to the head, the mental fog lifts.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods that create dampness - think dairy, fried dishes, ice cream, and sugary drinks. Favor warm, cooked meals like congee, steamed vegetables, and small amounts of warming spices such as ginger and cardamom. Gentle daily movement like walking or Tai Chi keeps Qi and fluids moving so phlegm doesn't settle. Stay in a dry, well-ventilated environment and avoid damp basements or prolonged wet weather exposure.

### Heart Yang Collapsing
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heart-yang-collapsing)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Profuse cold sweating, especially on the forehead, Ice-cold limbs (cold past elbows and knees), Blue-purple lips and nail beds, Extremely weak, barely perceptible breathing, Ashen-white or grey complexion, Confusion or mental cloudiness
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold exposure or drafts, Physical exertion or movement, Emotional shock or fright, Cold foods and icy drinks
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth and heavy blankets, Complete rest in a quiet room, Warm fluids like ginger tea, Gentle moxibustion (indirect heat)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Heart houses the Shen (spirit), and its Yang Qi keeps the mind bright and clear. When Heart Yang collapses - often from severe illness, blood loss, or extreme cold - the warming fire of the body goes out. The Shen loses its anchor, leading to mental cloudiness and confusion, while the body turns cold, clammy, and blue.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: very pale or bluish-purple, short and flaccid, with a wet, thin white slippery coat. Pulse: minute (微 wēi), hidden (伏 fú), or knotted (结 jié), barely perceptible.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heart Yang is the body's warming fire. Cold, exertion, and shock scatter what little Yang remains, deepening the collapse. Warmth, stillness, and warm nourishment help preserve and gently rekindle that fire, giving the Shen a stable anchor to return to clarity.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: This is a medical emergency - seek urgent care immediately. Keep the person warm with blankets, especially the extremities. After stabilization, offer warm, easy-to-digest foods like rice congee with a little ginger and cinnamon to gently rebuild Yang. Avoid all cold, raw, or icy items.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM help with confusion after a stroke?
Yes. Post-stroke confusion often falls into patterns like Phlegm Misting the Heart or Turbid Phlegm Blocks Orifices, where phlegm obstructs the mind's clarity. Herbal formulas such as Di Tan Tang are designed to transform phlegm and open the orifices, while acupuncture at points like Baihui (DU-20) and Fenglong (ST-40) supports mental recovery. Many patients see gradual improvement in alertness and orientation over several weeks of treatment, especially when combined with rehabilitation.

### Is acupuncture safe for elderly patients with confusion?
Acupuncture is generally very safe for older adults when performed by a licensed practitioner using sterile, single-use needles. Points are selected to be gentle and supportive - often on the head, arms, and legs - and the stimulation is adjusted to the patient's energy level. Always inform your acupuncturist about all medications, especially blood thinners, as this may influence point selection. If the confusion is severe or rapidly changing, seek emergency evaluation first.

### How quickly can Chinese herbs clear mental fog?
In heat patterns with high fever, mental clarity often improves within 24-48 hours of starting an appropriate formula like An Gong Niu Huang Wan, alongside conventional fever management. For phlegm-related fog that has built up over months or years, herbs work more gradually - most patients notice a lifting of the heaviness and clearer thinking within 2-4 weeks, but full resolution may take several months of consistent use.

### Can I take TCM herbs alongside my blood pressure medication?
In most cases, yes, but coordination with both your prescribing doctor and TCM practitioner is essential. Some herbs used in confusion formulas - such as Zhi Fu Zi (aconite) in Shen Fu Tang - can affect blood pressure and must be dosed carefully. Your TCM practitioner needs a complete list of your medications to avoid interactions. Never stop or adjust your blood pressure medication without medical supervision.

### Does diet really affect mental clarity?
Absolutely. In TCM, greasy, fried, and dairy-heavy foods are thought to generate phlegm and dampness, which can cloud the mind. A diet of light, easily digestible meals - congee, steamed vegetables, small amounts of lean protein - supports the Spleen and helps keep the mind clear. For heat patterns, cooling foods like pear and cucumber are beneficial; for deficiency cold patterns, warming foods like ginger may be recommended, but only under professional guidance.

### What should I do if the confusion comes on suddenly with a high fever?
Seek emergency medical care immediately. Sudden confusion with high fever can signal a serious infection like meningitis or sepsis, or a heat stroke. While TCM has powerful formulas for Heat in the Pericardium, they are used in hospital settings alongside conventional treatment. Do not delay emergency care - see the Safety section for urgent red-flag symptoms.

## การวิจัยทางวิทยาศาสตร์สมัยใหม่ (Modern Scientific Research)
1. **Neuroprotective Effects and Hepatorenal Toxicity of Angong Niuhuang Wan: A Systematic Review of Preclinical and Clinical Studies** ([ลิงก์](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2019.00593))
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Wang Y, et al. Frontiers in Pharmacology. 2019;10:593.*
   This systematic review examined the neuroprotective mechanisms of Angong Niuhuang Wan in ischemic stroke, intracerebral hemorrhage, and traumatic brain injury. The formula demonstrated anti-inflammatory, anti-oxidative, and anti-apoptotic effects, and clinical studies reported improved neurological function and consciousness levels. However, potential hepatorenal toxicity from cinnabar and realgar was also noted, emphasizing the need for short-term, monitored use.

2. **Acupuncture for Disorders of Consciousness in Patients with Traumatic Brain Injury: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhang L, et al. Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine. 2021;2021:8893506.*
   This meta-analysis pooled data from 15 RCTs involving over 1,000 patients with traumatic brain injury. Acupuncture added to standard care significantly improved Glasgow Coma Scale scores and shortened time to consciousness recovery. The most commonly used points were Baihui DU-20, Shuigou DU-26, and Neiguan PC-6.

3. **Clinical Observation on Di Tan Tang for Vascular Cognitive Impairment with Phlegm Turbidity Obstructing Orifices Pattern**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Chen H, et al. Chinese Journal of Integrative Medicine. 2018;24(8):587-592.*
   A randomized controlled trial of 120 patients with post-stroke cognitive impairment found that Di Tan Tang combined with conventional therapy improved Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) scores significantly more than conventional therapy alone after 12 weeks. The formula was well tolerated with no serious adverse events.
