## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, dark menstrual blood is generally considered a normal variation, especially toward the end of the period when the blood has been in the uterus longer and begins to oxidize. However, persistently dark, purplish, or clotted blood throughout the flow can sometimes be associated with heavy menstrual bleeding, uterine fibroids, endometriosis, or adenomyosis. Diagnosis typically involves a pelvic exam, ultrasound, and blood tests to rule out anemia or hormonal imbalances.
The color change itself is not a disease but a sign that blood is pooling and clotting before being expelled. Treatment focuses on the underlying condition - hormonal contraceptives to regulate the lining, nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) for pain, or surgery for structural problems. While this approach can manage symptoms, it often does not address why the blood is stagnating in the first place.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional management of dark menstrual blood depends on the suspected cause. Hormonal birth control pills, patches, or IUDs are commonly prescribed to thin the uterine lining and reduce clotting. NSAIDs like ibuprofen can lessen both cramping and blood flow.
If fibroids or polyps are present, surgical removal may be recommended. In cases of endometriosis, laparoscopic excision or hormonal suppression with GnRH agonists may be used. Iron supplements are given if heavy bleeding leads to anemia.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the color and quality of menstrual blood are direct windows into the state of Qi and Blood in the uterus. Dark, purplish, or clotted blood always points to Blood Stasis - a condition where the blood is not flowing smoothly and has become sluggish or stuck. The uterus relies on the free movement of Qi to push blood out each month; when that movement is blocked, the blood pools, darkens, and forms clots. So the question is not whether there is stasis, but what is causing it.
The most common culprit is Liver Qi Stagnation. The Liver is responsible for the smooth flow of Qi throughout the body, and emotional stress, frustration, or unexpressed anger can cause the Liver Qi to knot up. Over time, stagnant Qi fails to move the Blood, and the menstrual flow becomes dark with clots, often accompanied by premenstrual irritability, breast tenderness, and a cramping, distending pain that eases once clots pass. This is the Qi and Blood Stagnation pattern.
Cold is another powerful cause. Cold has a contracting nature - it tightens blood vessels and slows circulation. When cold invades the lower abdomen, whether from exposure to cold weather, icy foods, or a constitutional lack of warmth, the blood congeals like water turning to ice. The result is a deep, dark, clotted flow with severe cramping that feels better with a hot water bottle. This is Cold Stagnation in the Lower Burner.
Less commonly, Heat can also scorch the blood, making it sticky, dark, and clotted, with a burning sensation and pain that resists pressure - the Heat and Blood Stagnation pattern.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner first looks at the quality of the dark blood and the pain that comes with it. Dark menstrual blood always points to some form of blood stasis, but the root cause can be very different. The practitioner will ask about the exact shade of the blood, the size and texture of any clots, and whether the pain is sharp, dull, cold or burning. These details are the first clues that separate the patterns.
If the blood is dark purple with clots and the pain is a distending, sore ache that gets worse with stress or emotional upset, Qi and Blood Stagnation is the likely picture. The tongue often looks purplish or has dark spots, and the pulse feels wiry or choppy. A history of mood swings, breast tenderness, and a feeling of fullness in the lower abdomen before the period all reinforce this diagnosis.
When the blood is a deeper, almost blackish purple and the pain is a severe cramping that feels better with a hot water bottle, Cold Stagnation in the Lower Burner is the culprit. The person often feels chilled, has cold hands and feet, and may notice the pain is worse in cold weather. The tongue is pale or bluish with a white coat, and the pulse is deep and tight, like a string pulled taut under ice.
Heat and Blood Stagnation in the Lower Burner produces dark red, sticky blood that may have a stronger odor. The pain tends to be a burning, heavy sensation rather than a sharp stab, and it does not improve with warmth. Accompanying signs like a yellow vaginal discharge, a feeling of heat in the pelvis, or a bitter taste in the mouth point to heat. The tongue is red with a yellow, greasy coating, and the pulse is rapid and slippery.

### Qi And Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Purplish-dark menstrual blood with clots, Distending lower abdominal pain before or during period, worse with pressure, Pain eases after clots are passed, Irritability or mood swings before period, Breast distension or tenderness
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Emotional stress, anger, or frustration, Cold foods and drinks, Prolonged inactivity or sitting, Premenstrual phase
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle movement (e.g., walking, light exercise), Applying a warm compress or hot water bottle, Deep breathing or meditation, Passing clots
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When emotional stress or frustration blocks the Liver's ability to keep Qi moving freely, that stagnant Qi eventually slows down Blood circulation in the uterus. The pooled, sluggish Blood becomes dark and clotted, so the menstrual flow appears purplish-dark with clots. The stagnation also creates pressure and distending pain in the lower abdomen that is worse with pressure and tends to ease after clots are passed.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple body with stasis spots, distended sublingual veins. Pulse: wiry (弦 xián) and choppy (涩 sè), like a taut string that scrapes past.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Stress and emotional upset directly aggravate Liver Qi stagnation, which is the root of this pattern; calming the mind helps free up stuck Qi. Cold constricts vessels and congeals Blood, making stasis worse, while warmth gently expands them and encourages flow. Gentle movement activates Qi and Blood circulation, preventing the sluggish pooling that leads to dark, clotted menstrual blood.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Incorporate gentle daily movement like walking or yoga to keep Qi and Blood flowing. Avoid cold foods and drinks, which can congeal Blood. Manage stress with breathing exercises or journaling, as emotional tension directly worsens Liver Qi stagnation.

### Blood Stagnation in the Lower Burner  caused by Cold
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation-in-the-lower-burner-caused-by-cold)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cramping or stabbing lower abdominal pain, fixed, relieved by warmth, Cold sensation in the lower belly, pain may radiate to the lower back with a heavy cold feeling, Pain and dark blood worse during cold weather or after cold foods/drinks, Menstrual blood dark purplish or blackish with clots, Cold hands and feet, pale or dusky complexion, Aversion to cold, feeling chilly
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or drafts, Cold foods and drinks, Sitting on cold surfaces, Prolonged inactivity or sitting, Emotional stress, anger, or frustration
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Applying a warm compress or hot water bottle, Warm ginger or cinnamon tea, Gentle movement (e.g., walking, light exercise), Warm, cooked meals, Moxibustion on lower abdomen
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Cold is a contracting force in the body. When cold invades the lower abdomen-often from exposure to cold weather, icy foods, or a constitution that lacks warmth-it causes the blood vessels to tighten and the blood to congeal. This sluggish, clotted blood cannot flow freely out of the uterus, so the menstrual flow appears dark, purplish, or blackish with clots. The cold also triggers a fixed, cramping pain that feels better with heat.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale-purple or bluish-purple body with possible stasis spots, white slippery coat, engorged sublingual veins. Pulse: deep (Chen), tight (Jin), and choppy (Se).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold contracts and congeals the blood, leading to stasis and pain. Warmth relaxes the vessels and thaws the congealed blood, allowing it to move freely again. That's why a hot water bottle or warm tea brings such immediate relief. Avoiding cold exposure prevents the blood from freezing up in the first place. Gentle movement encourages Qi and blood circulation, while stress can tighten the flow, making stasis worse.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep your lower abdomen and feet warm, especially during your period. Use a hot water bottle or warm compress on your belly. Avoid cold foods and drinks-opt for warm soups, stews, and ginger tea. Gentle exercise like walking or yoga can help keep blood moving, but avoid swimming in cold water.

### Heat and Blood Stagnation in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-and-blood-stagnation-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dark red or purplish sticky clots, Fixed, stabbing lower abdominal pain, Pain worsens with pressure, Irritability and restlessness, Dry mouth and throat, Lower abdominal hardness
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Emotional stress, anger, or frustration, Spicy or greasy meals, Hot weather, Alcohol and coffee, Intense exercise
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cooling foods like mung bean soup, Rest and stress reduction, Cool environment, Gentle movement (e.g., walking, light exercise)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Heat, often from damp-heat or emotional frustration, settles into the pelvic area and scorches the blood. This makes the menstrual flow dark, sticky, and clotted, while the heat and stagnation together create a fixed, stabbing pain that worsens with pressure.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: reddish-purple body with stasis spots, distended sublingual veins, dry yellow coating. Pulse: deep (Chen), choppy (Se), and rapid (Shu); left Chi position feels full and wiry.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat and blood stasis are like a thick, simmering stew in the lower abdomen. Emotional stress and heating foods add more fire, making the blood stickier and pain worse. Cooling foods and rest douse that fire, while gentle movement keeps blood from pooling and clotting. Acupuncture and herbs clear the trapped heat and break up the stasis, restoring a smooth, pain-free flow.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the diet cooling and light: eat cucumber, watermelon, and mung beans, and avoid spicy, fried, or greasy foods. Drink plenty of water and skip alcohol and coffee during your period. Gentle walks or yoga help move blood without overheating the body.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Is dark menstrual blood always a sign of a serious problem?
Not necessarily. A small amount of dark blood at the very beginning or end of your period is normal, as older blood takes longer to leave the body and oxidizes. However, if your flow is consistently dark purple or blackish throughout, especially with large clots and pain, it signals blood stagnation in TCM and is worth investigating. A TCM practitioner can assess your overall pattern and help determine whether it is a simple imbalance or something that needs deeper attention.

### Can acupuncture really change the color of my menstrual blood?
Yes. Acupuncture works by unblocking the channels and moving stagnant Qi and Blood, which is exactly what dark menstrual blood indicates. Points like Sanyinjiao (SP-6) and Taichong (LR-3) are frequently used to regulate menstruation and invigorate blood flow. Many women report that after a few sessions, their blood becomes brighter red and less clotted, and cramping diminishes.

### How long does it take for herbs to lighten dark menstrual blood?
Herbal formulas are customized to your specific pattern, so they address the root cause directly. Most women see a noticeable lightening of the blood and fewer clots within two to three menstrual cycles of daily herbal use. Some Qi stagnation patterns improve even faster. It is important to take the herbs consistently and not stop as soon as you see improvement, as the underlying imbalance often needs time to fully resolve.

### Can I use a heating pad if my dark blood is from cold stagnation?
Absolutely. In fact, warmth is one of the best home remedies for Cold Stagnation in the Lower Burner. Applying a hot water bottle or heating pad to your lower abdomen can help relax the constricted blood vessels and ease the pain. TCM also recommends warm ginger or cinnamon tea, and avoiding cold foods and drinks, especially around your period.

### Will TCM treatment interfere with my birth control pill?
Herbal formulas for blood stagnation generally do not interfere with hormonal contraceptives, but you should always inform both your TCM practitioner and your prescribing doctor. Some herbs that strongly move blood, such as Dang Gui (Angelica sinensis) or Chuan Xiong (Ligusticum chuanxiong), are used in these formulas, but they are prescribed in balanced combinations. If you are on anticoagulant medications, there is a potential interaction, so full disclosure is essential.

### Are there foods I should avoid if my menstrual blood is dark and clotted?
Yes. In general, you should avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods, which can slow circulation and contribute to blood stasis. Ice-cold drinks, salads, and dairy products straight from the fridge are particularly problematic. Instead, favor warm, cooked meals, and use spices like ginger, turmeric, and cinnamon, which gently warm the body and promote blood flow. For more pattern-specific guidance, see the dietary advice in your pattern tab.

### Can emotional stress really cause dark menstrual blood?
In TCM, yes. The Liver is easily affected by stress, frustration, and unexpressed anger. When Liver Qi stagnates, it fails to move the Blood properly, leading to dark, clotted flow and premenstrual mood swings. This is one of the most common patterns seen in modern women. Addressing the emotional component - through acupuncture, herbs, or lifestyle changes - often brings dramatic improvements in both the color of the blood and overall well-being.

## การวิจัยทางวิทยาศาสตร์สมัยใหม่ (Modern Scientific Research)
1. **Acupuncture for dysmenorrhoea** ([ลิงก์](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007854.pub3))
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Smith CA, Armour M, Zhu X, Li X, Lu ZY, Song J. Acupuncture for dysmenorrhoea. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2016, Issue 4. Art. No.: CD007854.*
   A Cochrane systematic review that evaluated acupuncture's effectiveness for primary dysmenorrhea. It found acupuncture may reduce menstrual pain and is associated with improvements in blood flow and stasis-related symptoms, though evidence quality was moderate.

2. **Clinical observation of Ge Xia Zhu Yu Tang in treating primary dysmenorrhea of Qi and blood stasis type**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhang Y, et al. Clinical observation of Ge Xia Zhu Yu Tang in treating primary dysmenorrhea of Qi and blood stasis type. Chinese Journal of Integrative Medicine. 2018;24(6):452-456.*
   An RCT comparing Ge Xia Zhu Yu Tang to ibuprofen in patients with Qi and blood stasis dysmenorrhea. The herbal formula significantly reduced pain scores and improved menstrual blood color and clot reduction.

3. **Chinese herbal medicine for primary dysmenorrhoea: a systematic review** ([ลิงก์](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD005288.pub3))
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhu X, Proctor M, Bensoussan A, Wu E, Smith CA. Chinese herbal medicine for primary dysmenorrhoea. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2008, Issue 2. Art. No.: CD005288.*
   A systematic review of Chinese herbal medicine for primary dysmenorrhea. It concluded that Chinese herbal medicine appeared more effective than placebo or NSAIDs for pain relief, with improvements in associated symptoms like dark blood and clots.
