## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In conventional medicine, a cough that worsens at night is often attributed to postnasal drip from allergies or sinusitis, gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), asthma, or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Lying flat can trigger these mechanisms: mucus pools in the throat, stomach acid irritates the esophagus, or airways constrict in cooler night air. Diagnosis typically involves a history, physical exam, and sometimes chest X-rays, pulmonary function tests, or allergy testing.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Treatment depends on the suspected cause. Antihistamines, decongestants, or nasal steroids address postnasal drip. Proton pump inhibitors or H2 blockers are prescribed for GERD-related cough. Inhaled bronchodilators and corticosteroids manage asthma or COPD. Over-the-counter cough suppressants or expectorants may be used for symptom relief, though evidence for their effectiveness is mixed. When no specific cause is found, a trial of treatment for the most likely culprit is common.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM understands night cough primarily through the Lungs and their relationship with the body's Yin energy. The Lungs are responsible for descending Qi - guiding breath downward - but when they become irritated, dry, or obstructed, Qi rebels upward as cough. Nighttime is governed by Yin, the body's cooling, moistening force. If the Lungs are already deficient in Yin, the natural Yin-dominant environment of night can't soothe them; instead, the relative excess of Yang (heat) flares up, triggering a dry, tickling cough that often wakes you from sleep.
But dryness is only one story. Phlegm-Heat creates a different mechanism: thick, sticky phlegm and heat clog the airways. During the day, movement and upright posture help keep things flowing. At night, when you lie down and the body's energy turns inward, the phlegm settles and obstructs Lung Qi even more, provoking forceful coughing fits to try to clear the blockage. External pathogens like Wind-Cold or Wind-Heat also exploit the night: as the body's defensive Qi retreats inward, the exterior becomes more vulnerable, and trapped Cold congeals or Heat flares, worsening cough just when you need rest.
This is why TCM doesn't treat all night coughs the same way. A dry, scant cough with night sweats points to Lung Yin Deficiency and needs nourishing, moistening herbs. A deep, rattling cough with thick yellow phlegm signals Phlegm-Heat and requires clearing and cooling formulas. A cough with clear, watery phlegm and chills indicates Wind-Cold, treated with warming, dispersing herbs.
The Lungs may also be simply too weak - Lung Qi Deficiency produces a feeble, persistent cough that worsens with fatigue and lying flat. Each pattern reflects a different root imbalance, and the treatment follows accordingly.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner starts by asking about the sound of the cough and the sputum. A cough that is worse at night (夜咳, yè ké) can arise from several different imbalances, so the quality of the cough and the person’s overall feeling are the first clues that point toward one pattern rather than another.
If the cough is mostly dry, with only a little sticky sputum that may be blood-tinged, and the throat feels parched especially at night, Lung Yin Deficiency is likely. The tongue is red with little or no coating, and the pulse feels thin and rapid. This pattern often follows a lingering illness or overwork that has depleted the body’s cooling, moistening resources.
A rattling, productive cough that brings up thick yellow or green sputum points to Phlegm-Heat in the Lungs. The chest may feel tight, and the cough worsens at night because phlegm gathers when lying still. The tongue is red with a greasy yellow coating, and the pulse is slippery and rapid. There may also be a sensation of heat and thirst.
When the cough sounds heavy and brings up clear, white, watery sputum, and the person feels chilled and has a runny nose with clear discharge, Wind-Cold invading the Lungs is the pattern. The cough intensifies at night because cold congeals in the Lungs. The tongue is pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse is floating and tight.
A cough that comes in sudden, paroxysmal fits, often worse when lying down, suggests Wind-Heat invading the Lungs, provoking a spasm of the Lung Qi. The sputum is often thick and yellow, and the person might have a mild fever or sore throat. The tongue coating is thin and yellow, and the pulse is floating and rapid.
If the cough is stubbornly dry, with no sputum at all, and the throat feels itchy and scratchy, especially at night, the pattern is Dry-Wind. The tongue is red with a thin, dry coating, and the pulse may be floating and rapid. This often occurs in autumn or in dry climates, when external dryness robs the Lungs of moisture.
A weak, low-sounding cough that comes with noticeable shortness of breath and fatigue, and that disturbs sleep without much sputum, indicates Lung Qi Deficiency. The tongue is pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse is weak. This pattern is more common in people who are constitutionally frail or recovering from a long illness.

### Lung Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/lung-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dry cough with little or no phlegm, Night sweats, Dry throat and mouth, Afternoon tidal fever or heat in palms and soles, Restlessness and irritability
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Dry, heated indoor air, Spicy or fried foods, Smoking, Late nights and overwork, Stress and worry
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, moist air, Sipping warm water with honey, Adequate rest, Eating pears or lily bulb
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Nighttime is governed by Yin, the body's cooling and moistening energy. When your Lungs are depleted of Yin, they become dry and overheated. As evening falls, the relative excess of Yang (heat) flares up, irritating the airways and triggering a dry, tickling cough that often wakes you from sleep.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, dry, thin body with cracks, little or no coating. Pulse: fine (thin) and rapid, with the right cun (Lung) position particularly weak or floating-empty.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Lung Yin Deficiency means the Lungs lack cooling moisture. Dry, heated air and spicy foods further deplete fluids, making the cough worse. Rest and moistening foods replenish Yin, calming the cough. Nighttime is Yin time, so conserving Yin during the day helps prevent the nighttime flare-up.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat moistening foods like pears, honey, and lily bulb porridge. Avoid spicy, fried, and drying foods. Use a humidifier in the bedroom to keep airways moist. Stop smoking and limit alcohol, which dry out the Lungs.

### Phlegm-Heat in the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-heat-in-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cough with thick yellow or green sputum, Chest tightness and feeling of oppression, Worse at night or when lying down, Thirst with desire to drink cold fluids
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Lying flat at night, Greasy, fried, or spicy foods, Hot, stuffy environments, Emotional stress or frustration
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Sitting up or propping up with pillows, Cool, fresh air, Drinking warm water, Light, non-greasy meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Phlegm-Heat in the Lungs means thick, sticky phlegm and heat are clogging the airways. During the day, movement helps keep things flowing. At night, when you lie down and the body's energy turns inward, the phlegm settles and obstructs Lung Qi even more, triggering forceful coughing fits to try to clear the blockage. The heat also rises, irritating the throat and making the cough more urgent.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a yellow greasy coating, possibly swollen at the front. Pulse: slippery and rapid (滑数, huá shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Lying flat allows phlegm to pool in the airways while heat rises, both blocking Lung Qi and triggering coughing. Greasy and spicy foods generate more phlegm and heat, directly feeding the pattern. Sitting up uses gravity to help Lung Qi descend, and cool air and light foods help clear heat and reduce phlegm production.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid rich, greasy, and spicy foods that create more phlegm and heat. Eat light, cooling foods like pears, radish, and leafy greens. Drink plenty of warm water to help thin the phlegm. Sleep with your head slightly elevated to help Lung Qi descend.

### Wind-Cold invading the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/wind-cold-invading-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cough with thin white watery phlegm, Chills more prominent than fever, Clear watery nasal discharge, Worse at night especially when lying down, Absence of sweating
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or drafts, Cold drinks and raw foods, Lying flat at night, Exposure to wind
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth and covering up, Hot ginger tea, Rest, Steam inhalation
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Wind-Cold pathogens invade the body's surface and block the Lungs' ability to diffuse and descend Qi. At night, the body's Yang Qi retreats inward and the external Cold becomes more dominant, congealing in the Lungs and triggering cough. The cough is often worse when lying down because the cold phlegm obstructs the airways more easily.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: normal or slightly pale body, thin white moist coating. Pulse: floating and tight (浮紧 fú jǐn).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Wind-Cold is a yin pathogen that contracts and obstructs the Lungs. Warmth and hot drinks help to disperse cold and relax the exterior, allowing Lung Qi to flow freely. Rest conserves the body's yang energy to fight the pathogen. Cold environments, cold foods, and lying flat all exacerbate the cold congealing in the Lungs, making the cough worse.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep warm, especially the neck and chest. Drink warm fluids like ginger tea with brown sugar to help disperse cold. Avoid cold drinks and raw foods. Use a humidifier or steam inhalation to moisten the airways without chilling them.

### Wind-Heat invading the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/wind-heat-invading-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cough with thick yellow phlegm, Fever stronger than any chills, Sore or painful throat, Thirst and dry mouth, Paroxysmal coughing fits worse when lying down
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy or fried foods, Alcohol, Stuffy, overheated rooms, Lying completely flat
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Sipping cool or lukewarm water, Rest in a well-ventilated room, Eating light, bland foods (congee, steamed vegetables), Propping up on pillows to sleep semi-upright
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Wind-Heat is an exterior pathogen that invades through the nose and mouth, settling in the Lungs and disrupting their natural descending and diffusing rhythm. At night, the body's defensive Qi retreats inward, leaving the exterior more vulnerable, which allows the trapped Heat to flare and agitate the Lung Qi, triggering sudden coughing fits. Lying flat also makes it mechanically harder for the Lung Qi to descend smoothly, so the cough often intensifies the moment you lie down.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red tip with a thin yellow coating, body slightly red. Pulse: floating and rapid (浮数, fú shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Wind-Heat is a hot, exterior pathogen. Spicy, fried, and alcoholic foods add more internal heat, which fans the flames and makes the cough fiercer. A stuffy room traps heat around you, while lying flat physically compresses the chest and makes it harder for the Lung Qi to descend. Cooling, moistening drinks and foods help clear the heat, and resting in a fresh, airy space supports the body's defensive Qi as it pushes the pathogen out. Sleeping propped up gives the Lungs a little more room to breathe and descend smoothly, easing the nighttime coughing fits.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat light, moistening foods like pear, congee, and steamed greens. Avoid spicy, fried, or greasy dishes that add internal heat. Drink plenty of lukewarm water or mint tea, and sleep with your head slightly elevated to help Lung Qi descend.

### Dry-Wind
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/dry-wind)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dry, non-productive cough (little or no sputum), Itchy, scratchy throat that triggers coughing, Worse at night and in dry environments, Dry mouth, nose, and lips, Mild aversion to wind or low-grade fever
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Dry, heated indoor air, Spicy or fried foods, Exposure to dry wind, Smoke or dust
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Moist, cool air (humidifier), Warm, soothing drinks (pear juice, honey water), Rest and staying indoors on windy days
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  This pattern arises when a dry, warm wind invades from the outside and saps the Lung's moisture. The Lungs need to be moist to descend Qi smoothly; once they become dry and irritated, they rebel upward as a dry, tickling cough. At night, Yin energy is naturally weaker, so the already-depleted fluids cannot moisten the airways, making the cough even more persistent and disruptive.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: slightly red body with a dry surface, thin white dry coating, possible cracks. Pulse: floating (浮 fú) and rapid (数 shuò), with the right cun position feeling relatively stronger.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Dry-Wind directly steals moisture from the Lungs, leaving them parched and irritable. Anything that adds moisture back - humid air, warm fluids, pear juice - soothes the Lungs and helps Qi descend. Dry, heated air and spicy foods do the opposite, fanning the dryness and provoking more coughing. Resting indoors shields the body from the external Wind that started the problem, allowing the Lungs to recover their normal moist state.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Use a humidifier in the bedroom to keep air moist. Sip warm water or pear juice throughout the evening. Avoid spicy, fried, or overly dry foods, and protect your throat from cold, dry winds with a scarf.

### Lung Qi Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/lung-qi-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Weak, low-sounding cough, worse when lying down, Shortness of breath that worsens with activity, Fatigue and tiredness, Clear, thin, watery phlegm, Spontaneous sweating during the day, Pale complexion and low, reluctant voice
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overexertion and fatigue, Cold weather, Raw and cold foods, Lying flat
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm drinks and soups, Rest and avoiding overexertion, Gentle exercise like walking
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Your Lungs rely on Qi to keep the airways open and to guide the breath downward. When Lung Qi is weak, it can't descend properly, so a gentle but stubborn cough pushes upward instead. At night, the body's Qi naturally withdraws inward and becomes still, which makes an already deficient Lung even less able to keep things moving - that's why you wake up coughing or the cough gets worse when you lie down. The cough is weak-sounding and often comes with clear, watery phlegm because there isn't enough Qi to transform fluids efficiently.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale and tender body, possible teeth marks, thin white coating. Pulse: weak, especially in the right cun (Lung) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Overexertion drains the Qi you're already short on, making the cough worse. Cold constricts and weakens the Lung's defensive barrier, while raw foods dampen the Spleen's ability to produce Qi - the very energy your Lungs need. Rest conserves Qi, warm foods gently replenish it, and light movement encourages Qi to circulate without depleting your reserves.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, nourishing foods like chicken soup, rice porridge, and root vegetables. Add a slice of astragalus root to soups or tea to gently build Lung Qi. Avoid raw salads, ice-cold drinks, and dairy, which can weaken digestion and create more phlegm. Rest deeply and avoid pushing through fatigue - gentle walks and deep breathing exercises are better than intense workouts while you rebuild your strength.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Why does my cough get worse when I lie down?
In TCM, lying down can worsen a cough for different reasons depending on your pattern. If you have Phlegm-Heat or Phlegm-Dampness, phlegm settles and obstructs the airways when you're horizontal, triggering a cough to clear it. If you have Lung Yin Deficiency, the dry, irritated airways become more noticeable without the distraction of daytime activity, and the cough often feels tickly and relentless. If the cause is Wind-Cold, the cold congeals in the Lungs when you're still and under covers, making the cough more frequent. Propping yourself up with pillows can help, but addressing the root pattern is key.

### Can TCM help a chronic night cough that hasn't responded to other treatments?
Yes, this is a common reason people seek TCM care. Because TCM differentiates patterns like Lung Yin Deficiency, Phlegm-Heat, or Lung Qi Deficiency, it can often succeed where a one-size-fits-all approach fails. For example, a dry, lingering cough after a respiratory infection often responds well to herbs that nourish Lung Yin, such as Bai He (Lily Bulb) and Mai Dong (Ophiopogon). A cough with thick, stubborn phlegm may clear with formulas that transform Phlegm-Heat. A thorough TCM assessment, including tongue and pulse diagnosis, helps pinpoint the right treatment.

### Is it safe to take TCM herbs alongside my prescription cough medicine?
In most cases, yes, but you should inform both your TCM practitioner and your prescribing doctor about all medications and supplements you're taking. Some herbs, like Ma Huang (Ephedra), can interact with stimulants or blood pressure medications and are used only in specific acute patterns under professional guidance. Generally, TCM formulas for night cough are gentle and can complement conventional treatments, but coordination is essential. Never stop prescribed medications abruptly without consulting your doctor.

### What foods should I avoid if I have a night cough?
Regardless of pattern, avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods, which can impair digestion and encourage phlegm production. Dairy, fried foods, and excessive sweets tend to create Dampness and Phlegm, worsening productive coughs. Spicy, heating foods can aggravate dry coughs from Yin Deficiency. Very cold drinks and ice cream can constrict the Lungs and trigger coughing in Wind-Cold patterns. Instead, favour warm, easily digested foods like congee, soups, and steamed vegetables. Pears and honey can soothe dryness, while ginger tea warms and disperses Cold.

### Does acupuncture hurt when treating a cough?
Acupuncture for cough typically uses points on the arms, back, and legs, such as Lieque (LU-7), Chize (LU-5), and Feishu (BL-13). The needles are hair-thin, and most people feel only a brief pinch or a dull ache upon insertion, followed by a sense of relaxation. Many patients find the treatment soothing and notice an easing of chest tightness or throat irritation during the session. It's generally well-tolerated, even by those who are needle-sensitive.

### How long does it take for TCM to work for a night cough?
Acute coughs from Wind-Cold or Wind-Heat often improve within a few days of starting herbs. Dry-Wind coughs may take about a week. Chronic patterns like Lung Yin Deficiency or Phlegm-Heat typically show noticeable improvement within 2-4 weeks of consistent treatment, though complete resolution may take longer. Lung Qi Deficiency, which involves rebuilding energy, requires patience - 4-8 weeks or more - but sleep disruption often lessens earlier. Your practitioner will adjust the formula as your symptoms evolve.
