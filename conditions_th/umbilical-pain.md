## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Western medicine typically views umbilical pain as a symptom arising from structures in the mid-abdomen, such as the small intestine, appendix, or colon. It can be associated with conditions like irritable bowel syndrome, constipation, gastroenteritis, or functional abdominal pain. Diagnosis often involves a physical exam, review of bowel habits, and sometimes imaging or endoscopy to rule out serious pathology.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Treatment focuses on managing the underlying condition: antispasmodics for cramping, laxatives or fiber for constipation, antidiarrheals for loose stools, and acid reducers for suspected gastritis. For functional pain, dietary adjustments and stress management are often recommended, along with pain relievers as needed.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM sees the navel as the center of the middle burner, governed by the Spleen and Stomach, and closely linked to the Small and Large Intestines. Pain arises when Qi and Blood flow is disrupted, either by obstruction (excess) or by lack of nourishment (deficiency). The type of pain reveals the cause: sudden, severe cramping that improves with warmth suggests Cold invading the Interior, while a bloated, distending pain with sour belching points to Food Stagnation.
A dull, chronic ache that feels better with pressure and warmth indicates Spleen Yang Deficiency, and a sharp, fixed pain like a knife suggests Blood Stagnation. The Liver can also play a role, as emotional stress can cause Qi to stagnate, leading to intermittent cramping and bloating that eases with gas. And Damp-Heat in the Large Intestine produces cramping pain with urgent, burning diarrhea.
Because each pattern has a distinct mechanism, TCM treatment is tailored - warming the Interior, moving Qi, clearing Heat, or nourishing deficiency - rather than a one-size-fits-all approach. This means two people with the same Western diagnosis of 'functional abdominal pain' might receive completely different herbal formulas and acupuncture point prescriptions, depending on whether their pain is cold, hot, stuck, or malnourished in nature.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the quality of the pain - is it a sudden cramp, a dull ache, a stabbing sensation, or a bloated distension? They also want to know what makes it better or worse, such as warmth, pressure, eating, or emotional stress. The tongue’s coating and color, along with the pulse’s rhythm and strength, provide crucial clues that point toward one pattern rather than another.
If the pain strikes suddenly, feels like a severe cramp, and gets much worse with cold drinks or cold weather while improving with a hot compress, the picture suggests Interior Cold. The tongue often looks pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse feels tight and deep, like a frozen rope. This pattern reflects cold obstructing the flow of Qi in the abdomen.
When the pain is a bloated, distending sensation around the navel that worsens with pressure and comes with sour belching or foul breath, Food Stagnation in the Stomach is likely. The tongue coating appears thick and greasy, and the pulse feels slippery. This points to undigested food sitting in the digestive tract, blocking normal movement.
If the umbilical area feels heavy, distended, and painful, and there is diarrhea with mucus or a sense of incomplete emptying, Damp-Heat in the Large Intestine may be the culprit. The tongue shows a yellow, greasy coating, and the pulse is slippery and rapid. This pattern often arises from consuming greasy or spicy foods that create heat and dampness.
A dull, persistent ache that feels better with a warm compress and gentle pressure, and worsens when hungry or tired, points to Spleen Yang Deficiency. The person often looks tired, has loose stools, and a pale tongue with a thin white coat. The pulse is deep and thin, indicating that the digestive fire is too weak to warm the middle burner properly.
Intermittent cramping and distension that moves around the navel, often triggered by stress or frustration, and eased by belching or passing gas, suggests Liver Qi Stagnation. The tongue may appear normal or slightly dusky, but the pulse is distinctly wiry, like a guitar string. Here, emotional tension disrupts the smooth flow of abdominal Qi.
Fixed, stabbing pain that feels like a knife and is worse with pressure - especially if there is a history of injury or surgery - indicates Blood Stagnation. The tongue may have purple spots or a dusky hue, and the pulse feels choppy or rough. This pattern means blood is not moving freely through the local vessels, causing intense focal pain.

### Interior Cold
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/interior-cold)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Sudden, severe cramping pain around the navel, Pain sharply relieved by warmth and pressure, Pain worsens with cold food, drink, or weather, Cold hands and feet, feeling cold easily, No thirst, or only desire for warm drinks
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Exposure to cold weather or drafts, Cold or raw foods and iced drinks, Cold dairy products
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm compress or heat on the belly, Warm, cooked foods and soups, Ginger tea or warm water, Rest in a warm room
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When External Cold invades the body or settles deep in the Interior, it obstructs the smooth flow of Qi and Blood through the channels around the navel. Cold has a contracting nature - like ice freezing a pipe - so it causes the muscles and vessels to tighten and cramp, producing sudden, severe pain that grips the abdomen. Because the pain stems from a real blockage of Qi rather than a lack of nourishment, it feels intense and constricting, and anything that warms the area helps the flow resume.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body with a white moist coating. Pulse: deep and tight (Chen Jin), reflecting the Interior location and Cold constriction.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold has a contracting, obstructing nature - it freezes the Qi in the channels like ice blocking a river, which causes the sudden, severe cramping pain of this pattern. Applying external warmth or drinking warm fluids directly counteracts that cold, allowing the muscles to relax and the Qi to flow again. Conversely, exposure to cold weather, drafts, or ingesting cold substances deepens the blockage and tightens the pain further.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the abdomen covered and warm, especially in cold weather or air-conditioned rooms. Drink warm fluids like ginger tea and eat cooked, warm foods such as soups and stews - avoid raw salads, iced drinks, and cold dairy. A warm compress or hot water bottle on the belly can bring quick relief when pain strikes.

### Food Stagnation in the Stomach
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/food-stagnation-in-the-stomach)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Pain is distending and worse with pressure, Sour, rotten-smelling belching, Nausea or vomiting of sour, undigested food, Aversion to food and its smell, Foul flatulence and loose stools with a sour smell, Thick, greasy tongue coating, thickest in the centre, Slippery (Hua) and full pulse
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overeating or heavy, greasy meals, Pressure on the abdomen, Eating too quickly, Cold or raw foods and iced drinks
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Fasting or eating lightly, Gentle movement or walking, Vomiting, Warm, easily digested congee
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When you eat too much or consume heavy, greasy foods, your Stomach can't break them down efficiently. The undigested food sits and ferments, creating a physical blockage that disrupts the normal downward movement of Qi. This trapped, stagnant mass creates pressure and distending pain around the navel, which is the area that reflects the Stomach and intestines in TCM.
The key mechanism is obstruction-the undigested mass blocks the passage of food and Qi, causing pain that feels worse with pressure. The fermenting food also produces foul-smelling gas that rises as rotten belching and descends as putrid flatulence, which is a hallmark of this pattern.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: normal body colour with a thick, greasy, curd-like coating, thickest in the centre. Pulse: slippery (滑, huá) and full (实, shí), most prominent at the right middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: This pattern is a pure excess condition-the pain comes from a physical blockage of undigested food. Overeating, greasy food, and cold raw items all add to the mass or further weaken digestion, worsening the stagnation. Pressure aggravates the pain because it increases the sensation of fullness. Relief comes from emptying the digestive tract, either through fasting, vomiting, or passing the food, which directly removes the blockage causing the pain.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Fast for a meal or eat very lightly-congee or a simple broth-to give your Stomach a chance to clear the backlog. Chew food thoroughly and avoid raw, cold, greasy, or heavy meals until the thick coating on your tongue clears. Gentle walking after meals can help encourage downward movement of Qi.

### Damp-Heat in the Large Intestine
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-large-intestine)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cramping and distending pain around the navel, Urgent, burning bowel movements with a sense of incomplete evacuation, Mucus or blood-streaked stools, Feeling of body heaviness and thirst
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Greasy, fried, or heavy foods, Damp, hot weather, Alcohol, Overeating or heavy, greasy meals, Dairy products
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, bland foods (congee, cucumber), Rest or relaxation, Hydration with plain water, Cool compress on abdomen, Avoiding heavy meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Damp-Heat accumulates in the Large Intestine, it obstructs the smooth flow of Qi and Blood in the abdomen, leading to distension and cramping pain around the navel. The Heat and Dampness irritate the intestinal lining, causing urgent, burning diarrhea that may contain mucus or blood. This pattern often develops after eating too many greasy, spicy, or heavy foods, or from living in a damp, hot environment.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a thick, yellow, greasy coating, thickest at centre and root. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and rapid (数 shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Greasy, spicy, and heavy foods generate Damp-Heat, which worsens the accumulation in the Large Intestine. Hot, humid weather adds external Damp-Heat, aggravating symptoms. Cooling, light foods and adequate hydration help clear Heat and drain Dampness, while rest conserves Qi to aid recovery.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat cooling, light foods such as congee, cucumber, and watermelon. Avoid greasy, fried, spicy, and dairy products that create Dampness and Heat. Stay hydrated with plain water. Rest during acute bouts of diarrhea to conserve Qi.

### Spleen Yang Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-yang-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dull, persistent ache that improves with warmth and pressure, Loose, watery stools with undigested food, Cold hands and feet, feeling cold easily, Fatigue and heaviness in the limbs, Abdominal bloating that worsens after eating
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold or raw foods and iced drinks, Exposure to cold weather or drafts, Overexertion and fatigue, Overeating or eating late at night
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm compress or heat on the belly, Gentle pressure or resting, Warm, cooked foods and soups
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is the body's main digestive engine, responsible for transforming food into warmth and energy (Qi). When its Yang, or warming fire, runs low, the digestive process slows down and a cold emptiness settles into the middle burner around the navel. Without enough Yang to power movement, the smooth flow of Qi stalls, and the resulting stagnation creates a dull, persistent ache - the kind of pain that feels better with a hot water bottle or gentle pressure because these help supply the warmth and movement the Spleen can no longer generate on its own.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, tender body with scalloped teeth marks and a white, slippery coating. Pulse: deep, slow, and weak, especially at the right Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: The core problem is a lack of warming Yang energy in the digestive system. Cold foods and environments demand extra Yang to heat them up, which depletes the Spleen further and worsens the pain. Warmth, whether from food or external heat, temporarily supplies the Yang that is missing, relaxing the cold-induced spasm and restoring a smoother flow of Qi. Rest conserves the body's limited energy, while overexertion burns through it and leaves the middle burner even colder.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Make warm, cooked foods the rule - think soups, stews, and congees. Add a little ginger, cinnamon, or a pinch of black pepper to your cooking to gently stoke the digestive fire. Avoid raw salads, iced drinks, and dairy, which chill the Spleen and make the pain worse. A warm compress or hot water bottle on the belly can bring quick relief during a flare-up.

### Liver Qi Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-qi-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cramping and distending pain around the navel, Relief with belching or passing gas, Worse with emotional stress, Irritability and frequent sighing
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Stress, anger, or frustration, Greasy, fried, or heavy foods, Prolonged sitting or sedentary lifestyle
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Belching or passing gas, Gentle movement or walking, Rest or relaxation
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver is responsible for the smooth flow of Qi throughout the body. When emotional stress or frustration disrupts this function, Qi stagnates in the middle burner, causing intermittent cramping and distension around the navel. Because the stagnation is functional rather than structural, the pain often shifts and eases with belching or passing gas.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Normal or slightly dark body, red or slightly purple sides, thin white coating. Pulse: Wiry (弦 xián), taut like a guitar string.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Emotional stress, especially anger and frustration, directly stirs Liver Qi and causes it to stagnate. This stagnation blocks the smooth flow of Qi in the middle burner, leading to cramping and distension around the navel. Belching and passing gas release trapped Qi, offering immediate relief. Gentle movement encourages Qi circulation, while heavy, greasy foods burden the digestive system and worsen stagnation.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat smaller, more frequent meals and avoid heavy, greasy foods that can further stagnate Qi. Gentle exercise like walking or yoga helps move Qi. Manage stress with relaxation techniques, as emotional tension directly triggers this pain.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed stabbing pain around the navel, Pain worsens at night, Pain aggravated by pressure, Dark purple lips or nails, Dark menstrual blood with clots (if applicable)
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or cold drinks, Prolonged sitting or sedentary lifestyle, Stress, anger, or frustration, Pressure on the abdomen
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle movement or walking, Warm compress or heat on the belly, Rest or relaxation, Warm, cooked foods and soups
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When blood flow in the abdominal channels becomes obstructed-often after an old injury, surgery, or long-standing emotional stress-the stagnant blood acts like a dam. This blockage prevents the tissues around the navel from getting proper nourishment, producing a sharp, fixed pain that feels like a knife. The pain is worse at night because circulation naturally slows during rest, and any pressure on the area further compresses the stuck blood, intensifying the ache.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple body, possible stasis spots, distended dark sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩, Sè), rough like a knife scraping bamboo.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold congeals blood and worsens stasis, so warmth and gentle movement help to invigorate blood flow and ease the knife-like pain. Stress and frustration cause Qi stagnation, which in turn makes blood stasis heavier; calming the mind and moving the body gently helps Qi and blood circulate freely. Pressure directly compresses the obstructed area, increasing the stabbing sensation, so avoiding pressure and sitting still for long periods is key.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold and raw foods, which congeal blood and make stasis worse. Gentle daily movement like walking or stretching helps keep blood flowing. Apply a warm compress to the area to ease the pain. Steer clear of prolonged sitting, which encourages blood to pool.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### What are the most common TCM causes of pain around the navel?
The most common patterns are Interior Cold (sudden cramping that worsens with cold), Food Stagnation (bloating and sour belching after eating), Spleen Yang Deficiency (dull ache that feels better with warmth and pressure), and Damp-Heat (cramping with urgent diarrhea). Less commonly, Liver Qi Stagnation from stress or Blood Stagnation from an old injury can also be at play. Each requires a different treatment approach.

### Can acupuncture really help with belly button pain?
Yes, acupuncture can be very effective for umbilical pain, especially when combined with herbal medicine. Points around the navel, such as Tianshu (ST-25) and Shenque (REN-8), are used to directly regulate the local Qi flow, while distal points on the legs and arms address the underlying pattern. Many patients feel relief during the first session, though lasting improvement builds over several treatments.

### How long does TCM treatment take for umbilical pain?
