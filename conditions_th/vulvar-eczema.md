## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Vulvar eczema (also called vulvar dermatitis) is an inflammatory skin condition that causes itching, redness, scaling, and sometimes weeping or crusting of the vulvar skin. It can be triggered by irritants, allergens, or underlying conditions like atopic dermatitis or psoriasis. Diagnosis is usually made by a doctor based on the appearance of the skin and your history, and sometimes a biopsy is done to rule out other conditions.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Standard treatment includes topical corticosteroid creams or ointments to reduce inflammation, calcineurin inhibitors for sensitive areas, and antihistamines to control itching. Emollients and barrier creams are recommended to keep the skin moisturized. In severe or resistant cases, oral steroids or other systemic medications may be prescribed. Avoiding known irritants and allergens is also a cornerstone of management.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the Liver channel runs directly through the genital area, so many cases of vulvar eczema are linked to the Liver and Gallbladder. When emotional stress, frustration, or a diet rich in spicy and greasy foods creates excess heat and dampness, these pathological factors can pour down the channel, causing the classic acute flare: red, swollen, weeping skin with intense burning and itching. This is the Damp-Heat in the Liver Channel pattern.
But the Liver isn't the only player. The Spleen is responsible for transforming fluids in the body. If the Spleen is weakened by poor diet, overwork, or constitutional tendency, it fails to manage moisture, and dampness accumulates. Because dampness is heavy, it sinks to the lower body, leading to a chronic, dull, moist eczema that comes with bloating, fatigue, and loose stools. This is Spleen Deficiency with Dampness.
When vulvar eczema lingers for months or years, it can drain the body's Yin and Blood - the cooling, moisturizing essences that keep skin supple. The skin becomes dry, thickened, and scaly, and itching often worsens at night. This is Yin and Blood Deficiency, and it requires a nourishing approach rather than just clearing heat.
Less commonly, an acute outbreak of red, itchy papules can arise from an invasion of external Wind-Heat, often after a respiratory infection or exposure to an allergen. This pattern is more superficial and usually resolves quickly with the right herbs. Because each pattern has a different root, TCM doesn't use a one-size-fits-all cream. Instead, we treat the whole person to stop the cycle of flares.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner first asks about the nature of the itch and discharge. Vulvar eczema with intense burning, redness, and sticky yellow discharge that worsens with heat or humidity strongly suggests Damp-Heat in the Liver Channel (肝胆湿热, gān dǎn shī rè). The tongue is red with a greasy yellow coating, and the pulse feels rapid and slippery. This acute pattern often flares after rich, spicy food or emotional stress.
When the eczema is chronic and the skin looks dull, thickened, and slightly moist rather than fiery red, the practitioner suspects Spleen Deficiency with Dampness (脾虚湿蕴, pí xū shī yùn). The person may also have bloating, loose stools, and fatigue. The tongue is pale and puffy with teeth marks, and the pulse is soft or weak. This pattern points to a sluggish digestive system that fails to transform fluids.
Long-standing vulvar eczema that becomes dry, scaly, and persistently itchy, with little or no oozing, often indicates Yin and Blood Deficiency (阴虚血燥, yīn xū xuè zào). The skin is undernourished because the body’s cooling and moistening resources have been depleted. The tongue looks red and may lack coating, and the pulse is thin. Nighttime itching is common, and the person may feel dry in other areas too.
An acute outbreak with red papules, burning, and a floating rapid pulse, but less weeping than the damp-heat pattern, may be driven by Wind-Heat (风热, fēng rè). This pattern is less common in the genital area, but when wind-heat invades the skin it can trigger a sudden flare. The tongue may be red with a thin yellow coat, and the onset often follows exposure to wind or allergens.

- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol, Hot, humid weather, Emotional stress, anger, or frustration, Tight synthetic clothing
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool showers, Avoiding spicy and greasy food, Wearing loose, breathable clothing, Keeping the area dry, Reducing emotional stress
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver channel wraps around the genitals, so when the Liver and Gallbladder become overloaded with Dampness and Heat, that pathological mix pours downward along the channel. This is what creates the acute redness, swelling, weeping, and intense itching of vulvar eczema. Emotional frustration, rich foods, and alcohol all fuel the internal fire that drives this pattern.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Red body with redder sides, yellow greasy coating thicker at centre and root. Pulse: Wiry (弦 xián), slippery (滑 huá), and rapid (数 shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat thrives on heat and moisture, so spicy and greasy foods, alcohol, and hot weather pour fuel on the fire. Stress and anger stagnate Liver Qi, which then generates more Heat. Cooling, drying, and calming habits work directly against this mechanism, helping the Liver channel settle and the genital skin heal.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and deep-fried foods, as well as alcohol and sweets, which all generate Damp-Heat. Keep the genital area clean and dry, wear loose-fitting cotton underwear, and shower with cool or lukewarm water. Gentle stress management, such as walking or deep breathing, helps prevent Liver Qi from stagnating and generating Heat.

### Spleen Deficiency with Dampness
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-deficiency-with-dampness)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dull, thickened skin with mild oozing, Chronic, lingering itching, Poor appetite and bloating, Loose stools, Heaviness and fatigue
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, raw foods, Greasy, sweet foods, Damp, humid weather, Overwork and stress, Prolonged sitting or sweating
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, dry environment, Light, cooked meals, Rest and adequate sleep, Gentle exercise, Keeping the area dry
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Your Spleen is responsible for transforming food and fluids into energy and keeping dampness in check. When the Spleen is weak, it fails to manage fluids, allowing dampness to accumulate. Because dampness is heavy and tends to sink, it settles in the lower body, including the vulva, causing chronic, oozing eczema with thickened skin. Unlike the acute burning redness of damp-heat, this pattern produces a dull, lingering rash that reflects the underlying digestive weakness.
The root is Spleen Qi deficiency, so you will likely also experience bloating, loose stools, fatigue, and a heavy sensation in your body.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen with teeth marks, white greasy coating, moist surface. Pulse: soggy (濡 ruǎn) and slow (缓 huǎn), soft and lacking force.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: A weak Spleen struggles to transform fluids, so cold and greasy foods easily create dampness that worsens the eczema. Rest and warm, dry conditions support Spleen Qi and help evaporate dampness. Gentle movement invigorates Qi without depleting it, aiding fluid metabolism. Keeping the area dry prevents external dampness from adding to the internal problem.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw, greasy, and sweet foods that burden the Spleen and create dampness. Focus on warm, cooked meals like soups, stews, and congees with easily digestible grains and vegetables. Keep the vulvar area clean and dry, but avoid harsh soaps or excessive washing. Gentle daily exercise like walking helps move Qi and dry dampness.

### Yin and Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/yin-and-blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dry, scaly, thickened vulvar skin with intense itching, worse at night, Night sweats and a feeling of heat in the palms, soles, and chest, Dry mouth and throat, dizziness, and scanty or absent periods, Pale-red, thin, dry tongue with cracks and little to no coating, Fatigue with a restless, ungrounded quality
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Late nights and sleep deprivation, Emotional stress, anger, or frustration, Dry, heated indoor air, Overwork and overexertion, Excessive sexual activity
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Moisturizing the skin regularly, Cooling, yin-nourishing foods, Rest and adequate sleep, Gentle exercise, Reducing emotional stress
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Chronic vulvar eczema gradually consumes the body's Yin and Blood, which are the cooling and moisturizing essences that keep skin supple. When these reserves run low, the skin of the vulva loses its nourishment, becoming dry, scaly, and thickened. At the same time, the empty Yin allows mild internal heat to flare up, which intensifies the itching, especially at night. This pattern reflects a deep, long-standing depletion rather than an acute infection, so the skin is rarely red or weeping-it simply feels parched and irritated because it is undernourished from within.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale to pale-red, thin, dry body with cracks, scanty or peeled coating. Pulse: fine (细 xì) and rapid (数 shuò), especially weak at the left Guan (Liver) and both Chi (Kidney) positions.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Yin and Blood are the body's cooling and moistening substances. Spicy foods, late nights, stress, and overwork burn through these reserves like a candle burning too fast, leaving the skin dry and irritated. Rest, sleep, and yin-nourishing foods act like adding oil to the lamp, restoring the moisture and coolness that soothe the skin from the inside out. Moisturizing externally provides immediate relief while the deeper rebuilding takes hold.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Focus on foods that build Yin and Blood, such as black sesame, goji berries, mulberries, pears, spinach, and bone broths. Avoid spicy, greasy, or fried foods, alcohol, and coffee, which generate internal heat and dry the skin. Go to bed early and prioritize deep rest; chronic sleep deprivation is a major drain on Yin. Keep the vulvar skin moisturized with a gentle, unscented emollient, and wear breathable cotton underwear. Gentle movement like restorative yoga or walking supports circulation without overheating the body.

### Wind-Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/wind-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Acute onset of red, raised papules, Burning sensation, Mild fever or aversion to wind, Sore throat, Itching worse with heat
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Wind exposure, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Emotional stress, anger, or frustration, Hot, humid weather
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool environment, Rest and adequate sleep, Wearing loose, breathable clothing, Cooling foods
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Wind-Heat is an external pathogen that invades the body's surface, stirring up the skin and causing red, itchy papules. When it settles in the delicate skin of the vulva, it can trigger a sudden flare of eczema with burning and redness. This pattern often appears after a respiratory infection or exposure to wind and heat, and it tends to be acute rather than chronic.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Red tip and edges, thin yellow coating. Pulse: Floating and rapid.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Wind-Heat is an exterior pathogen that thrives on exposure to wind and heat, so avoiding these and keeping cool helps the body expel it. Rest allows the body's defensive Qi to focus on clearing the pathogen, while cooling foods reduce internal heat. Spicy food and stress generate more heat, worsening the condition.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods that generate internal heat. Drink plenty of water and consume cooling foods like cucumber and watermelon. Protect the area from wind and excessive heat, and rest during acute flare-ups.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM cure vulvar eczema?
TCM aims to correct the underlying imbalance that causes the eczema, which can lead to long-term remission rather than just temporary relief. Many women find that after a course of treatment, their skin stays clear for months or years, especially when they follow dietary and lifestyle advice. However, like any chronic condition, occasional flare-ups can still happen if triggers are encountered.

### How long until I see results?
Most people notice reduced itching and less weeping within 2-4 weeks of starting herbs and acupuncture. Acute patterns often respond faster; chronic, dry eczema may take longer to show visible skin changes. Your practitioner will adjust your formula as your symptoms evolve.

### Can I use my steroid cream alongside Chinese herbs?
Yes, it's common to combine conventional topical treatments with TCM. Steroids can manage acute inflammation while the herbs work on the deeper imbalance. Always tell both your TCM practitioner and your doctor what you are using, and never stop prescribed steroids abruptly without medical guidance. Over time, as your skin improves, you may be able to reduce the steroid dose under supervision.

### Are there any dietary changes I should make?
In general, avoid spicy, greasy, and sugary foods, as well as alcohol, which can create dampness and heat. Focus on warm, cooked meals like soups and congee. Your practitioner will give you specific advice based on your pattern - for example, someone with Spleen deficiency may need to avoid raw, cold foods, while someone with damp-heat should cut out rich, heavy meals.

### Is acupuncture safe for vulvar eczema?
Acupuncture is very safe when performed by a licensed professional. Needles are typically placed on points away from the affected area - on the legs, abdomen, or arms - to address the internal imbalance. Direct needling of the eczema is avoided to prevent irritation. Always ensure your practitioner uses sterile, single-use needles.

### Can stress cause vulvar eczema?
Emotional stress directly affects the Liver in TCM, causing Qi to stagnate and generate heat. This heat can then travel down the Liver channel to the genitals, triggering or worsening eczema. That's why many women notice a flare during or after a stressful period. TCM treatment often includes points and herbs to soothe the Liver and calm the mind.

### Will the eczema come back after treatment?
TCM aims to strengthen your body's resilience so that future flares are less likely and less severe. By addressing the root cause - whether it's dampness, heat, or deficiency - the body is better equipped to handle triggers. Some women may need occasional maintenance treatments, but the goal is to break the chronic cycle.

### Is TCM safe during pregnancy?
Many TCM treatments can be safely adapted for pregnancy, but you must inform your practitioner if you are pregnant or trying to conceive. Certain herbs are contraindicated during pregnancy, and acupuncture points will be chosen carefully. Always consult your obstetrician as well.
