## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Sciatica refers to pain that radiates along the path of the sciatic nerve, which branches from your lower back through your hips and buttocks and down each leg. It typically occurs when a herniated disk, bone spur, or narrowing of the spine compresses part of the nerve. This causes inflammation, pain, and often some numbness or tingling in the affected leg. Diagnosis is usually based on a physical exam, imaging like MRI or CT scans, and a review of symptoms.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Standard treatment includes pain relievers (NSAIDs, acetaminophen), muscle relaxants, corticosteroid injections, and physical therapy. In severe cases, surgery may be recommended to remove the bone spur or portion of the herniated disk pressing on the nerve. Most people recover with conservative care within a few weeks, but some develop chronic sciatica that can be difficult to manage.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM sees sciatica as a form of Bi syndrome - a painful obstruction - where something blocks the free flow of Qi and Blood in the channels. The sciatic nerve pathway aligns closely with the Bladder and Gallbladder meridians that run from the lower back, through the buttock, and down the leg. When external pathogens like Wind, Cold, and Damp invade these channels, they cause the hallmark sharp, fixed pain that worsens in cold, damp weather. This is why many people notice their sciatica flares after sitting on a cold surface or during a rainy spell.
But external invaders are only part of the story. An old fall or years of prolonged sitting can cause Blood to stagnate along the channel, producing a deep, stabbing pain that stays in one precise spot. Similarly, a diet heavy in rich, greasy foods or long exposure to humid heat can brew Damp-Heat inside the body, which then pours down the Gallbladder channel, creating a burning, heavy sensation down the outer leg. These excess patterns feel intense and demand immediate relief.
Over time, or with age, the Liver and Kidneys may weaken and fail to nourish the sinews and bones. This leads to a dull, chronic ache and a feeling of weakness - the pain isn't from a fresh blockage but from undernourished tissues. Even a general depletion of Qi and Blood can leave the channels malnourished, causing a tired, lingering pain that worsens with exertion. So the same sciatic pain can stem from very different roots, which is why TCM doesn't use a one-size-fits-all treatment.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
To understand which pattern is behind your sciatica, a TCM practitioner starts by asking about the pain itself - its quality, location, and what makes it better or worse. The tongue’s color and coating, along with the pulse’s rhythm and strength, then confirm the diagnosis and guide treatment.
If the pain is sharp and fixed, worsens in cold or damp weather and feels better with warmth, the likely pattern is Painful Obstruction with Wind-Cold-Damp. The tongue may appear pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse feels tight or wiry, reflecting cold and dampness blocking the channels.
When the pain is stabbing and stays in one very specific spot - perhaps after an old injury or long periods of sitting - Blood Stagnation is the key pattern. The tongue often looks purplish or has dark spots, and the pulse is choppy or wiry, showing that blood isn’t moving smoothly along the nerve pathway.
A burning, heavy sensation down the leg, sometimes with redness or a feeling of heat, points toward Liver and Gallbladder Damp-Heat. This pain gets worse in humid conditions. The tongue is typically red with a thick, yellow, greasy coat, and the pulse is rapid and slippery.
Chronic sciatica that is more of a dull, persistent ache with lower back soreness and weak knees suggests Painful Obstruction with Liver and Kidney Deficiency. The pain often worsens after overwork or at night. The tongue is pale with little coating, and the pulse is deep and weak - signs that the body’s deep reserves are insufficient to nourish the sinews.
If the pain is mild but lingering and comes with overall fatigue, a pale face, and a sense of heaviness, then Painful Obstruction with Qi and Blood Deficiency is considered. The tongue is pale and the pulse is thin and weak, indicating that the channels lack the nourishment they need to stay pain-free.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Painful Obstruction with Wind-Cold-Damp
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/painful-obstruction-with-wind-cold-damp)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Sharp, fixed pain along the sciatic nerve, worse with cold and damp, Heaviness and numbness in the leg, Pain worsens in cold, damp, or rainy weather, Relief from warmth and gentle movement, Stiffness and limited range of motion in the lower back and hip
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, damp weather, Sitting or lying on cold surfaces, Exposure to wind or drafts, Cold or raw foods, Prolonged inactivity
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Applying heat (warm compress, moxibustion), Gentle movement or stretching, Warm, dry weather, Eating warming foods like ginger and cinnamon, Wearing warm clothing on lower back
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Wind, Cold, and Damp invade the body-often from exposure to cold, damp weather or sitting on cold surfaces-they settle into the channels of the lower back and leg, particularly the Bladder and Gallbladder meridians that run along the sciatic nerve pathway. Cold contracts and tightens the channels, while Dampness creates heaviness and stagnation, blocking the smooth flow of Qi and Blood. This obstruction produces the sharp, fixed pain and stiffness typical of sciatica in this pattern, which flares up whenever the weather turns cold or damp.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, slightly swollen body with teeth marks, white greasy coating. Pulse: wiry and tight, or soggy and slow.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Wind, Cold, and Damp are the very pathogens that cause this pattern. Cold contracts and slows the flow of Qi and Blood, while Dampness creates heaviness and stagnation. Warmth and gentle movement disperse these pathogens, unblock the channels, and restore circulation. Cold, damp environments and foods introduce more of the same pathogens, worsening the obstruction and pain.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the lower back and legs warm, especially in cold or damp weather. Use a heating pad or moxibustion on the lower back regularly. Eat warming foods like ginger, cinnamon, and lamb, and avoid cold, raw foods and icy drinks. Gentle daily movement such as walking or tai chi helps circulate Qi and prevent stiffness, but avoid overexertion.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed stabbing pain along the buttock and leg, Pain worsens at night, Pain feels worse with pressure or touch, Dark or purplish spots on the tongue, History of trauma or prolonged sitting, Dark circles under the eyes or dusky lips
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Prolonged sitting, Cold, damp weather, Pressure on the painful area, Prolonged inactivity, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle movement or stretching, Applying heat (warm compress, moxibustion), Acupressure or massage, Rest
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When an old injury, a fall, or years of prolonged sitting damage the local circulation, blood gets stuck along the sciatic nerve pathway. This stagnant blood acts like a logjam in the channels that run from the lower back down the leg, creating a deep, stabbing pain that stays in one spot. Because blood flow is slowest at night and when you stay still, the pain often intensifies after dark and feels worse with pressure.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body with stasis spots and distended dark sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩, sè) or wiry (弦, xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood stasis is like a traffic jam in the channels. Sitting still or staying inactive lets the jam build up, while cold and dampness congeal blood even more. Gentle movement and warmth, on the other hand, get circulation moving again, which is why walking, stretching, and heat often bring relief. Acupuncture and massage physically unblock the stagnation, easing the stabbing pain.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid icy drinks and raw, cold foods that can congeal blood. Gentle daily movement like walking or stretching keeps circulation open, but avoid sitting for more than 30 minutes at a time. Warm compresses or a heating pad on the lower back and hip can make a noticeable difference. Add turmeric, ginger, and small amounts of dark leafy greens to your meals to support healthy blood flow.

### Liver and Gallbladder Damp-Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-and-gallbladder-damp-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Burning, heavy pain along outer hip and thigh, Worse in humid or hot weather, Bitter taste in the mouth, Irritability and restlessness, Redness and warmth along the nerve path
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Humid or hot weather, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol, Emotional stress and anger, Sitting in damp places
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, dry weather, Bitter and cooling foods, Gentle movement or stretching, Rest in a cool place, Reducing alcohol and greasy food
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Damp-Heat can accumulate in the Liver and Gallbladder system from rich foods, alcohol, or prolonged exposure to humid heat. When this heavy, hot pathogen pours downward, it gets trapped in the Gallbladder channel that runs through the buttock and down the outer leg, causing burning, heavy pain along the sciatic nerve path.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, especially at the sides, with a thick yellow greasy coating. Pulse: wiry, slippery, and rapid.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: <p>Damp-Heat is a combination of heavy moisture and heat trapped in the body. External dampness and heat (humid weather, hot environments) add to the burden, while greasy, spicy, and alcoholic foods create more internal Damp-Heat. Emotional stress and anger stir up Liver Qi, which can transform into Heat. Cooling, drying foods and environments help counteract the Damp-Heat, and gentle movement encourages Qi flow without generating more Heat.</p>
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, spicy, and fried foods, alcohol, and excessive dairy, which all generate Dampness and Heat. Eat more cooling, bitter foods like celery, bitter melon, cucumber, and mung beans. Stay in a cool, dry environment and avoid prolonged sitting on damp surfaces. Gentle movement like walking or swimming helps Qi flow without overheating.

### Painful Obstruction with Liver and Kidney Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/painful-obstruction-with-liver-and-kidney-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dull, chronic aching pain rather than sharp or burning, Lower back and knee soreness and weakness, Pain worsens in cold, damp weather, Numbness or tingling in the leg, Fatigue and weak, heavy legs
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, damp weather, Overexertion or prolonged standing, Cold or raw foods, Fatigue and lack of sleep
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest, Warmth, Gentle movement or stretching, Warm, cooked meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Liver and Kidneys are weak, they cannot properly nourish the sinews and bones along the sciatic nerve pathway. This leaves the lower back and legs undernourished and vulnerable, so even minor strain or a change in weather can trigger a dull, persistent ache. The pain is not from a fresh invasion of wind or damp, but from the body's own depleted reserves failing to keep the channels strong and supple.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, thin body with a thin white coating. Pulse: fine (thin) and weak, often slow; the rear (Kidney) position feels especially faint.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: This pattern is rooted in emptiness and cold. ### Painful Obstruction with Qi and Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/painful-obstruction-with-qi-and-blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dull aching pain along the leg, Numbness or tingling in the limb, Fatigue and lack of strength, Pale face and lips, Pain worse with activity, better with rest
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overexertion, Prolonged standing, Cold, damp weather, Skipping meals, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest, Warmth, Gentle movement or stretching, Nutrient-dense meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the body's Qi and Blood are depleted, the channels that run through the lower back and legs are not properly nourished. The sciatic nerve pathway becomes malnourished, leading to a lingering, dull ache and numbness because the sinews lack the Blood to stay supple and the Qi to move freely. Unlike sharp pain from stagnation, this pain is a deep, tired ache that gets worse with activity, which further drains the body's limited reserves.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body, possibly thin with teeth marks, thin white coating. Pulse: fine (细 Xi) and weak (弱 Ruo), may be slow (缓 Huan), especially weak in the rear (Chi) positions and the right middle (Guan) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Qi and Blood are the body's fuel. Rest and nourishing food build them up, while warmth helps them circulate to the legs. Overexertion and prolonged standing drain these reserves, leaving the channels undernourished and the pain worse. Cold and damp constrict the vessels, further impeding the flow of already deficient Qi and Blood, and emotional strain consumes Qi, making fatigue and ache more pronounced.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Focus on warm, easily digestible, nourishing foods like bone broths, congee with red dates (大枣 dà zǎo) and goji berries (枸杞子 gǒu qǐ zǐ), and cooked root vegetables. Avoid cold, raw foods and excessive standing that drains energy. Gentle, consistent movement like walking or tai chi can help build Qi without depleting it.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture really help sciatica?
Yes. Acupuncture is one of the most researched TCM treatments for sciatica. It works by stimulating specific points along the affected channels to unblock Qi and Blood, reduce inflammation, and release natural painkillers. Many patients feel some relief after the very first session, though lasting change builds over a course of treatment.

### How many sessions will I need?
For acute pain, 6-8 weekly sessions are a common starting point, with many people feeling significant improvement by the fourth or fifth visit. Chronic or deficiency-based sciatica may need 10-12 sessions over several months. Your practitioner will reassess your progress regularly and adjust the treatment plan accordingly.

### Will I need to take herbs forever?
No. Herbal formulas are prescribed for the active treatment phase and are adjusted as your pattern changes. Once the pain is under control and the underlying imbalance is corrected, the herbs are tapered and then stopped. Some people with chronic deficiency patterns may choose to take a gentle maintenance formula during winter or times of stress, but this is optional.

### Can I continue my regular pain medication?
Yes, you can usually continue NSAIDs or other prescribed medications while starting TCM. In fact, acupuncture and herbs may help you reduce your reliance on painkillers over time. Just be sure to tell your TCM practitioner about all medications you're taking, especially blood thinners, as some herbs can interact. Never stop a prescribed medication without consulting your doctor.

### Is it safe if I'm pregnant?
Sciatica is common during pregnancy, and acupuncture can be a safe and effective option when performed by a licensed practitioner with prenatal training. Certain acupuncture points and herbs are avoided during pregnancy, so it's essential to inform your practitioner. Always check with your obstetrician before starting any new treatment.

### What if my sciatica is from a herniated disc?
TCM treats the pain and dysfunction caused by a herniated disc, not the disc itself. By reducing inflammation, relaxing muscles, and improving circulation around the nerve, acupuncture and herbs can often relieve symptoms even when the structural issue remains. Many patients find they can avoid surgery or manage their condition well with regular TCM care.
