## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Sunburn is an acute inflammatory reaction of the skin caused by overexposure to ultraviolet (UV) radiation, primarily UVB. Within hours, the skin becomes red, warm, tender, and painful; in more severe cases, swelling and blistering develop. A few days later, the damaged skin may peel. Diagnosis is based on the history of sun exposure and the characteristic appearance. While most sunburns are mild and self-limiting, repeated severe burns increase the risk of skin cancer and premature aging.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Standard first aid includes getting out of the sun, cooling the skin with cool compresses or a cool bath, and applying moisturizers or aloe vera gel to soothe and hydrate. Over-the-counter pain relievers like ibuprofen or aspirin can reduce pain and inflammation. For severe burns with extensive blistering, doctors may prescribe topical corticosteroids or, rarely, oral steroids. Staying well hydrated is also important, as sunburn draws fluid to the skin surface.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM views sunburn as an invasion of ‘fire toxin' (光毒, guāng dú) - an intense external heat pathogen carried by the sun's rays. Under normal circumstances, the body's protective Qi (Wei Qi) and the skin's natural moisture shield us from such heat. But when exposure is too strong or the body's defenses are already compromised, the fire toxin overpowers the surface, creating a state of Toxic-Heat. This is why the skin turns red, hot, and swollen: it's the body's attempt to push the pathogen back out.
The Lung system governs the skin and its opening and closing. When fire toxin strikes, the Lung's function is disrupted, and the skin's pores can't regulate heat properly. If the heat penetrates deeper, it can enter the Blood level, causing a more intense, internal burning sensation that often feels worse at night when Yin should be dominant. This Heat in the Blood pattern shows a crimson tongue and a rapid, thin pulse - signs that the heat has moved beyond the surface.
Not all sunburns are purely dry and fiery. When the skin weeps, oozes, or forms yellow crusts, TCM recognizes that internal Dampness is mixing with the external Heat. Dampness is a heavy, sticky pathogenic factor often produced by a weak Spleen or a diet rich in greasy, sweet foods. This Damp-Heat pattern is common in people with a tendency toward skin conditions like eczema, and it requires both cooling and drying herbs to resolve.
In people with a constitutionally dry or Yin-deficient body type, sunburn may appear as a dry, red rash with a lingering burning sensation but little swelling or blistering. Here, the body's cooling and moistening resources are already depleted, so even mild sun exposure can stir up an internal Empty-Heat. This pattern often recurs easily and heals slowly. Recognizing these different patterns allows a TCM practitioner to choose herbs and acupuncture points that not only clear the acute heat but also address the root weakness, reducing the likelihood of future burns.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A practitioner first looks at the intensity of the burn. If the skin is bright red, swollen, and fiercely hot with clear blisters and a strong thirst, the pattern is Toxic-Heat (热毒炽盛). This is a direct invasion of external fire toxin. The tongue is red with a yellow coat and the pulse feels rapid and forceful.
When the redness is darker and the skin feels burning hot to the touch away from the sun, the heat has entered the blood level. This Heat in the Blood (血热) pattern brings a dry mouth, a red tongue with a thin yellow dry coating, and a rapid, thready pulse. The discomfort is more about internal heat than surface blistering.
If the sunburned area weeps clear or yellowish fluid or develops honey-colored crusts, Dampness is combining with the Heat. This Damp-Heat (湿热) pattern points to an internal imbalance where body fluids have become pathogenic. The tongue coating appears thick and greasy yellow, and the pulse feels slippery, like beads rolling under the fingers.
In someone with a naturally dry constitution, sunburn may appear as a dry, red rash with a persistent burning feeling but little swelling. This Empty-Heat from Yin Deficiency (阴虚火旺) pattern often recurs after mild sun exposure. The tongue is red with little coating, and the pulse is thin and rapid, indicating a lack of cooling Yin fluids.
When a burn leaves purplish patches or a stabbing pain that doesn’t fade, the heat has damaged local blood vessels and caused Blood Stagnation with Heat (血热血瘀). This pattern may follow a severe or repeated burn. The tongue can show dark spots, and the pulse feels wiry or rough, reflecting poor circulation in the heated area.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Toxic-Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/toxic-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Intense redness and swelling, Burning skin pain, Blisters filled with clear or yellowish fluid, High fever and severe thirst for cold drinks, Dark, scanty urine, Restlessness and irritability
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Sun exposure, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Alcohol, Hot showers, baths, or saunas, Emotional stress or anger
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool compresses, Drinking cold water or herbal tea, Rest in a cool, dark room, Aloe vera gel
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Sunburn is a direct invasion of external fire toxin (光毒, guāng dú) from the sun. When the skin is overexposed, this intense heat penetrates the surface and overwhelms the body's defenses, rapidly creating a state of Toxic-Heat. The heat flares upward and outward, causing the skin to become red, swollen, and fiercely painful, often with blisters that mirror the body's struggle to contain the toxin.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with prickly thorns and a dry yellow coating. Pulse: rapid (shu) and forceful, often full or overflowing.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cooling measures directly oppose the external heat toxin, drawing fire out of the skin and calming the fierce inflammation. Rest conserves Qi and Yin, which are easily depleted by the heat. Spicy foods, alcohol, and hot water all add internal or external heat, stoking the fire and making the skin reaction worse. Stress stirs up Liver Qi, which can transform into fire and fan the existing heat, so a calm mind helps the body cool down.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Drink plenty of cooling fluids like chrysanthemum tea or plain water, and eat water-rich, cooling foods such as cucumber, watermelon, and mung bean soup. Avoid spicy, fried, or greasy meals, as well as alcohol, which all add internal heat. Apply cool (not icy) compresses and pure aloe vera gel to the skin. Stay out of the sun and rest in a cool, dark room until the acute heat subsides.

### Heat in the Blood
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-in-the-blood)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Burning skin pain, Skin redness that deepens in the evening, Restlessness and irritability, Intense thirst for cold drinks, Feeling of internal heat, like a furnace
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Sun exposure, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Alcohol, Emotional stress or anger, Hot showers, baths, or saunas
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cold drinks and cooling foods, Cool compresses or aloe vera, Rest in a cool, dark room, Peppermint or chrysanthemum tea, Gentle cooling baths
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Intense sun heat penetrates the skin and quickly reaches the blood level. Once there, it agitates the blood, creating a deep, burning heat that feels worse at night when Yin should be dominant. This blood-level heat pushes outward, causing bright red skin, a sensation of internal furnace-like heat, and systemic signs like thirst and restlessness.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with red spots or prickles, thin yellow dry coating. Pulse: rapid (数 shuò), often wiry (弦 xián) and forceful.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat in the Blood is a deep-level heat that flares up with external heat (sun) and internal heat from diet or emotions. Cooling measures directly counteract the heat, while rest and darkness help Yin recover at night. Avoiding spicy food and alcohol prevents adding fuel to the fire.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Stay out of the sun, especially during midday. Apply cool compresses and drink plenty of cooling fluids like chrysanthemum tea or watermelon juice. Avoid spicy, fried, and greasy foods, as well as alcohol, which add heat to the blood.

### Damp-Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Weeping, oozing blisters or yellow crusts, Feeling of heaviness in the body and head, Sticky or greasy sensation in the mouth, Red, swollen skin with exudate rather than dry peeling, Poor appetite, nausea, or loose sticky stools
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot, humid weather, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Alcohol
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, dry environment, Light, bland foods (barley, mung beans), Cool compresses
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Sunburn usually brings dry heat, but when it weeps and oozes, that is a sign that internal Dampness is mixing with the external fire toxin. Dampness is heavy, sticky, and turbid - it slows things down and makes fluids seep out instead of evaporating. When sun exposure forces heat into a body already burdened by Dampness (from diet, humid weather, or weak digestion), the two combine into Damp-Heat that clogs the skin's pores and causes red, swollen blisters that weep yellow fluid and form crusts.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a thick, yellow, greasy coating that is difficult to scrape off. Pulse: slippery-rapid (滑数 huá shuò), feeling round and rolling under the fingers at an accelerated rate.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat is a combination of two pathological factors. Heat from sun exposure is aggravated by hot environments and spicy foods, while Dampness is worsened by humidity, greasy foods, and alcohol. Relief comes from measures that cool the body and drain excess moisture - a dry, cool environment and bland foods help the Spleen function properly, preventing Dampness from accumulating and allowing the skin to heal without weeping.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, fried, and spicy foods, dairy, and alcohol - all of which create more internal Dampness and Heat. Focus on light, bland, cooling foods that drain dampness, such as mung beans, barley, cucumber, and celery. Drink plenty of room-temperature water, but avoid iced drinks which can trap dampness internally. Keep the skin cool and dry; after a cool shower, pat the skin dry gently rather than rubbing.

### Empty-Heat caused by Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/empty-heat-caused-by-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dry, red skin with a lingering burning sensation, Worse in the evening or at night, Night sweats, Dry mouth and throat, thirst for small sips of water, Restlessness and irritability
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot, sunny weather, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Late nights and stress, Excessive sweating or vigorous exercise
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest in a cool, dark room, Sipping cool water, Applying aloe vera or a cool compress, Gentle, non-sweating rest
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the body's cooling, moistening Yin is already depleted, the skin loses its natural protective fluids. Sun exposure then easily stirs up an internal empty heat, producing a dry, red sunburn that feels hot but lacks the intense blistering of a purely external heat attack. The burn often lingers and worsens in the evening when Yin should be dominant but is too weak to balance the body's warmth.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin, dry body with cracks, little or no coating. Pulse: fine (thin) and rapid.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: In Yin deficiency, the body's cooling and moistening reserves are already low. External heat and spicy foods add more heat, pushing the internal empty fire upward and outward to the skin. Late nights and stress further consume Yin. Rest in a cool place, sipping water, and gentle habits allow the body to conserve and slowly rebuild Yin, while cooling compresses directly soothe the dry heat trapped in the skin.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, fried, or drying foods and alcohol. Eat cooling, moistening foods like cucumber, watermelon, pear, and tofu. Sip water frequently throughout the day rather than drinking large amounts at once. Protect skin from direct sun, but also avoid workouts that cause heavy sweating, as this depletes Yin further. Gentle movement like Tai Chi or a short walk is better.

### Blood Stagnation with Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation-with-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Purplish-red skin patches or bruising, Fixed stabbing pain, worse at night, Sensation of internal heat, especially at night, Thirst with desire to rinse mouth but not swallow
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Alcohol and spicy food, Emotional stress or anger, Sun exposure, Sedentary lifestyle, Hot weather
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool compresses, Light movement, Cooling foods (cucumber, celery), Avoiding sun exposure
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When sunburn is intense or repeated, the external heat toxin penetrates beyond the skin surface into the blood. There it damages the tiny vessels, making the blood thicken and congeal. This trapped heat and stagnant blood creates dark purplish patches, a lingering fixed stabbing pain, and a sensation of internal heat that often feels worse at night.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: reddish-purple body with stasis spots, thin dry yellow coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè) and rapid (数 shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: This pattern is driven by heat trapped in the blood that creates stagnation. Cold compresses and cooling foods directly reduce heat, while light movement helps move stagnant blood. Alcohol, spicy food, and stress add more heat and worsen stagnation, and sun exposure re-ignites the original trigger.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat plenty of cooling, blood-moving foods like celery, cucumber, hawthorn, and radish. Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods that add heat. Gentle movement like walking helps blood circulation. Apply cool compresses to the affected area and strictly avoid further sun exposure until healed.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM help a fresh sunburn?
Yes. Applying a cooled herbal wash or compress made with herbs like Jin Yin Hua (honeysuckle) or chrysanthemum can immediately draw out heat and reduce redness and pain. Internal formulas that clear Heat and toxins can also speed healing. The key is to start treatment as soon as possible after sun exposure.

### What herbs can I use on a sunburn at home?
For mild sunburns, you can brew a strong tea of Jin Yin Hua (honeysuckle flower), Ju Hua (chrysanthemum), or Bo He (peppermint), let it cool, and apply it as a compress several times a day. Aloe vera gel is also excellent for cooling and moistening. Avoid oily or heavy creams that can trap heat. If the burn is severe or blistered, consult a practitioner for a more targeted formula.

### How quickly will the pain go away?
With proper cooling herbs, the stinging and burning sensation often diminishes within hours. Redness may take a day or two to fade. If the pain persists or worsens, it may indicate a deeper Heat pattern that needs stronger treatment, so see a TCM practitioner or doctor.

### Can acupuncture help with sunburn?
Acupuncture can be used to clear Heat and calm inflammation, especially for widespread or painful burns. Points like Dazhui (DU-14) and Quchi (LI-11) are often needled to release Heat from the body. However, for localized sunburn, external herbal applications are usually the primary treatment, with acupuncture used to support the body's overall cooling response.

### Are there foods I should avoid after a sunburn?
Yes. Avoid spicy, greasy, and heating foods like chili, alcohol, coffee, and fried dishes, as they add more Heat to the body. Instead, eat cooling foods like cucumber, watermelon, mung beans, and drink plenty of water or chrysanthemum tea. These help clear the fire toxin and promote healing.

### Can TCM prevent sunburns in the future?
TCM can't replace sunscreen, but it can reduce your skin's sensitivity to the sun. By treating underlying patterns like Yin Deficiency or Damp-Heat, the skin becomes less reactive and more resilient. Many people find they burn less easily and recover faster after a course of constitutional treatment.
