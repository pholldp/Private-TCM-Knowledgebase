## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Chronic pyelonephritis is a persistent bacterial infection of the kidneys, usually resulting from recurrent or inadequately treated acute urinary tract infections. Over time, it can cause scarring and reduced kidney function. Typical symptoms include flank pain, fever, frequent and painful urination, and fatigue. Diagnosis relies on urine cultures and imaging studies like ultrasound or CT scans.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment centers on antibiotics to clear the infection, often a prolonged course for chronic cases. Some patients are prescribed low-dose prophylactic antibiotics to prevent recurrences. If structural abnormalities like kidney stones or reflux contribute, surgical correction may be recommended.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM understands chronic pyelonephritis as a form of strangury (淋证) called 'fatigue strangury' (劳淋, láo lín), a mixed condition of deficiency and excess. The primary organs involved are the Kidneys, which govern water metabolism and store the body's vital essence, and the Bladder, which receives and excretes urine. When Kidney Qi is weak - whether from overwork, chronic illness, or constitutional tendency - the Bladder loses its ability to hold and expel urine smoothly, leading to frequent, dribbling urination that worsens with fatigue.
At the same time, damp-heat often lingers in the lower burner, creating the burning, cloudy urine characteristic of an active infection. This damp-heat can arise from external pathogens that weren't fully cleared or from internal imbalances like Spleen deficiency failing to manage fluids. The result is a vicious cycle: deficiency allows damp-heat to take hold, and damp-heat further depletes the body's Qi and Yin.
Because the root can be a deficiency of Qi, Yin, or Yang, and the branch can be damp-heat or blood stasis, the same Western diagnosis manifests very differently from person to person. One patient may feel cold, exhausted, and have a pale tongue; another may have night sweats, a dry mouth, and a red tongue. TCM differentiates these patterns to treat not just the infection but the person who is suffering from it.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the quality of urination and what makes it worse. Chronic pyelonephritis (劳淋, láo lín) is a mixed condition of deficiency and excess, so the first step is to separate the underlying weakness from any acute flare. The timing of symptoms-whether they worsen with fatigue, cold, or stress-and the color and sensation of urine give crucial clues.
If you describe frequent but weak urination that leaves you straining, and the problem always worsens after a long day or a bout of overwork, the practitioner suspects Spleen and Kidney Qi Deficiency. The tongue is often pale with a thin white coat, and the pulse feels weak and thready. Accompanying signs like poor appetite, bloating, and a dragging lower back ache confirm that the body’s Qi is too depleted to hold urine properly.
When the urine is scanty, dark, and feels hot or burning, and you also notice lower back soreness, night sweats, and a dry mouth, the diagnosis shifts toward Kidney Yin Deficiency. The tongue appears red with little or no coating, and the pulse is thin and rapid. These signs indicate that long-standing damp-heat has dried up the body’s cooling Yin fluids, leaving a low-grade fire that irritates the bladder.
If instead you feel chilled easily, have cold hands and feet, and need to urinate multiple times at night, Kidney Yang Deficiency is more likely. The urine stream is weak and dribbling. The tongue is pale, puffy, and may have tooth marks on the edges, while the pulse is deep and slow. This pattern reflects a loss of the warming, transformative power that drives fluid metabolism, so water accumulates and coldness prevails.
During an acute flare, the urine becomes cloudy, dark yellow, and painful, often with a sense of urgency. A yellow, greasy tongue coating and a slippery, rapid pulse point to Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner. The practitioner asks about recent diet, stress, or exposure to dampness, because this excess pattern can suddenly layer on top of any underlying deficiency and must be cleared before tonifying the body.
When chronic inflammation causes a fixed, stabbing pain in the lower abdomen, and the tongue looks dark or has purple spots, Blood Stagnation is at play. The pulse may feel choppy or wiry. This pattern often develops after long-standing damp-heat or Qi deficiency has slowed the circulation of Blood in the lower burner, making urination even more difficult and painful.

### Spleen and Kidney Qi Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-and-kidney-qi-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Frequent, urgent urination that worsens with fatigue, Straining or dribbling when urinating, Deep, achy soreness in the lower back and knees, Poor appetite and loose, unformed stools, Pale, sallow complexion and cold hands and feet
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and prolonged standing, Cold, raw foods and icy drinks, Stress and worry, Damp, cold environments
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and lying down, Warm compress on the lower back, Warm, cooked, easily digested meals, Gentle daily movement (tai chi, walking)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  In this pattern, the Spleen is too weak to produce and raise clear Qi, while the Kidneys have lost the strength to consolidate and hold urine. The Spleen's failure to manage fluids lets dampness accumulate, and the Kidney's inability to govern the lower orifices leads to frequent, dribbling urination that gets markedly worse when the body is tired.
Because Qi is the body's motive force, the whole system sags - you feel a heavy, dragging sensation in the lower abdomen and a deep, achy soreness in the lower back and knees. Every episode of overwork or prolonged standing further drains the already depleted Qi, making the urinary symptoms flare.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale and swollen with tooth marks along the edges, and a white, moist coating. Pulse: deep (沉 chén), weak (弱 ruò), and fine (细 xì).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Overwork and prolonged standing drain Qi, especially the Spleen and Kidney Qi that are already deficient, causing the urinary symptoms to flare. Cold foods and environments further damage the Spleen's digestive fire and the Kidney's warming function, allowing dampness and cold to sink downward. Rest restores Qi, warmth supports the Kidneys' ability to hold urine, and easily digested foods spare the Spleen from extra work, allowing it to produce the Qi needed to lift the sinking sensation.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked, easily digested foods like congee, soups, and stews. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that further tax the Spleen. Keep the lower back and abdomen warm, and incorporate gentle daily movement like walking or tai chi - never to the point of exhaustion. Rest is medicine; short naps or lying down when tired can prevent a flare.

### Kidney Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Scanty, dark yellow urine, Burning sensation when urinating, Night sweats and five-centre heat, Lower back soreness and weakness, Dry mouth and throat, worse at night
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and fatigue, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Alcohol and coffee, Late nights and sleep deprivation, Hot, dry weather
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and adequate sleep, Cooling, moistening foods (pear, tofu), Gentle hydration, Cool environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Long-standing damp-heat from a lingering infection slowly consumes the body's cooling, moistening Kidney Yin. When Yin is depleted, deficiency heat flares up, irritating the bladder and producing scanty, dark, burning urine. The lower back-the mansion of the Kidneys-loses its nourishment, leading to the characteristic soreness and weakness that worsens with fatigue.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin body with little or no coating, dry surface, possible cracks. Pulse: fine (xi) and rapid (shu), weak at the left chi position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Yin is easily depleted by overwork, late nights, and heating foods, which generate deficiency heat that flares the urinary tract. Rest, cooling foods, and hydration replenish Yin and cool the Empty Heat, easing burning and soreness.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat cooling, moistening foods such as pears, tofu, black beans, and congee. Avoid spicy, fried, or greasy foods, alcohol, and coffee, which can worsen heat. Stay hydrated but avoid excessive fluids that strain the kidneys. Prioritize rest and avoid late nights to preserve Yin.

### Kidney Yang Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-yang-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dribbling or hesitant urination that feels better with warmth, Cold, aching pain in the lower back and knees, Waking several times at night to urinate (nocturia), Cold hands and feet, intolerance of cold, Pale, puffy tongue with teeth marks and a wet white coating
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or exposure to drafts, Overwork and fatigue, Cold, raw foods and icy drinks, Stress and anxiety, Excessive physical labor
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth on the lower back and abdomen, Moxibustion on the lower back, Gentle movement and stretching, Rest and adequate sleep, Warm, cooked meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Kidneys provide the body's warmth and transform fluids into urine. When Kidney Yang is weak, the bladder lacks the fire needed to push urine out smoothly, so it dribbles and feels incomplete. The coldness also makes the lower back and knees ache, and the urge to urinate becomes worse at night when Yang is naturally low. Over time, this deep cold weakens the body's defenses, allowing a chronic, low-grade infection to linger.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, tender body with teeth marks, wet white coating. Pulse: deep, slow, and weak, especially at the rear (Chi) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold directly depletes Kidney Yang, so warmth and moxibustion reinforce the body's fire. Overwork and stress drain Qi and Yang, making the urinary symptoms worse. Warm, nourishing foods provide the raw materials to rebuild Yang and support the bladder's function.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like lamb, ginger, cinnamon, and walnuts. Avoid raw, cold foods and iced drinks. Keep the lower back and abdomen warm with a belly band or moxibustion sticks. Gentle daily movement such as walking or tai chi helps circulate Yang Qi. Rest deeply and avoid overwork to protect your reserves.

### Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Burning or stinging pain during urination, Cloudy, dark yellow urine that may smell foul, Lower abdominal fullness and heaviness, Thirst with no desire to drink much, Feeling of heaviness in the legs
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Alcohol and spicy food, Damp, humid weather, Overwork and fatigue, Emotional stress, Greasy, fried foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Drinking plenty of water, Cool, dry environment, Rest during acute flares, Avoiding alcohol and spicy food
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Damp-Heat settling in the bladder creates the classic flare of chronic pyelonephritis. Dampness makes urine cloudy and creates a heavy, dragging sensation, while Heat causes burning and urgency. This combination obstructs the bladder's ability to empty cleanly, so even small amounts of urine feel uncomfortable and the urine appears dark and turbid.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a yellow greasy coating, thickest at the back. Pulse: slippery and rapid, more pronounced at the rear (chi) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat thrives on moisture and heat. Alcohol, spices, and greasy foods add internal heat and dampness, while humid weather saturates the body from outside. Stress stirs up liver qi, which can turn into heat and aggravate the bladder. Drinking water and resting help the body flush out the pathogens, and a cool, dry environment starves the damp-heat of what it needs to flourish.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Drink plenty of plain water to help flush the urinary tract. Avoid alcohol, coffee, spicy foods, and greasy or fried dishes, as these generate damp-heat. Keep the genital area clean and dry, and avoid sitting on damp surfaces. Light, cooling foods like cucumber, watermelon, and mung beans can help during a flare.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing pain in the lower abdomen or pelvis, Pain that worsens with pressure and at night, Dark purple or dusky tongue with stasis spots, Dark menstrual blood with clots (in women), Rough, dry skin or dark circles under the eyes
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Cold weather or exposure to drafts, Cold, raw foods and icy drinks, Emotional frustration or stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle movement and stretching, Warmth applied to lower abdomen, Warm, cooked meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Long-standing damp-heat or deficiency in the lower burner can slow the movement of Blood, causing it to congeal in the pelvic region. This creates fixed, stabbing pain that obstructs the bladder's normal opening and closing, making urination difficult and painful.
Because Blood Stagnation is a physical blockage, the pain tends to be sharp and unchanging, and it worsens with pressure or at night when circulation naturally slows.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body, possible stasis spots, distended dark sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 Sè) like a knife scraping bamboo, or wiry (弦 Xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood Stagnation is a condition of congealed, sluggish Blood. Cold and stillness further thicken and slow the Blood, while warmth and gentle movement encourage it to flow. Emotionally, frustration and stress can cause Qi to stagnate, which in turn worsens Blood stasis because Qi moves Blood. So keeping calm and moving gently directly counter the root of this pattern.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Gentle daily movement like walking or tai chi helps keep Blood circulating. Avoid sitting for long periods, and keep the lower abdomen warm with a heat pack or warm clothing. Eat warming, mildly spicy foods like ginger, turmeric, and cinnamon, and avoid icy drinks and raw foods that can congeal Blood.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM cure my chronic kidney infections?
TCM aims to correct the underlying imbalances that make you susceptible to recurrent infections. While it may not 'cure' the condition in the sense of eradicating all bacteria forever, many patients experience a significant reduction in the frequency and severity of flare-ups after consistent treatment. The goal is to strengthen your body's defenses so that it can resist infection on its own.

### Will I still need antibiotics?
During an active infection, antibiotics are often necessary to clear the bacteria quickly. TCM herbal formulas can work alongside antibiotics to resolve symptoms faster and support your body. Between infections, TCM treatment may reduce the need for prophylactic antibiotics, but you should never stop prescribed medication without consulting your doctor.

### How does acupuncture help with kidney infections?
Acupuncture points are selected to strengthen the Kidneys and Spleen, clear damp-heat from the bladder, and regulate urination. For deficiency patterns, points like Shenshu (BL-23) and Zusanli (ST-36) build Qi; for damp-heat, points like Zhongji (REN-3) and Yinlingquan (SP-9) drain excess. Many patients find that regular acupuncture reduces the dragging lower back ache and urinary urgency.

### Is it safe to take Chinese herbs with my antibiotics?
Yes, TCM herbal formulas are generally safe to combine with antibiotics when prescribed by a qualified practitioner who knows your full medication list. However, some herbs for damp-heat may have mild antibacterial effects, so your practitioner will adjust dosages accordingly. Always inform both your doctor and TCM practitioner about every medicine and supplement you take.

### What foods should I avoid?
In general, avoid spicy, greasy, and overly sweet foods, which can generate damp-heat. Alcohol and caffeine can irritate the bladder and should be minimized. Instead, focus on warm, cooked, easily digested meals like congee, steamed vegetables, and lean proteins. Drinking plenty of plain water is essential to help flush the urinary tract.

### Can TCM help if I already have kidney scarring?
TCM cannot reverse existing scar tissue, but it can improve overall kidney function and reduce the frequency of new infections that cause further damage. By strengthening the body's Qi and clearing lingering pathogens, treatment aims to preserve the remaining healthy kidney tissue.
