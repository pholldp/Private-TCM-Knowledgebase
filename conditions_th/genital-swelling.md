## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In conventional medicine, genital swelling is evaluated as a symptom of an underlying condition rather than a disease in itself. Common causes include infections (bacterial, viral, or fungal), allergic reactions, trauma, or systemic fluid retention. Diagnosis typically involves a physical exam, urine tests, blood work, and sometimes imaging to identify the root cause. Treatment is then directed at that cause, whether it be antibiotics, antifungals, anti-inflammatories, or diuretics.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment depends entirely on the diagnosis. Bacterial infections are treated with antibiotics, fungal infections with antifungals, and allergic reactions with antihistamines or corticosteroids. For swelling related to fluid retention, diuretics may be prescribed. Supportive measures such as ice packs, rest, and compression are also common. If no specific cause is found, management may focus on symptom relief and observation.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, genital swelling is seen as a sign that something is obstructing the normal flow of Qi and fluids in the lower body. The Liver channel passes directly through the genital region, so any disturbance in Liver Qi - whether from emotional stress, dietary indiscretion, or constitutional weakness - can manifest there. When Dampness and Heat combine and sink downward, they cause acute, red, hot swelling. When the Spleen is weak and fails to transform fluids, a soft, pitting swelling develops. And when Kidney Yin is depleted, a dry, burning swelling may appear at night.
The diagnostic process focuses on the character of the swelling, accompanying sensations, and overall constitution. A practitioner will ask whether the swelling is red and hot, heavy and pitting, or hard and fixed. They will examine the tongue for signs of heat, dampness, or deficiency, and feel the pulse to determine whether the pattern is excess or deficiency. This detailed differentiation allows TCM to treat the root cause, not just the symptom, which can lead to more lasting relief.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
To identify which pattern is causing genital swelling, a practitioner begins by asking about the character of the swelling, its onset, and associated sensations. The presence of redness, heat, pain, itching, heaviness, or a pitting quality each point in a different direction. The tongue and pulse are then examined to confirm the underlying imbalance.
If the swelling is acute, red, hot, and intensely painful, with a bitter taste, irritability, and perhaps yellow discharge, Damp-Heat in the Liver Channel is likely. The tongue is red with a yellow coating, and the pulse is wiry and rapid. This pattern often flares up with emotional stress or rich food.
When the swelling is accompanied by urinary urgency, burning, or cloudy urine, and there is a heavy, dragging sensation in the lower abdomen, Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner is the main suspect. The tongue shows a yellow, greasy coating and the pulse is slippery and rapid. This pattern often follows poor hygiene or a recent infection.
Chronic, pitting swelling that worsens with fatigue and improves with rest suggests Spleen Deficiency with Dampness. The person may have a poor appetite, loose stools, and a heavy sensation in the limbs. The tongue is pale with a white, greasy coating, and the pulse is weak and slow. This swelling is less red and hot than the damp-heat types.
Liver Qi Stagnation that transforms into Heat, where swelling fluctuates with mood and is accompanied by rib-side distension and a wiry pulse.
Blood Stagnation presents as a fixed, hard, purplish swelling after trauma or chronic stasis, with a purple tongue and a choppy pulse.
Finally, Kidney Yin Deficiency with Empty-Heat causes a dry, red swelling with burning that worsens at night, a red tongue with little coating, and a thin, rapid pulse. These patterns are less common but require distinct treatments.

### Damp-Heat in the Liver Channel
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-liver-channel)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Red, swollen genitals with burning pain, Bitter taste in the mouth, Yellow, greasy tongue coating, Scrotal dampness or yellow vaginal discharge
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Alcohol and rich, greasy meals, Emotional stress and anger, Hot, humid weather, Prolonged sitting
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool compress on genitals, Drinking plenty of water, Mild exercise to move Qi, Avoiding alcohol and spicy food
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver channel travels through the genitals. When emotional stress, frustration, or dietary excesses create Damp-Heat in the Liver, this heavy, hot pathogen can surge downward along the channel and settle in the genital area. The result is acute swelling, redness, and a burning sensation, often with itching or a yellow discharge.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with redder sides, yellow greasy coating thicker at centre and root. Pulse: wiry (弦 xián), slippery (滑 huá), and rapid (数 shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat thrives on rich, greasy foods and alcohol, which create more internal dampness and heat. Emotional stress and anger cause Liver Qi to stagnate, generating heat that combines with dampness. Cooling compresses and hydration help clear Heat, while gentle movement prevents Qi stagnation and encourages the body to drain Dampness.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, fried, and spicy foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which aggravate Damp-Heat. Eat more cooling, dampness-draining foods like mung beans, celery, and barley. Manage stress with gentle exercise to keep Liver Qi flowing smoothly.

### Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Burning or stinging urination, Cloudy, dark yellow urine, Foul-smelling vaginal discharge or scrotal dampness, Heaviness in the lower body, Thirst with no desire to drink
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot, humid weather, Greasy, spicy, or sweet foods, Alcohol, Prolonged sitting, Holding urine
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, dry weather, Light, bland meals, Frequent urination, Gentle walking
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Dampness and Heat combine and sink to the lower body, where they obstruct the normal flow of Qi and fluids. This stagnation causes swelling in the genital region, often with a heavy sensation, redness, and itching. The Heat component produces burning sensations during urination and a feeling of warmth, while the Dampness creates a persistent, soggy swelling and foul-smelling discharges.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, slightly swollen, with a yellow greasy coating thickest at the root. Pulse: slippery and rapid, more pronounced at the rear (Chi) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat thrives in humid environments and from greasy, spicy foods and alcohol, which add more Dampness and Heat to the body. Cool, dry air and light, bland foods help drain Dampness and clear Heat. Frequent urination is a direct way the body eliminates Damp-Heat from the Lower Burner, and gentle movement keeps Qi flowing to prevent stagnation.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid rich, greasy, spicy, and sweet foods that generate Dampness and Heat. Drink plenty of water to help flush the urinary tract. Keep the genital area clean and dry, and avoid prolonged sitting to reduce local Dampness.

### Spleen Deficiency with Dampness
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-deficiency-with-dampness)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Soft, pitting swelling of the genitals, Feeling of heaviness and distension in the lower abdomen, Loose, unformed stools, Poor appetite and bloating after meals, Fatigue and lack of energy, No redness, heat, or acute pain
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, raw foods, Overeating, Damp, humid weather, Fatigue and overwork
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth on the abdomen, Rest and lying down, Small, frequent meals, Dry, cooked foods
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen is weak, it loses the ability to transform food and fluids efficiently. This leads to an accumulation of dampness, which is heavy and tends to sink downward. Over time, this dampness settles in the lower body and genital region, causing a soft, pitting swelling that feels heavy rather than painful.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Pale, swollen body with teeth marks, white greasy coating that is moist. Pulse: Soggy (Ru) and slow (Huan), possibly slippery.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: A weak Spleen cannot handle cold or raw foods, which snuff out its digestive fire and generate more dampness. Overeating overwhelms the Spleen and leads to fluid accumulation. External dampness from weather adds to the internal burden. Rest conserves Qi, and warmth helps the Spleen's Yang transform and move fluids. Small, warm meals give the Spleen manageable work, allowing it to gradually clear dampness and reduce swelling.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like congee, soups, and steamed vegetables that are easy to digest. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that burden the Spleen. Eat small, frequent meals rather than large portions. Gentle movement like walking helps Qi circulation, but avoid overexertion. Keep the abdomen and lower back warm, especially in damp weather.

### Liver Qi Stagnation that transforms into Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-qi-stagnation-that-transforms-into-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Distending or burning sensation in genitals, Worse with anger or emotional stress, Rib pain or tightness, Bitter taste in the mouth, Irritability and explosive anger, Red face and eyes
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Anger and frustration, Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Alcohol and coffee, Hot weather
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Stress reduction, Cooling foods and drinks, Gentle exercise
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver channel runs through the genitals. When emotional stress and frustration build up, the Liver’s Qi becomes stuck. Over time that stagnation generates heat, which can travel down the channel and cause swelling, distension, and a burning sensation in the genital area. This is often an early-stage pattern before dampness accumulates.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with redder sides, yellow coating. Pulse: wiry and rapid (xian shu).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Anger and frustration stagnate Liver Qi, which generates heat that surges down to the genitals. Calming the mind, avoiding heat-producing foods, and keeping Qi moving with gentle exercise all help reverse this process. Cooling foods and stress relief directly counteract the heat, while movement prevents stagnation from building up again.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, alcohol, and coffee, which add heat. Eat more cooling, lightly cooked vegetables such as celery, cucumber, and chrysanthemum tea. Gentle daily movement like walking or yoga helps keep Qi flowing. Make time for stress management through meditation, journaling, or talking things out.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing pain in the swollen area, Hard swelling that does not pit on pressure, Purplish or dark discoloration of the skin, Pain worse at night, Possible palpable lump or mass
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold exposure, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Injury or pressure on the area, Emotional stress, Cold foods and drinks
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle movement, Warmth on the area, Light massage, Emotional calm
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When blood flow becomes sluggish or obstructed in the lower body, it pools in the tissues and creates a hard, fixed swelling. This often follows a local injury or chronic emotional tension that has congealed Qi and blood over time. The trapped blood gives the skin a dark, purplish hue, and the pain is sharp and stabbing - especially at night, when circulation naturally slows.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body, stasis spots, distended dark sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 Sè) or wiry (弦 Xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood stasis worsens with cold, which congeals blood, and with inactivity, which slows circulation. Gentle movement and warmth help to invigorate blood and dissolve stasis. Emotional stress tightens the Liver Qi, further obstructing blood flow, so calmness and stress reduction are essential.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Incorporate gentle movement like walking or tai chi to encourage blood flow. Avoid cold foods and drinks, which can congeal blood. Include small amounts of warming spices like turmeric, ginger, and black pepper in cooking. If trauma is involved, rest the area but gently move the rest of the body.

### Kidney Yin Deficiency With Empty-Heat Blazing
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-yin-deficiency-with-empty-heat-blazing)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dry, red swelling with burning sensation, Night sweats, Five-palm heat (heat in palms, soles, and chest), Lower back soreness and weakness, Thirst with preference for small sips, Symptoms worse in the evening and at night
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy and greasy foods, Alcohol, Late nights and overwork, Sexual overactivity, Hot weather
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and lying down, Cooling foods like pear and cucumber, Quiet dark room, Gentle movement like tai chi
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Kidney Yin is the body's deep cooling and moistening resource. When it runs low over time, the relative excess of Yang creates a dry, flaring heat that travels downward along the Kidney channel to the genitals.
This produces a lingering swelling that feels dry, red, and hot rather than heavy and fluid-filled, and it often worsens in the evening or at night when Yin should be dominant.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin, dry body with little or no coating, possible cracks. Pulse: fine (Xi) and rapid (Shu), may be floating-empty; Chi (rear) position weak.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: <p>Kidney Yin is easily depleted by overwork, late nights, and sexual excess, all of which consume the body's cooling and moistening resources. Spicy, warming foods and alcohol add heat that further flares the empty fire.</p><p>Rest, cooling foods, and quiet environments replenish Yin and allow the deep heat to settle, which reduces the burning swelling.</p>
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat cooling, moistening foods like pear, cucumber, tofu, and black beans. Avoid spicy, greasy, and warming foods like lamb, cinnamon, and alcohol. Rest early and avoid overwork, especially sexual overactivity, which further depletes Kidney essence.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM treat genital swelling caused by an infection?
Yes, TCM can be very effective for infections that present with Damp-Heat signs - such as redness, swelling, burning, and discharge - by clearing heat and draining dampness with formulas like Long Dan Xie Gan Tang. However, if you have a high fever, severe pain, or signs of systemic infection, seek urgent medical care (see Safety section).

### Is acupuncture used directly on the genitals?
No. Acupuncture points for genital swelling are typically located on the legs, lower abdomen, and back - such as Sanyinjiao (SP-6), Taichong (LR-3), and Zhongji (REN-3). These points influence the channels that pass through the genital area without needling the genitals themselves.

### Will I need to change my diet?
Diet plays an important role in managing dampness and heat. You will likely be advised to avoid greasy, fried, spicy foods, alcohol, and excessive sugar, which can worsen swelling. Your practitioner will give you specific recommendations based on your pattern - for example, cooling foods for Damp-Heat or warming foods for Spleen Deficiency.

### Can I combine TCM with antibiotics or other medications?
Yes, TCM can be safely used alongside conventional treatments. Herbal formulas that clear heat and drain dampness are generally compatible with antibiotics. Always inform both your TCM practitioner and your doctor about all medications you are taking, especially if you are on diuretics, as some herbs may enhance their effect.

### How long until I see results?
Many patients notice improvement within 2 to 4 weeks of starting herbs and acupuncture. Acute, hot, red swellings often respond quickly, while chronic, pitting swelling may take 6 to 8 weeks or more. Your practitioner will monitor your progress and adjust your treatment plan as needed.

### Is TCM safe for genital swelling during pregnancy?
Acupuncture can be safe during pregnancy when performed by a qualified practitioner who avoids certain points. However, many herbal formulas are contraindicated in pregnancy. Always inform your practitioner if you are pregnant or trying to conceive, so they can tailor treatment safely. If you experience any swelling with high blood pressure or sudden weight gain, seek immediate medical care.

### What if the swelling keeps coming back?
Recurrent swelling often indicates that the underlying pattern - such as Spleen Deficiency or Liver Qi Stagnation - has not been fully resolved. TCM aims to correct that root imbalance so that the symptom does not return. Your practitioner may recommend a longer course of herbs and dietary changes to prevent future episodes.
