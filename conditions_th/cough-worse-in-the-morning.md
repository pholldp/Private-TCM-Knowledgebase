## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, a cough that's worse in the morning is often linked to conditions that cause mucus to accumulate in the airways overnight. Common causes include postnasal drip from allergies or sinusitis, gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) where stomach acid irritates the throat while lying down, chronic bronchitis (especially in smokers), and asthma that worsens at night. Diagnosis typically involves a physical exam, chest X-ray, and sometimes spirometry or allergy testing.
Treatment aims at the suspected underlying cause - antihistamines for allergies, proton pump inhibitors for GERD, inhalers for asthma, or expectorants to thin mucus. While these approaches can provide relief, they often don't address why the body is producing excess phlegm in the first place, leaving many people with a recurring morning cough that never fully resolves.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment depends on the suspected cause. For postnasal drip, antihistamines, decongestants, or nasal steroid sprays are common. GERD-related cough is managed with antacids, H2 blockers, or proton pump inhibitors. Chronic bronchitis may be treated with bronchodilators, inhaled steroids, and smoking cessation. Cough suppressants like dextromethorphan may be used for dry cough, while expectorants like guaifenesin help thin mucus. Antibiotics are prescribed only if a bacterial infection is confirmed.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the Lungs are responsible for breathing and for dispersing Qi downward. A cough is a sign that Lung Qi is rebelling upward instead of descending. But why does it happen specifically in the morning? Overnight, when the body is still and horizontal, any phlegm that has accumulated in the lungs settles and pools. In the morning, as your body's Yang Qi begins to stir and you start moving, the Lungs try to expel this accumulation - resulting in a burst of coughing.
Where that phlegm comes from is the real key. TCM teaches that the Spleen produces phlegm and the Lungs store it. When the Spleen is weak - often from poor diet, overwork, or worry - it fails to transform fluids properly, and dampness congeals into phlegm. This phlegm then drifts upward and settles in the Lungs. So a morning cough with lots of phlegm is often not just a Lung problem but a Spleen problem.
Other organ systems can also be involved. If the Liver is stressed and overheated, its Fire can flare up and attack the Lungs, causing a sudden, hacking morning cough with a bitter taste. External pathogens like Wind-Cold can invade and lodge in the Lungs, making the cough worse in the early morning chill. This is why the same symptom - a morning cough - can have very different treatments depending on whether the root is weak digestion, emotional stress, or an external invasion.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A practitioner starts by asking what the phlegm looks like and how the cough feels. The colour, thickness, and amount of sputum first thing in the morning are the biggest clues, because different patterns produce very different phlegm. They also check the tongue coating and pulse to confirm what they suspect.
If the cough brings up lots of white, sticky or gluey phlegm and the chest feels heavy, the picture points to Damp-Phlegm in the Lungs. The tongue typically shows a thick, greasy white coat and the pulse feels slippery. This is the most common morning cough pattern, driven by weak digestion creating dampness that settles in the lungs overnight.
When the sputum is also white but thin, frothy, and accompanied by a sensation of cold in the chest or an aversion to cold weather, it suggests Cold-Phlegm in the Lungs. The tongue coating is white and may be slippery, and the pulse is slippery but slower than in pure Damp-Phlegm. Cold has congealed the fluids, making the phlegm more watery.
A cough that produces yellow, sticky, or even foul-smelling phlegm points to Phlegm-Heat in the Lungs. The person often feels hot, thirsty, and may have a red face. The tongue is red with a yellow, greasy coating, and the pulse is rapid and slippery. The heat “cooks” the phlegm, changing its colour and smell.
If the sputum is frothy and white, and the cough is clearly worse after lying flat or in the early morning, Phlegm-Fluids in the Lungs may be the cause. This is a more fluid, dilute type of phlegm accumulation. The tongue is often pale and puffy with a slippery white coat, and the pulse is slippery or wiry.
An acute morning cough with thin, white, frothy sputum, chills, a scratchy throat, and a floating tight pulse indicates Wind-Cold invading the Lungs. This pattern comes on suddenly, often after exposure to cold or wind, and the cough is usually accompanied by typical cold symptoms like a stuffy nose and body aches.
When the cough is dry, paroxysmal, and worse in the morning alongside a bitter taste in the mouth, irritability, and perhaps rib-side tension, the practitioner looks for Liver Fire insulting the Lungs. The tongue is red with a thin yellow coat, and the pulse is wiry and rapid. Stress and anger often trigger or worsen this pattern.

### Damp-Phlegm in the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-phlegm-in-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Copious white sticky phlegm, easy to expectorate, Feeling of fullness or heaviness in the chest, Poor appetite and loose stools, Fatigue and heaviness of the body
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, damp weather, Heavy, greasy, or dairy-rich meals, Lying down after eating, Cold drinks and raw foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, cooked foods, Gentle exercise, Dry, airy environment, Moderate portions, no overeating
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen is weak, it fails to transform fluids properly, and dampness accumulates, eventually congealing into phlegm that settles in the Lungs. Overnight, while the body is still, this phlegm pools in the airways, and the morning cough is the Lung's effort to expel the accumulation. The result is a heavy, productive cough with copious white sticky phlegm, often accompanied by chest congestion and fatigue.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks and a thick, white, greasy coating. Pulse: slippery (huá), like beads rolling under the fingers, and may feel soft or soggy.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Phlegm forms when the Spleen's digestive fire is too weak to process fluids, so cold, raw, and heavy foods worsen the condition by adding more dampness. Warm, cooked meals and gentle movement strengthen the Spleen and help the Lung Qi descend, making it easier to clear phlegm. A dry environment and avoiding lying flat after meals prevent phlegm from pooling in the chest overnight.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods, as well as dairy and sweets, which all encourage dampness and phlegm. Favor warm, cooked meals like soups and stews with ginger or cardamom. Gentle morning movement such as walking or stretching helps circulate Qi and clear phlegm from the chest.

### Cold-Phlegm in the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/cold-phlegm-in-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Thin, white, frothy sputum, Sensation of cold in the chest, Cold hands and feet, No thirst, preference for warm drinks, Cough worse in the early morning
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or drafts, Cold drinks and raw foods, Heavy, greasy, or dairy-rich meals, Lying flat
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm drinks, Warm compress on chest, Spicy, warming foods, Steam inhalation
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the body is chilled over time, fluids congeal into a thin, frothy phlegm that settles in the Lungs. Yang Qi is naturally weakest at dawn, so the cold phlegm obstructs the airways most at that hour, triggering a wet, rattling cough that eases once you are up and moving.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with a white greasy or slippery coating. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and slow (迟 chí), possibly wiry (弦 xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold congeals fluids into phlegm, so warmth from drinks, foods, and compresses helps dissolve it. Lying flat allows phlegm to pool in the airways, making the morning cough worse; sitting up helps drain it. Dairy and raw foods are damp and cold, which further burden the Spleen and create more phlegm.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold and raw foods, especially in the morning. Drink warm ginger tea throughout the day to help dissolve phlegm. Keep the chest and upper back warm, and use a humidifier if the air is dry. Gentle exercise like walking can help circulate Yang Qi.

### Phlegm-Heat in the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-heat-in-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cough with thick yellow or green sputum, Chest oppression and feeling of heat, Worse in the morning upon waking, Thirst with desire to drink, Restlessness and irritability
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy or greasy food, Hot, stuffy environment, Emotional stress, Smoking, Lying down after eating
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, fresh air, Drinking warm water, Eating pears or radish, Gentle breathing exercises, Rest
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Heat and Phlegm combine in the Lungs to form a sticky, inflammatory obstruction. Overnight, while the body is still, this hot phlegm settles and stagnates in the airways. When you wake and begin to move, the Lungs try to expel the accumulated phlegm, triggering a productive morning cough with thick yellow sputum.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a yellow greasy coating, possibly swollen at the front with red prickles. Pulse: slippery and rapid (滑数 huá shuò), strongest at the right cun position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Spicy and greasy foods create more Heat and Phlegm, directly feeding the pattern. Emotional stress stirs up Liver Fire, which can insult the Lungs and worsen the cough. Cool air and cooling foods help clear Lung Heat, while gentle breathing exercises and rest allow the Lung Qi to descend naturally.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and deep-fried foods that add Heat and Phlegm. Eat more pears, radish, and loquat to cool and moisten the Lungs. Keep well hydrated, breathe fresh air, and avoid smoking.

### Phlegm-Fluids in the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-fluids-in-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Frothy white or grey sputum, Cough worse after lying down, Chest stuffiness that eases after expectoration, Gurgling or rattling sound in throat, Loose stools
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Lying flat, Cold, damp weather, Heavy, greasy, or dairy-rich meals, Overexertion
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm drinks, Expectoration, Sitting upright, Warm dry weather
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen's Yang energy is weak, it cannot properly transform and transport fluids. These unprocessed fluids accumulate and turn into a thin, watery phlegm that collects in the Lungs. Overnight, lying flat allows this fluid to pool, so the cough is worst upon waking and large amounts of frothy white sputum are coughed up.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Pale, swollen body with teeth marks, thick white greasy or slippery coating. Pulse: Slippery (Hua) or wiry (Xian).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Lying flat allows fluids to pool in the lungs, triggering the cough; sitting upright helps them drain. Cold and damp weaken Spleen Yang, which is already deficient, so warmth and dry conditions support its function. Expectoration provides immediate relief by clearing the obstruction.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep warm, especially the chest and upper back. Avoid dairy, cold drinks, and raw foods which burden the Spleen. Gentle daily movement like walking helps circulate Qi and fluids. A warm, dry environment is best.

### Wind-Cold invading the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/wind-cold-invading-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cough with thin white watery or frothy phlegm, Chills and aversion to cold, Nasal congestion with clear watery discharge, Itchy throat, Absence of sweating, Body aches and headache, Heavy or muffled cough sound
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or drafts, Cold drinks and raw foods, Exposure to wind, Overexertion
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth and staying covered, Warm drinks, Steam inhalation, Rest
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Wind-Cold is an external pathogen that attacks the body's surface and first enters the Lungs. It blocks the normal downward flow of Lung Qi, trapping it and forcing it upward as a cough. In the early morning, the body's rising Yang Qi struggles against the lodged Cold, triggering a burst of coughing with thin, white, frothy phlegm.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: normal or slightly pale body with a thin white moist coating. Pulse: floating (fú) and tight (jǐn).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Wind-Cold is an exterior pathogen that constricts the Lung Qi. Warmth and rest support the body's defensive Qi to push the pathogen out, while cold and wind tighten the exterior further, locking in the cough. Warm fluids and steam gently open the pores and soothe the airways, helping the Lungs resume their normal downward flow.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the neck and chest warmly covered, especially in the early morning. Sip hot ginger tea with a pinch of brown sugar throughout the day. Eat warm, easily digested foods like rice congee with spring onion; avoid raw, cold, or dairy-heavy meals that can create more phlegm. Steam inhalation with a few drops of eucalyptus oil can help open the nasal passages and soothe the throat.

### Liver Fire insulting the Lungs
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-fire-insulting-the-lungs)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Forceful cough in sudden bouts, Bitter taste in the mouth, Irritability and quick temper, Pain or distension along the ribs, Dry throat with sticky phlegm hard to expectorate, Face turns red during coughing
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Anger and frustration, Spicy or fried food, Alcohol, Hot, stuffy environment
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Stress reduction, Cooling foods and drinks, Gentle exercise, Deep breathing
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver is responsible for the smooth flow of Qi. When stress and frustration cause Liver Qi to stagnate, it can turn into Fire. This Fire flares upward along the Liver channel and attacks the Lungs, forcing Lung Qi to rebel upward instead of descending. The result is a sudden, forceful cough. It is often worse in the morning because Liver Qi is naturally more active at that time, and the accumulated Fire easily flares after a night of rest, irritating the Lungs.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with redder sides, thin dry yellow coating. Pulse: wiry and rapid, especially at the left middle position (Liver).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Emotional stress, especially anger and frustration, causes Liver Qi to stagnate and turn into Fire, which then flares upward to attack the Lungs. Spicy, greasy, and hot foods add more Heat to the body, feeding the Fire. Cooling and calming habits, on the other hand, help clear Heat and smooth the Liver Qi, allowing the Lungs to descend normally and stopping the cough.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, all of which add Heat. Eat more cooling, moistening foods like pear, cucumber, and chrysanthemum tea. Practice deep breathing and gentle exercise like Tai Chi to help Qi flow smoothly, and find healthy outlets for frustration and stress.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Why is my cough always worse in the morning?
In TCM, phlegm tends to settle and pool in the lungs while you sleep because you're lying still and horizontal. When you wake and start moving, your body's Yang Qi rises and the Lungs try to clear that accumulation, triggering a productive cough. The type of phlegm you bring up gives clues: white and sticky suggests Damp-Phlegm from weak digestion; yellow and thick suggests Phlegm-Heat; thin and frothy with a cold sensation points to Cold-Phlegm.

### What does the colour of my morning phlegm mean?
White, sticky, and easy to bring up usually indicates Damp-Phlegm - your Spleen isn't processing fluids well. Thin, white, and frothy with a feeling of coldness points to Cold-Phlegm. Yellow, thick, or greenish phlegm that may smell bad indicates Phlegm-Heat, often from infection or too much spicy, greasy food. If your phlegm is mostly watery and frothy, you may have Phlegm-Fluids from long-term Spleen weakness. A TCM practitioner will also look at your tongue coating and pulse to confirm.

### Can acupuncture help with a morning cough?
Yes, acupuncture can be very effective, especially when combined with herbal medicine. Points like Fenglong (ST-40) are famous for transforming phlegm, while Zusanli (ST-36) strengthens the Spleen to stop phlegm at its source. Feishu (BL-13) helps the Lungs descend Qi properly. Most patients have weekly sessions for 4-8 weeks, and many notice less morning congestion and easier expectoration after just a few treatments.

### How long does it take for Chinese herbs to work for a morning cough?
You may notice some improvement within the first week - easier breathing, less chest heaviness, or phlegm that's easier to bring up. Full resolution of a chronic morning cough typically takes 4-8 weeks, because herbs aren't just suppressing the cough but gradually strengthening the Spleen and clearing accumulated phlegm. Sticking with the formula even after the cough subsides helps prevent recurrence.

### Can I take Chinese herbs alongside my regular cough medicine?
In many cases, yes, but it's essential to tell both your TCM practitioner and your doctor about everything you're taking. Some herbs can interact with medications - for example, Ma Huang (Ephedra) should not be combined with stimulants or certain heart medications. Even if your formula doesn't contain Ma Huang, always get professional guidance. Never stop prescribed medications abruptly without consulting your doctor.

### What foods should I avoid if I have morning cough?
Dairy products, greasy and fried foods, and cold or raw foods are the biggest culprits - they create dampness and phlegm. Sugar and refined carbohydrates also weaken the Spleen and encourage phlegm production. Instead, favour warm, cooked meals like soups and stews, and include foods like pear (for dry or hot coughs) or ginger (for cold coughs). Drinking warm water throughout the day helps thin mucus.

### Can stress really cause a morning cough?
Yes. In TCM, the Liver is easily affected by stress and frustration, and when Liver Qi stagnates it can turn into Fire. This Fire can flare up and attack the Lungs, causing a sudden, hacking cough that's often worse in the morning and may come with a bitter taste or rib pain. This pattern - Liver Fire insulting the Lungs - responds well to herbs that clear Liver heat and direct Lung Qi downward, along with stress management.

### Is a morning cough a sign of something serious?
A morning cough that lasts more than three weeks, produces blood, or comes with fever, chest pain, shortness of breath, or unintentional weight loss should be evaluated by a doctor to rule out serious conditions. TCM is excellent for chronic phlegm patterns but is not a substitute for urgent medical care when red-flag symptoms are present. See our Safety section for a full list of warning signs.
