## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In conventional medicine, dark circles under the eyes are understood as a combination of thin, translucent skin revealing the blood vessels beneath, and sometimes excess pigment.
They can be caused by genetics, aging (which thins skin and loses fat), allergies (the 'allergic shiners'), fatigue, dehydration, and lifestyle factors. Diagnosis is visual; treatment often focuses on topical creams, laser therapy, fillers, or addressing underlying allergies.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatments range from topical creams (retinoids, vitamin C, caffeine) to procedures like chemical peels, laser therapy, and dermal fillers to thicken the skin or reduce pigment. For allergy-related circles, antihistamines and avoiding triggers are recommended. These can help, but they often address the surface appearance rather than the internal imbalances that make the circles recur.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the skin beneath the eyes is like a delicate mirror reflecting the state of your blood, fluids, and organ reserves. This area is governed by the Spleen and Kidney systems, and it depends on a rich supply of nourishing blood to stay bright and even-toned. When that supply is low or stagnant, or when fluids accumulate as dampness, the mirror clouds over - and dark circles appear.
Blood plays the starring role. If your blood is deficient, the skin becomes thin and pale, allowing the dark vessels underneath to show through. If your blood is also stagnant - often from stress, poor sleep, or emotional strain - the sluggish flow pools in the tiny vessels, creating a bruised, purple-blue shadow that deepens with fatigue. This is the most common pattern, and it often comes with a pale tongue, scanty periods, and dizziness.
The Kidney system provides the deep yin that moistens and plumps the skin. When Kidney Yin runs low from overwork, aging, or chronic stress, the under-eye area loses its support, becoming thin, dry, and shadowed. At the same time, empty heat rises, further darkening the skin. These circles tend to look sunken and come with lower back soreness, night sweats, and a dry mouth.
Finally, the Spleen manages fluids. A weak Spleen fails to transform fluids, which turn into dampness - a heavy, turbid substance that settles in the loose under-eye skin, creating puffy, dull circles that feel heavy. This pattern is linked to bloating, loose stools, and a sluggish feeling, and it worsens with rich foods or humid weather. Because each pattern has a different root, TCM treatment is tailored to whether you need to build blood, move stagnation, nourish yin, or drain dampness.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner starts by asking about the appearance and feel of the dark circles, as well as your overall energy, sleep, digestion, and any other symptoms. The quality of the discolouration - whether it is dull, dry, or puffy - and the timing of when it worsens offer early clues that guide the diagnosis toward one pattern or another.
If the circles look dull and are accompanied by fatigue, pale lips, dizziness, or menstrual irregularities, the practitioner suspects Blood Deficiency and Stagnation. Emotional stress and poor sleep often drive this pattern by weakening liver blood and slowing circulation. The tongue may appear pale with a thin coating, and the pulse feels thin or choppy.
When the dark circles have a dry, almost sunken quality and come with lower back soreness, night sweats, dry mouth, or a feeling of heat in the palms and soles, Kidney Yin Deficiency is likely. This pattern is more common with aging or constitutional weakness. The tongue is red with little coating, and the pulse is thin and rapid.
If the circles look puffy and dull, and you also feel heavy in the limbs, bloated, or have loose stools, Spleen Deficiency with Dampness is the probable cause. Poor diet or a weak digestive system fails to transform fluids, allowing dampness to settle around the eyes, especially after rich, greasy foods or in humid weather. The tongue coating is thick and greasy, and the pulse feels slippery.

### Blood Deficiency and Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-deficiency-and-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dusky or purplish dark circles, Pale or sallow face with a dusky tinge, Scanty or late menstruation with dark clots, Dizziness or palpitations, Fatigue and low energy
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Emotional stress, Lack of sleep or overwork, Cold, raw, or greasy foods, Sedentary lifestyle, Excessive screen time
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Adequate sleep, Warm, easily digestible meals, Gentle exercise, Stress management, Applying a warm compress
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The delicate skin under the eyes relies on a rich supply of nourishing blood to stay bright and even-toned. When blood is deficient, this area becomes thin and pale, allowing the dark vessels beneath to show through.
Stagnation compounds the problem: sluggish blood flow pools in the tiny vessels, creating a dusky, purplish discoloration that looks like a bruise. Emotional stress and chronic sleep loss often trigger this by draining the liver blood that keeps circulation smooth.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body with a purple cast, thin, possible stasis spots, thin white coat. Pulse: fine and choppy, often wiry at the left Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Stress and poor sleep drain liver blood, while cold and inactivity congeal blood, worsening stagnation. Rest and warm, nourishing foods rebuild blood; gentle movement and warmth promote smooth flow, gradually lightening the dark discoloration.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods rich in blood-nourishing ingredients: dark leafy greens, beets, red dates, goji berries, and small amounts of liver or lean red meat. Avoid raw, cold foods and icy drinks, which weaken the spleen and impair blood production.
Prioritize 7-8 hours of sleep and take breaks from screens to rest the eyes. Gentle daily movement like walking or yoga helps blood circulate without depleting energy.

### Kidney Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dark circles with a thin, dry, almost translucent quality, Lower back and knee soreness or weakness, Night sweats and a feeling of heat in the palms, soles, and chest, Dry mouth and throat, especially at night, Insomnia or restless sleep with vivid dreams
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Lack of sleep or overwork, Spicy, fried, or drying foods, Excessive heat, saunas, or hot baths, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Consistent, early bedtime, Cooling, moistening foods like pear and cucumber, Gentle exercise, Stress management
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Kidney Yin is the body's deep cooling and nourishing reserve. When it runs low-often from overwork, aging, or chronic stress-the delicate skin around the eyes loses its support and becomes thin, dry, and shadowed. At the same time, the lack of Yin allows empty heat to rise, which further darkens and dries the periorbital area, producing the classic dark circles that deepen with fatigue and time.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin, and dry with little or no coating; cracks may be visible. Pulse: fine and rapid (细数, xì shuò), especially weak at the left rear position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Yin is like a deep well of cooling water. Overwork, late nights, and stress drain that well, while spicy foods and excessive heat act like a fire that evaporates it further. When Yin is depleted, empty heat rises and shows itself as dark circles, dryness, and restlessness. Sleep, calming routines, and moistening foods slowly refill the well, quenching the heat and restoring the skin's natural support.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Prioritize deep, restorative sleep before 11 p.m., as the hours before midnight are the most replenishing for Yin. Avoid overwork and prolonged screen time, which drain the Kidney reserves.
Eat cooling, moistening foods like black sesame, walnuts, goji berries, pears, and cucumber. Steer clear of spicy, fried, or overly stimulating foods that generate heat and consume precious Yin.

### Spleen Deficiency with Dampness
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-deficiency-with-dampness)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dull, dark circles with puffiness, Heavy sensation in the body and limbs, Abdominal bloating, worse after eating, Loose, unformed stools, Poor appetite and fatigue
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overeating or irregular meals, Cold, raw, or greasy foods, Damp, humid weather, Sedentary lifestyle, Excessive worry or pensiveness
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, easily digestible meals, Gentle exercise, Adequate sleep, Dry, warm environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is responsible for transforming food and fluids into usable energy. When it becomes weak, fluids are not processed properly and turn into dampness-a heavy, turbid substance that tends to settle and stagnate. Because the skin around the eyes is delicate and loose, this dampness easily pools there, creating puffiness and a dull, dark discoloration that looks like shadows under the eyes.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks, white greasy coating, moist surface. Pulse: soggy (Ru) and slow (Huan), often weak at the right Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: The Spleen dislikes dampness and irregular eating; cold, raw foods introduce more dampness, while worry knots its Qi. Warm, cooked meals and light movement strengthen Spleen Qi and help it transform fluids, so dampness is drained rather than settling under the eyes. Rest and a dry environment reduce the burden on the Spleen, allowing it to recover.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked meals like soups and stews; avoid cold, raw, greasy, and dairy-heavy foods that create dampness. Chew thoroughly and keep meal times regular. A short, gentle walk after eating aids digestion. Dry, warm environments are best-limit exposure to damp, humid conditions.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture really help dark circles under the eyes?
Yes - acupuncture can improve local blood circulation and address the underlying pattern causing the circles. Fine needles are often placed around the eyes and on body points that regulate the Spleen, Kidney, or Liver. Many patients notice a brighter, less puffy appearance after a few sessions, especially when combined with herbs.

### Do the herbs have side effects?
TCM herbal formulas are generally gentle when prescribed by a qualified practitioner. They are tailored to your specific pattern, so side effects are rare. However, if you notice any digestive upset or skin reactions, inform your practitioner - the formula can be adjusted. Always disclose any medications you’re taking to avoid interactions.

### How long until I see results?
Improvements in energy and sleep often come first, within 2-3 weeks. Visible lightening of dark circles typically takes 4-6 weeks, though stubborn patterns may require 2-3 months. Consistency with herbs, acupuncture, and lifestyle changes is key.

### Can I still use my regular eye cream or skincare?
Absolutely. TCM treatments work from the inside out, so you can continue your usual topical products. Just let your practitioner know if you’re using prescription retinoids or undergoing laser treatments, as blood-moving herbs might increase skin sensitivity.

### Will the dark circles come back after treatment?
If the underlying pattern is fully corrected and you maintain healthy habits, the circles are less likely to return. However, if you return to chronic stress, poor sleep, or a diet that weakens your Spleen, the imbalance can recur. Your practitioner will give you long-term dietary and lifestyle advice to prevent this.

### Is TCM safe for dark circles during pregnancy?
Many acupuncture points and herbs are safe during pregnancy, but some are contraindicated. Always inform your practitioner if you are pregnant or trying to conceive. They will modify the treatment to support both you and your baby. For urgent concerns, see the Safety section.
