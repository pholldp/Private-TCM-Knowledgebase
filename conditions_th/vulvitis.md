## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Vulvitis is inflammation of the vulva, the external female genital area. It can cause itching, burning, redness, swelling, and sometimes abnormal discharge. Common causes include yeast infections, bacterial vaginosis, trichomoniasis, contact dermatitis from soaps or fabrics, and skin conditions like lichen sclerosus. Diagnosis is usually made through a pelvic exam, swabs to test for infection, and occasionally a skin biopsy if the cause is unclear.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment depends on the cause. Yeast infections are treated with antifungal creams or oral fluconazole. Bacterial infections may require antibiotics. Steroid creams are used to reduce inflammation, and antihistamines can help with itching. Avoiding irritants-such as scented products, tight clothing, and excessive moisture-is often recommended. For chronic, non-infectious vulvitis, topical estrogen or immune-modulating creams may be prescribed.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM understands vulvar itching primarily through the Liver, whose channel wraps around the genitals. When the Liver's Qi is disrupted-often by emotional stress, frustration, or a diet heavy in greasy, spicy foods-it can stagnate and generate Heat. This Heat then combines with Dampness (often from a weakened Spleen that fails to transform fluids) and surges downward along the Liver channel to the vulva.
The result is intense burning, redness, and a thick yellow discharge-the classic Damp-Heat in the Liver Channel pattern.
Sometimes the Damp-Heat settles more deeply in the lower burner, creating a warm, stagnant environment that allows parasitic factors to thrive. This is the Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner pattern, where the itch takes on a distinctive crawling, insect-like quality, and the discharge may be frothy or curd-like.
Here, the body's internal terrain has shifted to favor an overgrowth that conventional medicine might call a yeast infection or trichomoniasis-but the TCM treatment focuses on clearing the Damp-Heat and drying the swamp that let it flourish.
Not all vulvar itching is about Heat and Dampness. When the Kidney and Liver Yin are depleted-through chronic illness, overwork, multiple childbirths, or simply aging-the delicate tissues of the vulva lose their nourishment and moisture. This Yin deficiency creates a dry, wind-like itch that can be relentless, often worse at night and with little or no discharge.
The skin may appear pale, thin, or even cracked. In this case, the treatment must rebuild Yin and moisten the tissues, not just clear pathogens.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the quality of the itching and the nature of any discharge. Burning pain versus a dry, crawling sensation points in very different directions. The color, texture, and smell of the discharge, as well as accompanying signs like thirst, mood, or dizziness, help narrow the picture to one of three common patterns.
When the itch feels burning and is accompanied by yellow, sticky, foul-smelling discharge, Damp-Heat in the Liver Channel is the likely culprit. The person may also feel irritable, have a bitter taste in the mouth, and show a red tongue with a thick yellow greasy coating. The pulse tends to be wiry, slippery, and rapid, reflecting heat and dampness surging downward along the liver pathway.
If the itching is described as an intense crawling sensation, as if insects were moving, and the discharge is frothy or curd-like (like cottage cheese) with a strong odor, the pattern shifts to Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner. This indicates that damp-heat has brewed for some time, giving rise to parasitic factors. The tongue is still red with a yellow greasy coat, but the pulse is more slippery and rapid without the wiry quality, and the crawling sensation is the key differentiator.
In chronic or recurrent cases, the itching is dry rather than burning or crawling, and the genital tissues may appear pale, dry, or even thickened. This is Kidney and Liver Yin Deficiency. There is little to no discharge, but there may be a feeling of heat in the palms and soles, dizziness, and tinnitus. The tongue is red with little or no coating, and the pulse is thin, wiry, and rapid-signs of malnourished tissues and deficiency heat.

### Damp-Heat in the Liver Channel
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-liver-channel)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Genital burning and itching, Foul-smelling, yellow sticky vaginal discharge, Bitter taste in the mouth, Irritability and frustration, Rib-side fullness or discomfort
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy and greasy foods, Alcohol, Emotional stress, Hot, humid weather, Tight synthetic clothing
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool compresses, Light, bland diet, Avoiding alcohol, Stress management, Keeping the area dry
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When stress, frustration, or a diet rich in greasy and spicy foods upsets the Liver, Qi stagnates and turns into Heat. This Heat combines with Dampness (often from a weakened Spleen) and follows the Liver channel downward to the genital area. The result is the intense burning, itching, and yellow sticky discharge typical of this pattern.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Red body with redder sides, thick yellow greasy coating especially at centre and root. Pulse: Wiry, slippery, and rapid, most prominent at the left middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Spicy, greasy, and rich foods, along with alcohol, directly generate Dampness and Heat in the body, the very culprits behind this pattern. Emotional stress stirs Liver Qi, which can transform into Fire and combine with Dampness to rush downward. Cooling compresses and a bland diet help clear Heat and drain Dampness, while keeping the area dry prevents external Dampness from adding to the problem. Stress reduction keeps the Liver smooth and prevents the upward and downward surge of Heat.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Steer clear of spicy, greasy, and deep-fried foods, as well as alcohol and excessive sugar-these all create Dampness and Heat. Instead, eat plenty of lightly cooked vegetables, whole grains, and cooling foods like cucumber and watermelon. Keep the genital area clean and dry, wear breathable cotton underwear, and avoid tight synthetic clothing. Managing emotional stress through gentle exercise like walking or yoga can prevent Liver Qi from stagnating and flaring up.

### Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Crawling sensation as if insects are moving, Frothy or curd-like yellow discharge, Foul-smelling discharge, Heavy sensation in lower body or legs, Burning or painful urination, Lower abdominal fullness and distension
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot, humid weather, Spicy and greasy foods, Alcohol and sugar, Sitting on damp surfaces, Tight synthetic clothing
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, dry weather, Light, bland diet, Herbal sitz baths, Loose cotton underwear, Staying hydrated
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Damp-Heat settles in the lower burner, it creates a warm, moist environment that is perfect for parasites to thrive. These parasites irritate the genital tissues, producing an intense crawling-like itch that feels as if something is moving. The Heat component adds burning and redness, while Dampness causes swelling, heaviness, and a frothy or curd-like, foul-smelling discharge.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a thick yellow greasy coating, especially at the root. Pulse: slippery (滑, huá) and rapid (数, shuò), more pronounced at the rear (Chi) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat thrives in hot, humid conditions and is aggravated by foods that create more internal dampness and heat, such as spicy, greasy, and sugary items. Cooling, drying measures-like a light diet, dry environment, and herbal washes-directly counteract these pathogenic factors, while loose clothing and hydration help the body drain dampness and clear heat from the lower burner.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, spicy, and sugary foods, which feed damp-heat. Keep the genital area clean and dry; wear loose, breathable cotton underwear. Avoid sitting on damp surfaces. Drinking plenty of water helps flush out heat, and herbal sitz baths with Ku Shen (Sophora root) and She Chuang Zi (Cnidium fruit) can soothe itching and dry dampness.

### Kidney and Liver Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-and-liver-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Severe dry itching, Vaginal dryness and pale, thin skin, Worse at night, Night sweats, heat in palms and soles, Dizziness and tinnitus
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, fried, or drying foods, Emotional stress, Overwork and late nights, Hot weather or saunas
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, calm environment, Rest and adequate sleep, Moistening foods (pears, tofu), Gentle exercise like tai chi
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver and Kidneys nourish the genital tissues. When their Yin is depleted by chronic illness, overwork, or aging, the tissues become dry and malnourished. This dryness creates a wind-like itching that is severe and persistent, often worse at night when Yin is naturally lower.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin body with cracks, little or no coating, dry. Pulse: fine, rapid, and wiry.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney and Liver Yin Deficiency creates internal heat and dryness. Spicy foods, stress, and overwork further deplete Yin and aggravate heat, intensifying itching. Rest, cooling foods, and a calm environment replenish Yin and soothe the tissues, relieving the dryness and wind that cause the itch.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat moistening, cooling foods such as pear, tofu, black sesame, and cucumber. Avoid spicy, fried, and drying foods like coffee and alcohol. Prioritize rest and avoid overwork, especially late nights, to preserve Yin.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### How quickly can TCM relieve vulvar itching?
Many women notice significant relief within the first week of treatment, especially when using a combination of internal herbs and external herbal washes. The external wash can soothe the itching almost immediately, while the internal formula begins to shift the underlying pattern. For acute Damp-Heat conditions, the itch often subsides substantially within 1-2 weeks; chronic Yin deficiency may take 3-6 weeks to see a lasting change.

### Can I use TCM alongside my over-the-counter antifungal cream?
Yes, they can generally be used together. Apply the antifungal cream at a different time than the herbal wash or ointment, and let your TCM practitioner know what you are using. The internal herbs will not interfere with topical antifungals. In fact, many patients find that TCM reduces their reliance on repeated antifungal treatments over time.

### Is acupuncture used directly on the vulva?
No, acupuncture needles are not placed on the vulva itself. Points on the lower abdomen, sacrum, legs, and feet are used to affect the channels that pass through the genital area. These points can powerfully redirect Qi and clear Damp-Heat without any need for local needling. Moxibustion (heat therapy) on the sacrum is also commonly used for Yin deficiency patterns.

### What dietary changes will help the most?
The single most important change is to reduce foods that create Dampness and Heat: sugar, dairy, fried foods, alcohol, and very spicy dishes. Instead, emphasize lightly cooked vegetables, whole grains like barley and rice, and modest amounts of lean protein. Drinking plenty of warm water and avoiding iced drinks also supports the Spleen’s ability to manage fluids.

### Will the itching come back after I stop the herbs?
When the underlying pattern is fully resolved, recurrences are much less likely. Your practitioner will typically continue treatment for a short while after symptoms disappear to strengthen the body and prevent relapse. If you notice the itch returning during times of stress, after dietary indiscretions, or around your period, a brief follow-up course of herbs can often nip it in the bud.

### Is TCM safe for vulvar itching during pregnancy?
Yes, but it must be managed by a practitioner experienced in pregnancy care. Many herbs are contraindicated in pregnancy, so self-treatment is not safe. Gentle external washes with mild herbs can often be used, and acupuncture with careful point selection is generally safe after the first trimester. Always inform your practitioner if you are pregnant or trying to conceive.
