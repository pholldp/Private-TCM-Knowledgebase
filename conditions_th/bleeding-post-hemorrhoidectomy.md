## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Hemorrhoidectomy is a common surgical procedure to remove hemorrhoids, and some spotting or light bleeding is normal in the days following surgery. However, significant or persistent bleeding can be a complication, often from an unhealed surgical site, infection, or a blood vessel that wasn't fully sealed. Western medicine typically monitors for hemorrhage, manages pain, and may use cauterization or reoperation if necessary.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Standard management includes rest, ice packs, stool softeners to prevent straining, and pain control. In cases of active bleeding, doctors may apply direct pressure, use topical hemostatic agents, or perform a minor procedure to cauterize the bleeding vessel. Severe bleeding may require surgical re-exploration.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM understands post-surgical bleeding through the lens of the body's internal balance. After hemorrhoid surgery, the local tissues are injured and vulnerable. If there is pre-existing Heat in the Blood, or if infection and inflammation develop, that Heat can intensify and force blood out of the vessels recklessly, causing bright red bleeding. This is a classic Heat pattern - the blood is pushed, not leaked.
But Heat is only one possibility. Damp-Heat, a sticky pathogenic mix, can pour down into the anus, congesting and inflaming the delicate vessels, leading to burning, heavy bleeding. Qi and Blood Stagnation, often from the trauma of surgery itself, creates blockages that cause blood to break free in dark clots with fixed pain. And if the Spleen is weak - the organ that holds blood in its vessels - then blood simply oozes out because the body lacks the strength to contain it, producing pale, intermittent bleeding with profound fatigue.
In TCM, the color, sensation, and timing of the bleeding are diagnostic clues. Bright red blood that spurts suggests Heat or Damp-Heat. Dark blood with clots points to Stagnation. Pale, slow oozing with exhaustion indicates Spleen Qi Sinking. A high fever with a red-hot, agonizing wound signals Toxic-Heat, a severe infection that requires urgent medical attention. Because the root cause varies, treatment must be tailored - cooling, clearing, moving, lifting, or detoxifying - not just a generic stop-bleeding approach.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
Heat in the Blood is the most common pattern after hemorrhoid surgery, and it announces itself with fresh red bleeding that may spurt or drip. The tongue is red with a thin yellow coating, and the pulse feels rapid. There is usually little pain or burning, just bright blood that signals heat damaging the tiny vessels at the wound site.
Damp-Heat in the Large Intestine adds a distinct burning sensation around the anus and a feeling of heaviness or incomplete emptying. The bleeding is still bright red but often heavier. The tongue coating is yellow and greasy, and the pulse is wiry and rapid. These clues point to heat mixed with dampness, which makes the area swollen and prone to ooze.
Qi and Blood Stagnation produces bleeding that comes and goes, often with dark clots and a noticeable distending or fixed pain. The tongue may look dark purple or have purplish spots, and the pulse feels wiry and choppy. This pattern arises when the local flow of Qi and blood is blocked after surgery, creating stasis that forces blood out of the vessels.
Spleen Qi Sinking shows up as pale or intermittent oozing, accompanied by deep tiredness, a pale face, and loose stools. The tongue is pale with a thin white coat, and the pulse is weak and soft. Here the body’s holding power is too weak to keep blood in the vessels, so even a small wound bleeds sluggishly.
Toxic-Heat is a less common but serious pattern marked by high fever, intense redness, swelling, and throbbing pain around the surgical site. The tongue is deep red with a yellow or dry coat, and the pulse is rapid and forceful. This indicates a severe infection that can provoke secondary bleeding and needs urgent care.

### Heat in the Blood
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-in-the-blood)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Profuse bright red bleeding, Anal burning and swelling, Thirst with desire for cold drinks, Restlessness and irritability, Feeling of internal heat, worse at night
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy or greasy foods, Alcohol, Hot weather or hot baths, Emotional stress, Straining during bowel movements
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool environment, Cold drinks, Cooling foods (watermelon, cucumber), Rest and lying down
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  After hemorrhoid surgery, the local tissues are already injured and vulnerable. If there is pre-existing Heat in the Blood or if post-operative infection develops, that Heat intensifies and enters the blood. Heat makes the blood move recklessly, forcing it out of the vessels at the surgical site, resulting in fresh, bright red bleeding that can be profuse.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, possibly crimson, with thin yellow dry coating. Pulse: rapid (数 shuò), wiry (弦 xián), and slippery (滑 huá).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat in the Blood is directly aggravated by anything that adds heat to the body-spicy food, alcohol, and hot environments all fuel the fire. Emotional stress, especially anger, stirs up Liver fire which can worsen the reckless movement of blood. Cooling foods, cold drinks, and a calm environment help to clear the Heat and settle the blood, allowing the vessels to close and bleeding to stop.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid all spicy, greasy, and warming foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which can add heat. Eat cooling, blood-nourishing foods like cucumber, watermelon, mung beans, and celery. Keep the anal area clean and dry, and avoid straining during bowel movements. Gentle rest helps the body cool down and heal.

### Damp-Heat in the Large Intestine
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-large-intestine)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Bright red bleeding with anal burning, Heaviness in the body or limbs, Yellow greasy tongue coating, Feeling of incomplete evacuation, Scanty dark urine
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy or greasy foods, Alcohol, Hot weather or hot baths, Prolonged sitting
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool sitz baths, Light, bland diet, Gentle walking, Cool environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  After hemorrhoid surgery, the local trauma and stagnation can easily generate or attract Damp-Heat. This pathogenic mix pours down into the lower burner, congesting and inflaming the delicate blood vessels around the anus. The Heat scorches the collaterals and forces blood out, resulting in bright red bleeding, while the Dampness creates a heavy, burning sensation and a feeling of incomplete evacuation.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with a thick, yellow, greasy coating, thickest at the center and root. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and rapid (数 shuò) or wiry (弦 xián) and rapid.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat thrives on rich, spicy, and greasy foods and is exacerbated by humid, hot environments, which add more pathogenic accumulation and drive Heat downward. Cooling, light foods and a cool environment help drain Dampness and clear Heat, while gentle movement keeps Qi and fluids moving so they do not stagnate and generate more Dampness. Cool sitz baths directly soothe the local Heat and reduce swelling.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and rich foods, as well as alcohol, which all generate Damp-Heat. Eat light, cooling foods like cucumber, watermelon, and mung beans. Drink plenty of water and keep the anal area clean and dry. Gentle walking helps move Qi and drain Dampness, but avoid prolonged sitting.

### Qi And Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing or distending pain that worsens with pressure, Intermittent bleeding with dark clots, Dark purple tongue with stasis spots, Wiry and choppy pulse
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Emotional stress, Cold exposure, Spicy or greasy foods, Prolonged sitting
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle walking, Warm sitz baths, Avoiding prolonged sitting, Light stretching
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  After surgery, the local flow of Qi and Blood is easily disrupted. When Qi stagnates, Blood cannot move smoothly, leading to stasis that obstructs the vessels. This stasis can cause the vessels to rupture, producing intermittent bleeding that is dark with clots, often accompanied by a fixed, distending pain in the anal area.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple body with stasis spots and distended sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: wiry (弦 xián) and choppy (涩 sè).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Stress and emotional tension cause Qi to stagnate, which worsens blood stasis and bleeding. Cold constricts the vessels and slows blood flow, making stasis harder to resolve. Movement and warmth, on the other hand, encourage Qi and Blood to circulate, helping to dissolve stasis and stop bleeding.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods and avoid cold or raw items that can congeal blood. Gentle movement like walking helps keep Qi flowing, but avoid sitting for long periods. Sitz baths with warm water can soothe the area and encourage circulation.

### Spleen Qi Sinking
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-qi-sinking)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Pale or intermittent bleeding, Chronic fatigue and lethargy, Bearing-down or dragging sensation in the abdomen, Pale complexion, Loose stools or diarrhea
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Standing for long periods, Overwork or exhaustion, Raw, cold foods and drinks, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Lying down or sitting with feet up, Warm, cooked meals, Gentle walking, Abdominal support or gentle compression
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is responsible for holding blood inside the vessels. When Spleen Qi is weak and sinking, it loses its grip - blood can no longer be contained and seeps out, especially after the added trauma of surgery. The bleeding tends to be pale and oozing rather than bright red and forceful, because the Qi is too exhausted to push hard.
This pattern often appears in people who were already depleted before surgery, with a history of fatigue, poor appetite, and loose stools.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, tender body with teeth marks; thin white coating. Pulse: weak, especially at the right Guan position, may be deep and forceless.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Resting with the feet up helps the sinking Qi rise back toward the center. Warm, cooked foods support Spleen Yang and prevent further Qi loss, while raw and cold items damage the Spleen's digestive fire. Overwork and prolonged standing drain Qi and pull it downward, making bleeding worse. Stress knots the Qi and weakens the Spleen's ability to hold blood, so calm and gentle movement restore balance.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked, easy-to-digest meals like rice porridge, stewed root vegetables, and ginger tea. Avoid raw, cold, or greasy foods that tax the Spleen. Rest with your feet elevated when possible, and avoid standing or sitting for long stretches. Gentle, consistent movement like short walks supports Qi circulation without overexerting you.

### Toxic-Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/toxic-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: High fever and chills, Severe redness, swelling, and throbbing pain at the surgical site, Pus or foul-smelling discharge, Bleeding that may be dark or mixed with pus, Thirst with desire for cold drinks, Restlessness and irritability
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy or greasy foods, Alcohol, Exertion or straining, Hot weather or hot baths, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool compresses on the area, Rest and lying down, Drinking cooling herbal teas (chrysanthemum, dandelion), Cool environment, Gentle cleansing with mild saline
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  After hemorrhoid surgery, the surgical wound is an open door. When intense Toxic-Heat invades or flares internally, it creates a raging fire in the local tissues. This fire scorches the blood vessels, causing them to rupture and bleed, while also generating pus, severe swelling, and tissue death. The result is a dangerous combination of secondary bleeding, high fever, and extreme pain.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with prickly thorns and a dry yellow coating. Pulse: rapid (数 shuò) and forceful, often full (洪 hóng) or overflowing.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Toxic-Heat is a raging fire that consumes body fluids and damages tissues. Cooling foods, rest, and cool compresses help counteract the heat and prevent further damage. Spicy foods, alcohol, and heat exposure add fuel to the fire, while exertion and emotional stress stir up internal heat, worsening the infection and bleeding.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat cooling foods like mung bean soup, bitter melon, and cucumber. Avoid all spicy, fried, or greasy foods that add heat. Drink plenty of water to help flush toxins. Rest completely and keep the surgical area clean and dry to prevent further infection.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can Chinese herbs stop bleeding after hemorrhoid surgery?
Yes, TCM herbal formulas are specifically designed to stop bleeding according to the underlying pattern. For Heat in the Blood, cooling herbs like Di Yu and Huai Hua can arrest bright red bleeding quickly. For Spleen Qi Sinking, tonifying formulas like Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang help the body hold blood in the vessels. Many patients see a reduction in bleeding within a few days. However, heavy or persistent bleeding must be evaluated by a doctor first.

### Is it safe to take Chinese herbs while using stool softeners or pain medication?
Generally yes, but it's important to tell your TCM practitioner about all medications, including stool softeners, pain relievers, and antibiotics. Some herbs may interact with blood thinners or affect bowel movements. A qualified practitioner will select a formula that complements your current care without causing conflict.

### How long will it take for the bleeding to stop with TCM?
For acute Heat or Damp-Heat patterns, bleeding often lessens within 3-5 days of starting herbs. Qi and Blood Stagnation may take 1-2 weeks to resolve. Spleen Qi Sinking requires longer rebuilding, typically 4-6 weeks, to restore the Spleen's holding function and energy. Acupuncture can support healing throughout this period.

### Can acupuncture help with post-surgery bleeding?
Yes, but it's important to work with a practitioner who understands post-surgical care. Points like Changqiang (DU-1), Chengshan (BL-57), and Zusanli (ST-36) can help stop bleeding, reduce pain, and lift Qi. Acupuncture is often used alongside herbs for a quicker recovery.

### What foods should I avoid to prevent further bleeding?
Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, which generate Damp-Heat and can worsen bleeding. Also avoid alcohol and coffee. Eat soft, easily digestible foods to prevent constipation and straining. Cooling foods like cucumber, watermelon, and mung beans are helpful if you have Heat signs; for Spleen deficiency, warm, cooked congees and stews are best. Drink plenty of water.

### Should I stop my pain medications if I start herbs?
Never stop prescribed pain medications or antibiotics without your doctor's approval. TCM herbs can often be taken alongside them, but your practitioner needs to know the full list to avoid interactions. If pain is well-controlled, your doctor may adjust dosages over time, but this must be a joint decision.

### When is bleeding after hemorrhoid surgery an emergency?
Seek emergency care if you have heavy, pulsating bleeding that soaks a pad quickly, high fever with chills, fainting, or bleeding that won't stop with pressure. These are red flags that require immediate medical attention, not just TCM management. See the Safety section on this page for a full list.

## การวิจัยทางวิทยาศาสตร์สมัยใหม่ (Modern Scientific Research)
1. **中医药治疗痔病术后并发症的研究现状 (Current Research Status of TCM Treatment of Postoperative Complications of Hemorrhoids)**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhang L, et al. Hans Journal of Traditional Chinese Medicine, 2023.*
   A systematic review summarizing TCM interventions for hemorrhoid surgery complications. Oral herbal formulas, topical treatments, and acupuncture significantly reduced bleeding, pain, and swelling compared to conventional care alone, with a favorable safety profile.

2. **Clinical observation of Liangxue Dihuang Decoction in preventing postoperative bleeding of mixed hemorrhoids**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Li X, et al. Chinese Journal of Coloproctology, 2018.*
   In this randomized controlled trial, patients receiving Liangxue Dihuang Decoction after hemorrhoid surgery had a significantly lower incidence of postoperative bleeding and faster wound healing compared to routine care.

3. **Acupuncture for pain and bleeding after hemorrhoidectomy: a randomized controlled trial**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Wang Y, et al. Acupuncture in Medicine, 2016.*
   Acupuncture at Changqiang (DU1), Chengshan (BL57), and Zusanli (ST36) significantly reduced pain intensity and the number of bleeding episodes during the first postoperative week, suggesting acupuncture as a safe adjunctive therapy.
