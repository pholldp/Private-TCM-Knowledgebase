## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Sensorineural hearing loss (SNHL) is the most common type of permanent hearing loss, caused by damage to the delicate hair cells of the inner ear or to the auditory nerve itself. It can be triggered by aging, loud noise exposure, certain medications, infections, or head trauma, and it often comes with tinnitus - a ringing or buzzing in the ear. Diagnosis is made through audiometry, which measures your ability to hear different pitches and volumes. Unlike conductive hearing loss, which involves the outer or middle ear, SNHL is generally not reversible with surgery or medication, though hearing aids and cochlear implants can help manage it.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
For permanent sensorineural hearing loss, hearing aids are the mainstay, amplifying sounds to compensate for damaged hair cells. In severe-to-profound cases, cochlear implants can bypass the damaged structures and directly stimulate the auditory nerve. Sudden sensorineural hearing loss is treated as an emergency with high-dose oral or injected corticosteroids to reduce inflammation and swelling, ideally within the first 72 hours. Beyond that, management focuses on auditory rehabilitation, assistive listening devices, and coping strategies for tinnitus.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM understands hearing loss not as a single ear problem, but as a mirror reflecting the health of your deepest organ systems. The ears are the external opening of the Kidneys, which store our essential life force, called Essence. When Kidney Essence is full, the ears are well-nourished and hearing is sharp. When it declines - through aging, overwork, or chronic illness - the ears are the first to signal the emptiness with gradual, high-pitched hearing loss and tinnitus.
But the Kidneys are only part of the story. The Spleen and Stomach produce the clear, light Qi that rises to the head to nourish the senses. If digestion is weak, that clear Qi never reaches the ears, leaving them undernourished and vulnerable. This is why hearing loss often accompanies fatigue, poor appetite, and a heavy sensation in the ears.
Meanwhile, emotional stress and anger can stir up Liver Fire, which blazes upward along the Gallbladder channel that circles the ear. A sudden, roaring hearing loss after a fight or prolonged frustration points directly to this fiery excess.
Physical blockages matter too. A diet heavy in greasy, sweet, or dairy-rich foods generates Phlegm-Heat, a sticky, turbid substance that can literally clog the delicate ear passages, causing muffled hearing and a feeling of fullness.
And if blood circulation stagnates - after a head injury or from long-standing Qi stagnation - the ear's tiny vessels become choked, leading to persistent, stubborn loss with a fixed, stabbing pain. This is why the same Western diagnosis of sensorineural hearing loss can unfold from five entirely different TCM root causes, each demanding its own treatment strategy.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the onset and sound of the hearing loss. A slow, gradual decline over months or years, often with low-pitched tinnitus, points toward a deficiency pattern such as Kidney Essence Deficiency or Spleen and Kidney Qi Deficiency. A sudden or severe loss, especially after emotional stress, suggests an excess pattern like Liver Fire Blazing.
If the hearing loss came on gradually and is accompanied by weakness in the lower back and knees, dizziness, and a tendency to feel cold or have poor memory, Kidney Essence Deficiency is likely. The tongue is often pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse feels deep and weak. This pattern is especially common in older adults.
When chronic hearing loss appears alongside fatigue, poor appetite, loose stools, and a sallow complexion, the practitioner suspects Spleen and Kidney Qi Deficiency. The Spleen fails to lift clear Qi to the ears. The tongue may be pale and swollen with teeth marks, and the pulse is weak, reflecting the body's lack of vital energy to nourish the senses.
A sudden, roaring tinnitus and hearing loss that flares with anger or stress signals Liver Fire Blazing. The person often feels irritable, has a bitter taste in the mouth, and may have a red face or eyes. The tongue is red with a yellow coating, and the pulse is wiry and rapid-classic signs of heat and stagnation rising along the Liver and Gallbladder channels to disturb the ear.
If the ear feels blocked or full, with a muffled, heavy sensation and dizziness, Phlegm-Heat is obstructing the ear's clear orifices. The tongue coating is thick, greasy, and yellow, and the pulse feels slippery. This pattern often coexists with digestive sluggishness, as Phlegm arises when the Spleen fails to transform fluids.
Persistent hearing loss with a fixed, stabbing pain in or around the ear suggests Blood Stagnation. This may follow trauma or long-standing poor circulation. The tongue appears dark or purplish, sometimes with stasis spots, and the pulse is choppy. The pain is a key clue that distinguishes this from purely deficient patterns.

### Kidney Essence Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-essence-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Gradual hearing loss, often bilateral, High-pitched tinnitus like ringing cicadas, Weakness and soreness of the lower back and knees, Poor memory and difficulty concentrating, Premature greying or hair loss, Frequent urination at night
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Excessive sexual activity, Cold and raw foods, Chronic stress, Aging (natural decline)
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and adequate sleep, Warmth on the lower back, Eating black sesame and walnuts, Gentle exercise
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Kidneys store Essence, which produces marrow and fills the brain, and the ears are their external opening. When Kidney Essence is depleted, the ears lose their nourishment, leading to gradual hearing loss and a constant, high-pitched tinnitus. This pattern is often seen with aging, congenital weakness, or after long-term overwork.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, thin body with little or no coating, possibly with fine cracks. Pulse: fine (thin), deep, and weak, especially at the rear (Kidney) positions.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Essence is the body's deepest reserve; it is depleted by overwork, excessive sexual activity, and aging. Rest, warmth, and nourishing foods help conserve and replenish Essence, while stress and cold further drain it.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat kidney-nourishing foods like black sesame, walnuts, goji berries, and bone broth. Avoid excessive sexual activity and overwork, which deplete essence. Gentle exercises like tai chi and qi gong support Kidney energy.

### Spleen and Kidney Qi Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-and-kidney-qi-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Gradual, progressive hearing loss, Hearing worse after exertion or fatigue, Poor appetite and loose stools, Soreness and weakness of the lower back and knees, Cold hands and feet, Persistent tiredness and low stamina, Pale or sallow complexion
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Raw, cold, or greasy foods, Late nights and irregular sleep, Excessive talking or straining
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and adequate sleep, Warm, easily digested meals, Gentle exercise
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen transforms food into Qi and Blood, then sends clear Yang upward to nourish the head and ears. The Kidneys store the essence that supports hearing. When Spleen and Kidney Qi are both weak, the clear Yang cannot ascend and the ear loses its nourishment. This leads to gradual, chronic hearing loss that becomes noticeably worse when you are tired or overworked.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks; white, moist or slippery coating. Pulse: deep, weak, and fine, especially weak at the right middle (Guan) and both rear (Chi) positions.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Overwork and late nights drain Qi and prevent its upward movement, so hearing fades. Raw and cold foods damage Spleen Yang, further weakening the production of clear Yang. Rest, warm meals, and gentle movement conserve and gently build Qi, allowing the ears to receive the nourishment they need.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked, and easily digestible foods like congee, soups, and stews. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that weaken the Spleen. Gentle daily movement like walking helps Qi flow, but avoid overexertion. Prioritize rest and a regular sleep schedule.

### Liver Fire Blazing
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-fire-blazing)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Sudden hearing loss or loud roaring tinnitus, Bitter taste in the mouth, Intense irritability and quick temper, Red face and bloodshot eyes, Worse with anger or emotional stress
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Anger and frustration, Spicy and fried foods, Alcohol and coffee
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, quiet environment, Bitter and cooling foods, Reducing anger and stress
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Emotional stress, anger, and frustration can cause Liver Qi to stagnate. Over time, that trapped Qi generates Fire, which surges upward along the Liver and Gallbladder channels into the head. The ears are a key opening of the Gallbladder channel, so this fiery rush attacks the ear directly, causing sudden or loud hearing loss and tinnitus that sounds like rushing water.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with redder sides, dry yellow coating, possible red prickles. Pulse: wiry (弦 xián) and rapid (数 shuò), full and forceful especially at the left Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Emotional stress is the root cause that generates Liver Fire, so calming the mind directly reduces the fire’s source. Spicy, greasy, and heated foods add fuel to the fire, while cooling, bitter foods help drain it. A quiet, cool environment settles the rising Yang, preventing it from surging to the ears.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which add heat. Eat more cooling, bitter foods like chrysanthemum tea, mung beans, and celery. Practice stress management such as gentle walking, breathing exercises, or meditation to keep emotional tension from turning into fire.

### Phlegm-Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Sensation of ear fullness or blockage, Muffled or reduced hearing, Thick, yellow, greasy tongue coating, Chest oppression and coughing up sticky yellow phlegm, Restlessness and a feeling of heat
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Greasy, fried foods, Dairy and sweets, Alcohol and spicy meals, Humid weather, Chronic stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Light, cooling diet, Gentle exercise, Dry, cool environment, Hydration with warm fluids
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Phlegm and Heat combine, they form a thick, turbid obstruction that can rise upward and block the ear's delicate orifices. This physical blockage prevents clear hearing, creating a sensation of fullness and muffled sounds. The Phlegm often comes from a diet rich in greasy or sweet foods, while the Heat can be stirred up by stress, alcohol, or spicy meals.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, swollen, with a thick yellow greasy coating. Pulse: slippery (滑, huá) and rapid (数, shuò).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Phlegm-Heat is formed when heavy, damp-producing foods combine with fiery influences like stress or alcohol. Greasy and sweet foods directly generate Phlegm, while spicy and hot items add Heat, causing the turbid mixture to rise and block the ears. A clean, cooling diet and movement help the body clear the obstruction naturally, while a dry environment prevents external dampness from adding to the internal load.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid dairy, greasy, fried, and sugary foods that create Phlegm. Cut back on spicy dishes, alcohol, and coffee to reduce Heat. Instead, enjoy light, cooling meals like steamed vegetables, pearl barley, and small amounts of mint tea. Gentle daily exercise helps keep Qi moving so Phlegm does not stagnate.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed stabbing pain in or around the ear, Pain worse at night or with pressure, Dark purplish tongue with stasis spots, Hearing loss that is persistent and not fluctuating, Dark lips or dusky complexion
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or drafts, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Stress and frustration, Greasy, heavy, or cold foods, Head injury or trauma
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth and warm compresses, Gentle exercise, Hawthorn berry tea, Turmeric and ginger in cooking, Massage around the ear
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Blood stagnation in the tiny channels and collaterals of the ear blocks the smooth flow of Qi and blood that nourishes the hearing apparatus. This can happen after a head injury, from chronic poor circulation, or when long-standing Qi stagnation congeals into stasis. The result is a persistent, often stubborn hearing loss accompanied by a fixed, stabbing pain that feels worse with pressure or at night.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple or dusky body with stasis spots, distended dark sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩, Sè) like a knife scraping bamboo, or wiry (弦, Xián) if Qi stagnation is involved.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold causes blood vessels to constrict and blood to congeal, worsening stasis, so warmth and gentle movement are essential to keep blood flowing. Exercise and massage physically move Qi and blood through the ear's tiny channels. Blood-moving foods like hawthorn and turmeric help break up stasis, while stress tightens the vessels and worsens stagnation.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep warm, especially the head and neck, to prevent blood from congealing. Gentle daily exercise like walking or tai chi helps keep blood moving. Include blood-moving foods in your diet: hawthorn berry tea, turmeric, ginger, and small amounts of black pepper. Avoid cold drinks and raw foods that can chill the blood. A warm compress over the ear may temporarily ease the stabbing pain.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture really improve sensorineural hearing loss?
Yes - many patients notice improved clarity, reduced tinnitus, and less ear fullness after a course of acupuncture, especially when treatment begins early. Acupuncture works by increasing blood flow to the inner ear, reducing inflammation, and stimulating the auditory nerve. While it may not restore hearing to normal in long-standing or severe cases, it often stabilizes further loss and enhances the benefit of hearing aids.

### How soon can I expect results?
Acute, sudden hearing loss from Liver Fire or Phlegm-Heat often responds within 2-6 weeks of weekly acupuncture and daily herbs. Chronic, gradual loss from Kidney or Spleen deficiency is a slower process - expect 3-6 months of consistent treatment to see meaningful change, with subtle improvements in energy and sleep appearing first. Your practitioner will monitor progress and adjust the strategy as needed.

### Is TCM safe to use with my hearing aids or cochlear implant?
Absolutely. TCM does not interfere with hearing devices and can be used alongside them. Some patients find that herbs and acupuncture improve their ability to distinguish speech, making hearing aids more effective. Just let your acupuncturist know if you have a cochlear implant so they can avoid placing needles directly over the internal receiver.

### What if my hearing loss is sudden - should I start TCM right away?
Sudden sensorineural hearing loss is a medical emergency. Go to an ENT or emergency room immediately for evaluation and possible steroid treatment. You can begin TCM concurrently or shortly after, as acupuncture and herbs may support nerve recovery and reduce inflammation. Do not delay conventional care - time is critical.

### Does TCM work for age-related hearing loss?
Age-related hearing loss typically reflects Kidney Essence Deficiency, and TCM excels at nourishing the Kidney and slowing further decline. While it is challenging to fully reverse decades of gradual loss, many older patients report clearer hearing, reduced background noise distortion, and less ringing in the ears. The goal is preservation and quality of life improvement, not a cure.

### Will I need to take herbs forever?
Not usually. Once hearing stabilizes and the underlying pattern is corrected, your practitioner will gradually reduce the dose or switch to a maintenance formula taken only a few days a week. Some people with deep Kidney deficiency may benefit from long-term, low-dose nourishment, much like taking a daily supplement. The plan is always tailored to your progress.

### Can diet really affect my hearing?
Yes - in TCM, what you eat directly influences the Phlegm and Dampness that can clog the ears, or the Blood and Essence that nourish them. A diet high in greasy, fried, or dairy-heavy foods encourages Phlegm-Heat, while too many cold, raw foods weaken the Spleen's ability to lift clear Qi to the ears. Simple shifts - like adding black sesame, walnuts, and warm cooked meals - can support your treatment.

## การวิจัยทางวิทยาศาสตร์สมัยใหม่ (Modern Scientific Research)
1. **Efficacy of acupuncture for sudden sensorineural hearing loss: a systematic review and meta-analysis**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Lee S, Lee MS, Choi JY, et al. Efficacy of acupuncture for sudden sensorineural hearing loss: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Medicine (Baltimore). 2015;94(26):e1058.*
   This meta-analysis of 12 randomized trials involving 862 patients found that acupuncture combined with conventional treatment significantly improved pure-tone average hearing thresholds compared to conventional treatment alone. The effect was most pronounced when acupuncture was started within two weeks of onset.

2. **Effect of Er Long Zuo Ci Wan on age-related hearing loss: a randomized controlled trial**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Wang Y, Li J, Zhang H, et al. Effect of Er Long Zuo Ci Wan on age-related hearing loss: a randomized controlled trial. Chin J Integr Med. 2017;23(8):592-598.*
   In this trial of 120 elderly patients with presbycusis, the group receiving Er Long Zuo Ci Wan for 12 weeks showed a significant improvement in speech reception threshold and a reduction in tinnitus severity compared to placebo. The formula was well tolerated with no serious adverse events.

3. **Combined acupuncture and Chinese herbal medicine for sensorineural hearing loss: a clinical observation of 80 cases**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Chen X, Liu W, Zhao S. Combined acupuncture and Chinese herbal medicine for sensorineural hearing loss: a clinical observation of 80 cases. J Tradit Chin Med. 2018;38(3):456-461.*
   This prospective observational study reported that an integrated protocol of acupuncture at ear points and a modified kidney-nourishing herbal formula improved hearing levels by at least 10 dB in 65% of patients with chronic sensorineural hearing loss over a 3-month period. Improvements were maintained at 6-month follow-up.
