## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
A hydrocele is a painless buildup of fluid around one or both testicles, making the scrotum swell. It is common in newborns, where it often closes on its own, and can also appear in older boys and men after injury, infection, or for no clear reason. Doctors usually diagnose it by shining a light through the scrotum - the fluid lights up, confirming it is not a solid mass.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
In babies, most hydroceles disappear without treatment by age one. In adults, if the swelling is large or bothersome, the fluid may be drained with a needle (aspiration) or the sac removed surgically (hydrocelectomy). Aspiration is quick but the fluid often returns; surgery is more permanent but carries the usual risks of infection, bleeding, and anesthesia.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, your body's fluids are managed by a team of organs. The Spleen transforms and transports water, the Kidney warms and vaporizes it, and the Bladder excretes it. When any part of this system falters, water can pool where it shouldn't - and because water is heavy, it sinks to the lowest point: the scrotum. A hydrocele is therefore seen not as a local problem but as a symptom of a systemic fluid-handling weakness.
The most common root is Spleen Deficiency. A weak Spleen cannot manage fluids properly, so dampness builds up gradually, causing a soft, painless swelling that often worsens after eating or when tired. This pattern comes with digestive clues like bloating, loose stools, and a heavy feeling in the limbs.
If the Kidney's warming power is low, the fluid becomes cold and heavy. This is Kidney Yang Deficiency. The scrotum feels cool to the touch, and the person may have lower back ache, frequent urination, and feel chilly overall. Alternatively, cold and dampness can invade from the outside, settling in the lower burner and causing Cold-Dampness in the Lower Burner, where the swelling feels cold and heavy but is often accompanied by a thick white greasy tongue coating.
Dampness can also mix with heat, creating a red, hot, painful swelling - Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner - which is more acute and may follow an infection. Less commonly, a past injury leads to Blood Stagnation, where trapped blood blocks fluid flow, causing a fixed, stabbing pain.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner first asks about the swelling’s onset, temperature, and pain. A chronic, soft, non‑tender swelling that develops gradually alongside fatigue and poor appetite points toward Spleen Deficiency with Dampness. If the person also feels chronically cold, with frequent urination and a pale puffy tongue, the root is more likely Kidney Yang Deficiency. The tongue and pulse confirm the deeper organ system involved.
When the scrotum feels cold and heavy, enlarges slowly, and the tongue has a white greasy coating with a deep tight pulse, Cold‑Dampness in the Lower Burner is the likely pattern. The cold and dampness congeal locally, obstructing Qi, so warmth often brings relief and cold weather makes it worse.
A red, hot, painful swelling that appears more acutely, sometimes with a yellow greasy tongue coating and a slippery rapid pulse, signals Damp‑Heat in the Lower Burner. Here the body is fighting a damp‑heat invasion, so the practitioner also asks about urinary symptoms like burning or dark urine to confirm the heat component.
If there is a history of trauma or surgery, and the pain is fixed and stabbing rather than diffuse, Blood Stagnation is suspected. The tongue may appear purplish with dark spots, and the pulse feels choppy. This pattern often complicates other patterns, so the practitioner checks for any underlying deficiency or dampness that may be trapping the blood.

### Spleen Deficiency with Dampness
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-deficiency-with-dampness)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Soft, painless scrotal swelling, Chronic, gradual onset, Abdominal bloating and loose stools, Heavy limbs and fatigue, Poor appetite
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Raw, cold, and greasy foods, Damp or humid weather, Overwork and fatigue, Worry and overthinking, Prolonged sitting
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, cooked meals, Rest, Gentle walking or stretching, Warm, dry environment, Small, frequent meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is the body's main engine for transforming and transporting fluids. When it is weakened by poor diet, overwork, or worry, it fails to manage this process, allowing dampness to accumulate internally. Because dampness is heavy and tends to sink, it drifts downward and pools in the lower burner, creating the soft, painless swelling of a hydrocele.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks, white greasy coating, moist surface. Pulse: soggy (Ru) and slow (Huan), soft and lacking force.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: The Spleen dislikes dampness and cold. Raw, cold, and greasy foods further weaken its ability to transform fluids, causing dampness to build up and sink to the scrotum. Warm, cooked foods and gentle exercise support Spleen Qi, helping it to move and drain excess fluid. Rest and a calm mind prevent Qi stagnation and overconsumption of Spleen energy, while a warm, dry environment counteracts the dampness that makes the condition worse.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked, easily digestible foods like congee, soups, and steamed vegetables. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that burden the Spleen. Incorporate damp-draining grains like barley and adzuki beans. Gentle daily movement, such as walking or tai chi, helps Qi circulate and supports fluid metabolism.

### Cold-Dampness in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/cold-dampness-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Scrotal swelling feels cold and heavy, Worse in cold, damp weather, No redness, heat, or sharp pain, Cold hands and feet, Loose or watery stools
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold and damp weather, Cold foods and drinks, Prolonged sitting, Overwork and fatigue
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Applying warmth to the lower abdomen, Warm, cooked meals, Gentle walking or stretching, Moxibustion on the lower back, Warm, dry environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When cold and dampness invade the lower burner, they congeal and obstruct the normal movement of Qi and fluids. The warmth of Kidney Yang cannot properly transform water, so fluid accumulates locally, filling the scrotum with a cold, heavy swelling. Because the root is cold rather than hot, the area lacks redness, heat, or acute pain, and the swelling develops gradually, often worsening in cold or damp weather.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks, thick white greasy coating, excessively moist. Pulse: deep, slow, and soggy, especially weak in the rear (Chi) positions.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold and dampness congeal Qi and fluids in the lower burner, leading to stagnation and swelling. Warmth, dryness, and movement help disperse cold and transform dampness, restoring normal fluid metabolism. Cold and damp environments, as well as sedentary habits, encourage further accumulation, worsening the hydrocele.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the scrotal area warm and dry. Avoid cold, raw foods and icy drinks; favor cooked, warming meals with spices like ginger and cinnamon. Wear supportive underwear and avoid prolonged sitting in cold, damp places. Gentle daily movement like walking helps keep Qi and fluids flowing.

### Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Red, hot, painful scrotal swelling, Burning sensation during urination, Feeling of heaviness in the lower body, Yellow greasy tongue coating thickest at the root, Slippery, rapid pulse, Acute or subacute onset
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Hot, humid weather, Alcohol and greasy meals, Spicy, fried foods, Prolonged sitting
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool compresses, Avoiding alcohol and greasy foods, Drinking barley water, Resting with legs elevated
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Dampness and heat combine and sink downward into the lower burner, where they obstruct the flow of Qi and fluids in the scrotum. The heat causes redness, pain, and a feeling of warmth, while the dampness makes the swelling heavy, persistent, and sometimes itchy. Together they create an acute or subacute build-up of turbid fluid that forms the hydrocele.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, slightly swollen, with a yellow greasy coating thickest at the root. Pulse: slippery (Hua) and rapid (Shu), may feel soggy (Ru).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-heat is aggravated by hot, humid environments and by foods that are difficult to digest, greasy, or heating-these directly feed the pathological dampness and heat. Cooling, drying foods and rest help clear the heat and drain the dampness, allowing the body to reabsorb the excess fluid and reduce the painful swelling.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol, which all generate damp-heat. Eat cooling, damp-draining foods like mung beans, barley, cucumber, and celery. Stay hydrated with plain water or barley tea, but avoid iced drinks that can trap dampness. Gentle daily movement like walking helps Qi flow and prevents stagnation.

### Kidney Yang Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-yang-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Soft, non-tender scrotal swelling, Feeling cold all over, worse in lower body, Frequent urination, especially at night, Soreness and cold pain in lower back and knees, Fatigue and low spirits
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather, Cold foods and drinks, Overwork and fatigue, Prolonged standing, Sexual excess
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth, Rest, Warm drinks, Gentle exercise, Moxibustion
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Kidney Yang is the body's pilot light - it warms and transforms fluids. When it runs low, water cannot be processed properly and accumulates in the lower body, especially where gravity pulls it, like the scrotum. The resulting swelling is soft, cold, and non-tender, and is accompanied by other signs of internal cold such as frequent nighttime urination and a deep slow pulse.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, tender with teeth marks, wet white coating. Pulse: deep, slow, weak, especially at the Chi (Kidney) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Yang is the body's foundational warming fire. Cold, overwork, and excessive sexual activity further deplete this fire, worsening fluid accumulation. Warmth, rest, and gentle movement conserve and gently stimulate Yang, helping the body transform and move fluids. Moxibustion directly warms the meridians and organs, supporting the Kidney's function.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the lower body warm, especially the feet and lower back. Avoid cold drinks, ice cream, and raw foods. Gentle daily movement like walking or tai chi supports Yang Qi, but avoid exhaustion. Warm baths and moxibustion on the lower abdomen can be very helpful.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing scrotal pain that worsens at night, History of trauma, surgery, or chronic lower abdominal pain, Purplish or dusky tongue with stasis spots, Dark circles under the eyes or a dusky complexion, Swelling feels distended and heavy, not just soft
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Prolonged sitting, Cold weather or cold foods, Re-injury or trauma, Emotional stress and frustration
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm compresses, Gentle walking or stretching, Resting with legs elevated, Loose, supportive underwear
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Blood Stagnation in the lower burner often follows a direct injury, surgery, or long-standing Qi stagnation. When blood congeals and fails to flow smoothly, it blocks the local channels and collaterals, preventing normal fluid exchange. This trapped blood acts like a dam, causing water to accumulate in the scrotum. The result is a hydrocele with a fixed, stabbing pain that feels worse with pressure and at night, when circulation naturally slows.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body, with stasis spots and distended, dark sublingual veins; thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (Se) or wiry (Xian), feeling rough and uneven like a knife scraping bamboo.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood stasis is like a traffic jam in the vessels. Warmth and gentle movement act like a tow truck, encouraging blood to flow again, while cold and inactivity make it congeal and worsen the blockage. Prolonged sitting physically compresses the channels of the lower burner, directly obstructing circulation. Stress tightens the Liver Qi, and since Qi leads the blood, this tension further traps blood in place.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw foods and icy drinks, which can congeal blood and worsen stasis. Gentle daily movement like walking or tai chi encourages smooth circulation. If not acutely inflamed, a warm compress over the lower abdomen can help. Try not to sit for hours at a time; standing and stretching breaks prevent compression of the lower burner.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM really shrink a hydrocele without surgery?
Yes, in many cases. By strengthening the Spleen, warming the Kidney, or clearing Damp-Heat, the body can gradually reabsorb the excess fluid. This works best for mild to moderate hydroceles. Very large or long-standing swellings may still need drainage, but TCM can help prevent recurrence afterward.

### How long does it take to see results?
Most men notice less heaviness and discomfort within the first 2-3 weeks. The swelling itself shrinks more slowly. Acute patterns may resolve in a month; chronic deficiency patterns usually require 2-3 months of regular treatment. Your practitioner will track progress by feeling the scrotum and checking your tongue and pulse.

### Is acupuncture safe for a hydrocele?
Yes. Acupuncture points are chosen on the legs, abdomen, and lower back - never directly into the swollen scrotum. The needles are hair-thin and the treatment is gentle. Moxibustion (warming the points with a dried herb) is often added for cold patterns and feels soothing.

### Can I combine TCM with conventional treatment?
Absolutely. Many men use TCM alongside monitoring by their urologist. If you have had surgery, TCM can support healing and reduce fluid re-accumulation. Always tell both your TCM practitioner and your doctor about all treatments you are receiving, especially if you are taking blood thinners or other medications.

### Do I need to change my diet?
Diet plays a big role. Cold, raw, and greasy foods weaken the Spleen and promote dampness, so they should be minimized. Warm, cooked meals, soups, and foods like barley, adzuki beans, and ginger help drain dampness and support recovery. Your practitioner will give you pattern-specific advice.

### Is TCM safe for children with a hydrocele?
Yes, with appropriate adjustments. Pediatric hydroceles are often treated with very gentle herbal formulas and pediatric tuina (massage) rather than acupuncture. Many resolve as the child's Spleen and Kidney energy matures. Always consult a TCM practitioner experienced in pediatrics.
