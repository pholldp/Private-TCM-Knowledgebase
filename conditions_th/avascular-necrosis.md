## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Avascular necrosis (osteonecrosis) occurs when the blood supply to a bone is disrupted, causing bone tissue to die. The femoral head - the ball of the hip joint - is the most common site. Over time, the weakened bone can collapse, leading to severe arthritis, pain, and loss of mobility. Diagnosis is usually made by MRI, which can detect early changes before they appear on X-rays. Risk factors include trauma, long-term corticosteroid use, heavy alcohol consumption, and certain medical conditions like sickle cell disease.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Early-stage avascular necrosis is often managed with pain relievers, activity modification, and physical therapy to preserve joint function. Surgical options include core decompression (drilling holes to relieve pressure and stimulate new blood vessel growth), bone grafting, or osteotomy. Once the femoral head has collapsed, total hip replacement is typically recommended to relieve pain and restore mobility.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the health of bone is governed by the Kidneys. The Kidneys store essence (Jing), which produces marrow and fills the bones. When Kidney essence is strong, bones are dense and well-nourished. When it is depleted - through aging, overwork, chronic illness, or the toxic heat of long-term corticosteroid use - the bone becomes brittle and its blood supply dwindles.
The Liver also plays a key role, because it stores Blood and governs the smooth flow of Qi through the sinews and channels. If Liver Blood is deficient or its Qi stagnates, the channels that feed the hip joint become obstructed.
This obstruction is the second major piece of the puzzle. Blood stasis is almost always present in avascular necrosis. It can be triggered by a traumatic injury that causes local bruising and swelling, or it can develop more slowly from Qi stagnation and Phlegm-Dampness accumulation. When Blood congeals in the tiny vessels that feed the femoral head, the bone is literally starved of nourishment. This is why the pain is often fixed, stabbing, and worse with pressure - classic hallmarks of Blood stasis in TCM.
Phlegm-Dampness is a thick, turbid pathological substance that forms when the Spleen fails to transform fluids. In the context of avascular necrosis, it often combines with stagnant Blood to create a sticky, stubborn blockage in the channels. This pattern is especially common in people who consume a lot of alcohol or rich, greasy foods, which overwhelm the Spleen and generate internal Dampness and Heat. The hip joint feels heavy, swollen, and stiff, as if wrapped in wet cloth.
Finally, when the disease has dragged on for months or years, the body's fundamental resources of Qi and Blood become deeply depleted. This is the deficiency stage, where the hip joint weakens and atrophies. The pain shifts from sharp to a dull, persistent ache, and the whole person feels exhausted and pale. TCM treatment must then shift from simply moving stasis to rebuilding the blood and essence that can truly nourish the bone.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner starts by asking what the hip pain actually feels like and what brought it on. The quality of the pain, its timing, and any whole-body signs like fatigue or night sweats are the map that points to one pattern over another. The tongue and pulse are then checked to confirm the picture.
If the pain is sharp, stabbing, and fixed in one spot-worse after sitting still but easing slightly with gentle movement-Qi and Blood Stagnation is the likely culprit. This pattern often follows an injury, heavy drinking, or long-term steroid use. The tongue looks dark or purplish, perhaps with tiny dark spots, and the pulse feels wiry and rough, like a choppy river.
When the stabbing pain becomes relentless and more severe, Blood Stagnation has deepened. This pattern often overlaps with early Kidney weakness, so you might also feel lower back soreness or weakness in the legs. The tongue stays dark, and the pulse feels thin and choppy, as if struggling to flow.
If the dominant sensation shifts from sharp pain to a dull, aching soreness in the lower back and knees, Kidney and Liver Yin Deficiency is taking center stage. The hip may feel weak rather than stabbing, and you might notice night sweats, dry eyes, or ringing in the ears. The tongue looks red with a thin or absent coating, and the pulse is thin and rapid.
In later stages, the hip may feel heavy and swollen, with limited movement-this points to Phlegm-Dampness clogging the channels. The sensation is one of weight rather than just pain, and the tongue coating is thick, white, and greasy. The pulse feels soft and slippery, like a bead rolling in oil.
When the illness has dragged on, the body’s resources become depleted, leading to Qi and Blood Deficiency. The hip feels weak and atrophied, and you are easily fatigued, with a pale face and lips. The tongue is pale and puffy, and the pulse is thin and weak, almost fading away.

### Qi And Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed stabbing pain in the hip or groin, Worse with pressure or prolonged sitting, Dark purple tongue with stasis spots, Wiry and choppy pulse, History of trauma, alcohol, or corticosteroid use
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Alcohol consumption, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Trauma or overuse, Emotional stress and frustration
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle movement, Applying warmth to the hip, Light massage around the area
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Qi moves Blood, and when that flow is disrupted-often by a fall, heavy drinking, or long-term corticosteroid use-stagnation sets in. In the hip, this trapped Qi and Blood cannot nourish the bone, so the tissue starts to starve and die. The result is a fixed, stabbing pain that gets worse with pressure because the blockage is deep and stubborn.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple body with stasis spots, distended sublingual veins. Pulse: wiry (弦 xián) and choppy (涩 sè).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Qi and Blood Stagnation means the flow is blocked; movement and warmth help to disperse stagnation, while alcohol, inactivity, and stress make it congeal further.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid alcohol and smoking, which worsen blood stasis. Gentle movement like Tai Chi or walking helps Qi flow, but avoid high-impact activities. Warm compresses can relieve pain. Eat foods that invigorate blood such as black fungus, hawthorn, and turmeric.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing hip pain that does not move, Pain worsens dramatically at night, Pain is worse with pressure on the joint, Dark or purplish complexion, especially under the eyes, Stiff, restricted range of motion in the hip
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, damp weather, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Emotional stress and frustration, Heavy, greasy, or sweet foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle movement, Applying warmth to the hip, Light massage around the area
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  In this pattern, the smooth flow of Blood through the vessels and channels around the hip joint has become obstructed. This stagnation can develop from a traumatic injury, chronic overuse, or as a deeper progression of Qi Stagnation. When Blood cannot circulate freely, the bone and surrounding tissues are starved of nourishment, leading to the death of bone cells and the hallmark fixed, severe pain.
As the stasis deepens, the pain becomes more intense and unrelenting, especially at night when circulation naturally slows. The blocked Blood also creates a palpable sense of hardness and restriction in the joint, making movement stiff and painful.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple body with stasis spots and distended, dark sublingual veins; thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè) and wiry (弦 xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood Stagnation is like a traffic jam in the vessels. Movement and warmth gently encourage flow and break up the congestion, while cold and stillness make the Blood congeal further, intensifying the blockage and pain. Emotional stress tightens the Liver Qi, which is the force that pushes Blood, so frustration directly worsens the stasis.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the hip warm and avoid exposure to cold, damp environments which can congeal Blood. Gentle, non-weight-bearing movement like swimming or stationary cycling is excellent for encouraging circulation without stressing the joint. Avoid prolonged sitting or standing in one position. Include small amounts of circulatory spices like turmeric and ginger in your cooking, and steer clear of greasy, heavy foods that can contribute to internal stagnation.

### Kidney and Liver Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-and-liver-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Soreness and weakness of the lower back and knees, Night sweats and heat in the palms, soles, and chest, Dry mouth and throat, dry eyes with blurred vision, Dizziness and ringing in the ears, Red tongue with little or no coating, dry surface
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and physical exhaustion, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Alcohol and smoking, Emotional stress and frustration, Hot weather or overheated rooms
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and lying down, Cool, quiet environment, Nourishing, moistening foods (e.g., bone broth, black beans), Gentle stretching and Tai Chi
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  In TCM, the Kidneys govern bone and marrow, while the Liver nourishes the sinews and stores Blood. When Kidney and Liver Yin become depleted-often due to long-term illness, overwork, or prolonged use of corticosteroid medications-the bones and sinews lose their nourishment. The resulting deficiency heat consumes the marrow and fluids, leading to a gradual weakening and eventual collapse of the femoral head. This is a core mid-stage pattern where the hip pain is more of a deep, dull ache accompanied by systemic signs of Yin deficiency.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin body with cracks, little or no coating, dry surface. Pulse: fine (thready), rapid, and often wiry; the left Chi (Kidney) position is weak.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney and Liver Yin are the body's cooling, nourishing reserves. Rest and moistening foods help preserve and rebuild Yin, while gentle movement encourages Qi and blood flow without depleting it. Overwork, heat, and stimulants consume Yin, generating deficiency fire that further dries the bones and sinews. Emotional stress stagnates Liver Qi, which can transform into heat and worsen the underlying deficiency.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat foods that build Yin and strengthen bone, such as black beans, walnuts, bone broth, goji berries, and sesame seeds. Avoid spicy, greasy, and overly heating foods, as well as alcohol. Rest is essential-avoid overexertion and heavy lifting. Gentle, weight-bearing exercises like Tai Chi or walking can help maintain bone density without overheating the body. Protect the hip joint and consider using a cane to reduce pressure if pain is significant.

### Phlegm in the Channels joints and muscles
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-in-the-channels-joints-and-muscles)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Heavy, numb sensation in the hip and leg, Joint swelling that feels firm or hard to the touch, Fixed stabbing pain that worsens with damp weather, Stiffness with difficulty bending and straightening, White, greasy tongue coating, Purple or dark tongue body with stasis spots
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, damp weather, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Heavy, greasy, or sweet foods, Alcohol consumption
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, dry weather, Gentle movement, Light, non-greasy meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Phlegm-Dampness is a thick, sticky pathological substance that forms when the Spleen fails to transform fluids. In avascular necrosis, it congeals with stagnant Blood and obstructs the Channels that supply the femoral head. This blockage starves the bone of nourishment, leading to the heavy, swollen, and stiff sensation characteristic of later-stage disease.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple body with stasis spots, white greasy coating, distended sublingual veins. Pulse: wiry and slippery, or wiry and choppy.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Phlegm-Dampness is heavy and sticky by nature. Cold and damp weather congeals it further, while warmth and gentle movement help to disperse the stagnation. Greasy, sweet, or alcoholic foods overwhelm the Spleen and generate more Phlegm, so a clean, light diet lightens the burden on the Channels.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid dairy, sugar, fried foods, and alcohol, which create more Dampness and Phlegm. ### Qi and Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dull, aching hip pain that is not sharp or stabbing, Persistent fatigue and lack of energy, Pale or sallow complexion and pale lips, Poor appetite and reduced food intake, Numbness or tingling in the limbs, Dizziness or lightheadedness
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overexertion and prolonged standing, Poor nutrition or skipping meals, Cold, damp weather, Excessive worry and mental strain
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and lying down, Warm, nourishing foods, Gentle movement
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When avascular necrosis drags on, the body's fundamental resources of Qi and Blood become deeply depleted. Without enough Qi to push and enough Blood to moisten, the sinews and bones lose their nourishment. The hip joint becomes weak and atrophied, producing a dull, persistent ache rather than the sharp, stabbing pain seen in early blood-stasis patterns.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body, may be thin or slightly teeth-marked, with a thin white coating. Pulse: fine, weak, and may feel empty; weakest at the right Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Qi and Blood Deficiency is an Empty (虚) pattern where the body lacks the energy and substance to nourish bones and sinews. Rest and nourishing foods help replenish these resources, while overexertion, cold, and poor diet further drain them. Gentle movement keeps Qi flowing without consuming it, and avoiding mental strain protects the Spleen, which is the source of new Blood.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, easily digestible, nourishing foods like bone broth, black beans, walnuts, and moderate amounts of lean meat. Avoid overexertion and get plenty of rest. Gentle movement like Tai Chi or short walks can help circulate Qi without depleting it.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can acupuncture and herbs really help a dying bone?
Yes, especially in early stages. TCM does not claim to regrow dead bone, but it works to restore the circulation of Qi and Blood to the area, which can halt the dying process and support the body's own repair mechanisms. By reducing Blood stasis and nourishing the Kidney essence that governs bone health, treatment aims to keep the femoral head viable and prevent collapse. Many patients report reduced pain and improved mobility within the first few months.

### How long does TCM treatment take for avascular necrosis?
Most patients commit to at least 3-6 months of treatment. Stagnation patterns (sharp, stabbing pain) often respond more quickly, with pain relief in 4-6 weeks. Deficiency patterns (dull ache, weakness) take longer - typically 6-12 months - because the goal is to rebuild deep reserves of Kidney essence and Blood. Acupuncture is usually given 1-2 times per week, and herbs are taken daily. Consistency is the most important factor for success.

### Can I avoid hip replacement surgery with TCM?
In early-stage avascular necrosis (before the femoral head has collapsed), TCM may help delay or even avoid surgery by improving blood flow and supporting bone repair. However, if significant collapse has already occurred, surgery is often the most reliable way to relieve pain and restore function. In those cases, TCM can still be an excellent support before and after surgery to speed recovery, manage pain, and strengthen the constitution.

### Do I need to stop drinking alcohol during treatment?
Yes, strongly. Alcohol is a major contributor to the Phlegm-Dampness and Heat that clog the channels and starve the bone. Continuing to drink will work directly against your herbal treatment and can accelerate the disease. Your TCM practitioner will likely advise complete abstinence, along with dietary changes to reduce greasy, heavy foods that create Dampness.

### Is it safe to combine TCM with my pain medications or blood thinners?
Generally, yes, but you must inform both your TCM practitioner and your medical doctor. Many Blood-moving herbs (such as Dang Gui, Chuan Xiong, and Hong Hua) have mild antiplatelet effects, so if you are taking anticoagulants like warfarin or aspirin, your dosages may need monitoring. Never stop prescribed medications abruptly. Always bring a full list of your medications to your TCM consultation.

### What should I eat to support my bones during TCM treatment?
Focus on warm, nourishing foods that build Kidney essence and Blood. Bone broths, black beans, walnuts, dark leafy greens, and modest amounts of high-quality animal protein are excellent. Avoid cold, raw foods and icy drinks, which can constrict the channels and worsen stagnation. Greasy, fried, and sugary foods should also be minimized because they generate the Dampness and Phlegm that obstruct the hip joint.

   *วารสารวิชาการ: Li J, Zhang H, et al. China Journal of Orthopaedics and Traumatology, 2018.*
   This RCT compared core decompression surgery alone versus surgery combined with oral Taohong Siwu Tang in patients with ARCO stage I-II avascular necrosis. At 12-month follow-up, the combined group showed a significantly lower rate of femoral head collapse and better functional recovery, suggesting that activating blood and resolving stasis may enhance surgical outcomes.
