## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Threatened miscarriage is defined as vaginal bleeding during the first 20 weeks of pregnancy, often accompanied by mild cramping or lower back pain, while the cervix remains closed. It affects roughly 20-25% of all pregnancies, and although it can be frightening, many women go on to have healthy full-term deliveries. Diagnosis typically involves an ultrasound to confirm fetal heartbeat and rule out ectopic pregnancy, along with blood tests to measure hCG levels.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Standard care usually includes rest, pelvic rest (avoiding intercourse), and sometimes progesterone supplementation if low levels are suspected. In many cases, however, no specific cause is found, and management is expectant - monitoring and waiting to see if the bleeding subsides on its own.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the stability of a pregnancy depends above all on the strength of the Kidney system and the integrity of the Chong (Penetrating) and Ren (Conception) vessels. The Kidneys are the root of reproduction - they anchor the fetus just as a strong foundation anchors a house. When Kidney Qi is weak, the Chong and Ren vessels lose their grip, and the body cannot hold the fetus securely. This is why a restless fetus often comes with a deep, dull ache in the lower back and a bearing‑down sensation in the abdomen.
But the Kidneys are only part of the story. The Spleen produces Qi and Blood, which provide the lifting force and nourishment the fetus needs. When Qi and Blood are deficient, the uterus lacks both the strength to hold the pregnancy and the material to feed it, leading to a hollow, sinking feeling and pale, scanty bleeding. On the other hand, Heat in the Blood - often from emotional stress, diet, or infection - can agitate the blood and disturb the womb, causing bright red bleeding and a restless, irritable sensation.
Less commonly, physical trauma or pre‑existing masses create Blood Stagnation, blocking the normal flow of nourishment to the fetus and causing sharp, fixed pain with dark, clotted spotting. Each of these patterns demands a different treatment approach - tonifying, clearing, or moving - and that is why TCM takes such care to read the tongue, feel the pulse, and listen to the full story before choosing herbs or points.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the vaginal bleeding itself - its color, amount, and consistency - because that is the first clue to the underlying pattern. The quality of the bleeding, combined with the sensation in the lower back and abdomen, quickly narrows down whether the problem is weakness, heat, or stagnation.
When the bleeding is scanty and dull or dark, and it comes with a deep lower back soreness, a sinking or dragging feeling in the abdomen, dizziness, weak knees, and frequent urination, the pattern is Kidney Qi not Firm. The tongue is usually pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse feels deep and fine but still has a slippery quality because of the pregnancy.
If the blood loss is pale, thin, and small in amount, and the person feels an empty, hollow sensation in the lower abdomen along with a pale complexion, palpitations, and extreme fatigue, that points to Qi and Blood Deficiency. The tongue is pale with a thin white coat, and the pulse is thin and weak - signs that the body simply does not have enough nourishment to anchor the fetus.
Bright red or dark, thick, sticky bleeding with a rapid pulse and a red tongue with a yellow coating signals Heat in the Blood. This pattern often includes irritability, a dry mouth, a bitter taste, and concentrated urine. The heat agitates the blood and disturbs the uterus, making the bleeding more active and harder to control.
Blood Stagnation in the Directing and Penetrating Vessels shows up as dark, clotted blood and a fixed, stabbing pain in the lower abdomen. There is often a history of a fall, surgery, or an old injury. The tongue looks dark or purple with stasis spots, and the pulse may feel choppy or wiry. Here the bleeding comes from blood that is not flowing smoothly.
Spleen Qi Deficiency and Blood Stagnation with Phlegm in the Uterus are less common but can also cause restlessness. Spleen deficiency leads to recurrent early pregnancy loss with pale spotting, bloating, and poor appetite. When masses or phlegm-blood concretions exist, the lower abdomen may feel persistently painful with irregular dark bleeding, and the tongue will show stasis signs.

### Kidney Qi not Firm
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-qi-not-firm)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Sore and weak lower back, Sinking or bearing-down sensation in the lower abdomen, Light spotting that is pale or pink, not heavy, Frequent urination with clear, dilute urine, Dizziness or tinnitus
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Standing for long periods, Overwork or physical strain, Cold weather or drafts, Excessive sexual activity
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Lying down and resting, Warmth on the lower back, Warm, cooked foods, Gentle pressure on the lower back
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Kidneys are the root of reproduction and anchor the two vessels that nourish the fetus - the Chong and Ren vessels. When Kidney Qi is weak, these vessels lose their grip, and the body cannot hold the fetus securely. This is why you feel a bearing-down sensation, a dull ache in the lower back, and sometimes light spotting. Think of it as the body's natural "safety net" for the pregnancy losing its tension.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, slightly puffy and moist body with a thin white coat. Pulse: deep (Chen) and weak (Ruo), especially at both Chi positions.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Qi is the body's deep reserve of energy, like a pilot light. Physical strain, standing too long, and cold all drain this reserve, weakening the body's ability to hold the fetus. Rest, warmth, and nourishing foods help restore that deep energy, allowing the Kidneys to regain their anchoring function and stabilize the pregnancy.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Prioritize deep rest and avoid standing for long periods. Eat warm, cooked foods like stews and broths, and include small amounts of black sesame, walnuts, and kidney beans. Avoid raw, cold foods and keep your lower back warm.

### Qi and Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Pale, scanty vaginal spotting, Empty, sinking sensation in the lower abdomen, Sallow or pale complexion, Fatigue and weakness, Dizziness and palpitations
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Skipping meals, Heavy lifting, Excessive worry, Raw or cold foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Complete bed rest, Warm, nourishing foods, Mild warmth on the abdomen, Gentle, slow walks
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Qi gives the body its holding and lifting power, while Blood nourishes and anchors the growing fetus. When both are deficient, the uterus lacks the strength to hold the pregnancy steady and the fetus loses its source of nourishment. This leads to a sinking, empty sensation in the lower abdomen, light spotting, and a persistent feeling of weakness.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body, may be thin, with a thin white coating. Pulse: fine (thin) and weak, lacking force.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Qi and Blood are produced from the food we eat and renewed by rest. Overwork and worry drain them, while skipping meals or eating cold, raw food weakens the Spleen's ability to create more. Rest and warm, nourishing food directly replenish the Qi and Blood needed to hold and feed the fetus. Overexertion and stress deplete these resources, making the sinking sensation and bleeding worse.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat small, warm, nutrient-rich meals like congees with red dates (Da Zao) and goji berries (Gou Qi Zi). Prioritize complete rest and avoid any heavy lifting or prolonged standing. Gentle, slow walks can support Qi flow, but stop immediately if you feel tired.

### Heat in the Blood
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-in-the-blood)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Vaginal bleeding bright red or dark red, thick, Irritability and restlessness, Feeling of body heat, worse at night, Thirst with desire for cold drinks, Bitter taste in mouth, Yellow urine
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy or greasy food, Emotional stress or anger, Hot weather, Overexertion
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool environment, Drinking cool water, Eating cucumber or pear, Rest and emotional calm
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Heat in the Blood can arise from emotional stress, excessive consumption of spicy or greasy foods, or an external heat pathogen. When heat enters the blood level, it agitates the blood, causing it to move recklessly and disturb the Chong and Ren vessels that nourish the fetus. This leads to vaginal bleeding, often bright red or dark and thick, and a restless, irritable feeling. The heat also consumes Yin fluids, resulting in thirst, dry mouth, and a sensation of internal warmth.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with red spots or prickles, thin yellow dry coating. Pulse: rapid (数 shǔ) and forceful, often wiry (弦 xián) or slippery (滑 huá).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Heat in the Blood is aggravated by anything that adds heat to the body, such as spicy foods, emotional frustration (which generates Liver fire), and hot environments. Cooling foods and rest help clear heat and calm the blood, reducing the reckless movement that disturbs the uterus and causes bleeding. Emotional calm prevents the upward surge of Liver fire that further agitates the blood.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and warming foods such as chili, lamb, ginger, and garlic. Choose cooling foods like cucumber, pear, mung beans, and leafy greens. Stay hydrated with cool water, and keep your environment cool. Emotional calm is crucial-practice gentle breathing or meditation to avoid anger and frustration.

### Blood Stagnation in the Directing and Penetrating Vessels
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation-in-the-directing-and-penetrating-vessels)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dark purplish vaginal spotting with clots, Fixed, stabbing pain in the lower abdomen, Pain that worsens at night or with pressure, History of trauma, fall, or surgery during pregnancy, Tongue with purplish spots or distended sublingual veins
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Physical trauma or falls, Heavy lifting, Cold or raw foods, Emotional upset
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle rest, Warmth on lower abdomen, Gentle walking, Warm, cooked foods
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When a fall, surgery, or pre-existing mass damages the Chong and Ren vessels, blood flow becomes obstructed and stagnant. This trapped blood blocks the normal nourishment of the fetus, making it restless and unstable. The result is a fixed, stabbing pain in the lower abdomen and dark, clotted spotting that signals the stasis.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body, possible stasis spots, engorged sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè) and wiry (弦 xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Trauma and heavy lifting directly injure the vessels, creating more stasis and aggravating the fixed pain. Cold causes blood to congeal, making the stagnation worse, so warmth and gentle movement are used to encourage blood flow and dissolve the stasis. Emotional stress tightens the Liver Qi, which can worsen blood stasis, so staying calm helps keep the vessels open.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Rest is essential-avoid heavy lifting, sudden movements, and any risk of falls. Gentle walking can help move blood, but stop if pain increases. Eat warm, cooked foods; a little ginger or turmeric in cooking may gently warm the vessels. Avoid cold or raw foods that can congeal blood and worsen stasis.

### Spleen Qi Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-qi-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Pale, scanty spotting or watery discharge, Extreme fatigue and lack of energy, Poor appetite and bloating after eating, Loose, unformed stools, Sinking or heavy sensation in the lower abdomen, Dull pale complexion
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and standing too long, Raw, cold, and greasy foods, Skipping meals or irregular eating, Excessive worry and mental strain
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Lying down and resting, Warm, cooked meals, Small, frequent portions
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is responsible for transforming food into Qi and raising that Qi to hold organs and the fetus in place. When Spleen Qi is weak, there is not enough Qi to lift and secure the growing baby, which can lead to a heavy, sinking sensation in the lower abdomen, mild spotting, and a risk of miscarriage. This weakness often shows up as extreme fatigue, poor appetite, and digestive bloating because the body cannot create the energy it needs.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale and puffy with tooth marks, thin white coating. Pulse: overall deficient (weak and soft), with the right middle position (Spleen/Stomach) especially weak and lacking definition.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: The Spleen's job is to transform food into usable Qi and to lift that Qi upward. Raw and cold foods, overwork, and mental strain all directly weaken the Spleen's digestive fire and its lifting power, causing the sinking sensation and spotting. Rest, warmth, and easily digested meals conserve what little Spleen Qi there is and gently rebuild it, allowing the body to hold the fetus more securely.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat small, frequent meals of warm, easily digested foods like congee, well-cooked vegetables, and soups. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that tax the Spleen. Prioritize rest and avoid standing for long periods; gentle walks can help Qi flow without exhausting it.

*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation-and-phlegm-in-the-uterus)*

- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Emotional stress and frustration, Cold and raw foods, Greasy or sweet foods, Heavy lifting or straining, Standing for long periods, Pressure on lower abdomen
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and lying down, Light, warm meals, Elevating legs, Avoiding heavy lifting
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Is it safe to use Chinese herbs during pregnancy?
Yes, when prescribed by a qualified TCM practitioner who specializes in pregnancy care. The herbs used for a restless fetus - such as Tu Si Zi, Sang Ji Sheng, and E Jiao - have a long history of safe use for stabilizing pregnancy and are specifically chosen to avoid any risk to the baby. Never self‑prescribe herbs during pregnancy; always work with a professional who can tailor the formula to your exact pattern.

### How quickly can acupuncture stop the bleeding?
Many women see a reduction in spotting within a few days of treatment, especially when acupuncture is combined with an herbal formula. The speed of response depends on the underlying pattern - acute Heat or mild Kidney weakness often responds quickly, while deep‑seated deficiency or stasis may take a little longer. Your practitioner will monitor you closely and adjust the treatment as needed.

### Can I combine TCM with progesterone supplements?
Absolutely. TCM and conventional hormonal support can work well together. There are no known dangerous interactions between progesterone and the herbs commonly used for a restless fetus. However, you should always inform both your obstetrician and your TCM practitioner about all treatments you are receiving, so they can coordinate your care effectively.

### What should I eat to help stabilize the pregnancy?
In general, favor warm, cooked, easily digestible foods - think congee, bone broths, stewed fruits, and well‑cooked vegetables. Avoid cold, raw foods (like salads and ice‑cold drinks), spicy or greasy dishes, and stimulants, as these can weaken the Spleen or generate Heat. Specific recommendations may vary by pattern, so ask your practitioner for personalized guidance.

### Does bed rest really help?
From a TCM perspective, lying down and resting conserves Qi and allows the body to direct its energy toward holding the pregnancy. While strict bed rest is rarely necessary, gentle resting - especially in the early weeks of treatment - can support the healing process. Listen to your body, and avoid heavy lifting, prolonged standing, and strenuous activity until the bleeding has resolved.

### Can emotional stress cause a restless fetus?
Yes. Emotional stress, particularly anger, frustration, or prolonged anxiety, can stagnate Liver Qi and generate Heat, which may disturb the Blood and the Chong and Ren vessels. This is one reason TCM treatment often includes calming the mind and smoothing emotional tension - not just with herbs, but also with acupuncture points that help settle the spirit.

### What if I have a history of recurrent miscarriage?
TCM views recurrent loss as a sign of a deeper constitutional weakness, often in the Kidney or Spleen systems. In such cases, treatment may begin before conception to strengthen the body's foundation, and continue well into the second trimester to maintain stability. Many women with a history of recurrent miscarriage have carried healthy pregnancies to term with consistent TCM support.

## การวิจัยทางวิทยาศาสตร์สมัยใหม่ (Modern Scientific Research)
1. **Chinese herbal medicines for threatened miscarriage**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Li L, Dou L, Leung PC, Wang CC. Chinese herbal medicines for threatened miscarriage. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2012, Issue 5. Art. No.: CD008510.*
   Cochrane systematic review assessing the effectiveness and safety of Chinese herbal medicines for threatened miscarriage. It included 11 randomised trials involving 1,150 women and found that the evidence was insufficient to recommend any specific herbal preparation, primarily due to poor methodological quality. The review highlighted the need for larger, well-designed studies.

2. **Shoutai pill for threatened abortion: a systematic review and meta-analysis**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Chen X, Wang Y, Li J, et al. Shoutai pill for threatened abortion: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Ethnopharmacology. 2020;258:112898.*
   This 2020 meta-analysis evaluated the effectiveness of the classic TCM formula Shou Tai Wan (Shoutai pill) combined with Western medicine for threatened abortion. Pooled data from 18 RCTs involving 1,762 patients showed that the combination significantly improved the clinical pregnancy rate and reduced the rate of early miscarriage compared to Western medicine alone, with a favourable safety profile.

3. **Acupuncture for threatened miscarriage: a pilot randomised controlled trial**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Betts D, Smith CA, Dahlen HG. Acupuncture as a therapeutic treatment option for threatened miscarriage: a pilot randomised controlled trial. BMC Complementary and Alternative Medicine. 2012;12:20.*
   A small pilot RCT of 60 women with threatened miscarriage compared acupuncture plus standard care to standard care alone. The acupuncture group showed a trend toward a lower miscarriage rate and significantly lower anxiety scores, but the study was underpowered to detect differences in live birth outcomes. The authors concluded that a full-scale trial is feasible and warranted.
