## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, excessive drooling (sialorrhea) is often a symptom of neurological conditions like Parkinson's disease, cerebral palsy, or stroke, where muscle control over swallowing is impaired. It can also result from gastroesophageal reflux, certain medications, or oral infections. Diagnosis typically involves a physical exam and may include swallow studies or neurological evaluation to identify the underlying cause.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Standard treatments include oral motor therapy, anticholinergic medications (like glycopyrrolate) to reduce saliva production, botulinum toxin injections into salivary glands, or in severe cases, surgical procedures. Behavioral strategies and speech therapy are often first-line for children.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, drooling is understood as a sign that the body's fluid metabolism has gone awry. The Spleen is the main organ responsible for transforming and transporting fluids - it's like the body's drainage system. When Spleen Qi is weak, dampness builds up and overflows, often as thin, watery saliva that leaks from the mouth. This is the most common root, especially when drooling worsens after eating or with fatigue.
The Kidneys also play a crucial role. Kidney Yang is the body's foundational fire that warms and holds fluids in place. When that fire dims - common in older adults or after long illness - the Kidneys lose their grip on water, and saliva can spill out passively, especially during sleep. This type of drooling often comes with lower back soreness and frequent nighttime urination.
Beyond deficiency, excess patterns can also cause drooling. When heat and dampness brew together in the Stomach and Spleen, they produce a thick, sticky saliva with a bitter taste. And when Liver Blood is insufficient, the sinews around the mouth weaken and internal Wind can disrupt control, leading to involuntary drooling with pale complexion and dry eyes. So the same symptom - excess saliva - can stem from very different internal landscapes, each requiring its own treatment strategy.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner begins by asking about the drool itself - is it thin and watery, or thick and sticky? The quality of the saliva is a key clue, because each pattern produces a distinct kind of fluid. They will also ask when it happens, such as during sleep, after meals, or when feeling tired, to understand which organ systems are involved.
If the drool is clear, thin, and copious, and the person feels heavy and fatigued with a poor appetite, a Spleen Deficiency with Dampness pattern is likely. The tongue often looks pale and swollen with tooth marks, and the pulse feels slippery. This is the most common picture, where the digestive system is too weak to manage fluids properly.
When the same thin, watery drool comes with a feeling of cold - cold hands and feet, a preference for warm drinks, and loose stools - the pattern shifts to Spleen Yang Deficiency. Here the tongue is pale with a thin white coating and the pulse is deep, slow, and weak. The internal cold makes it harder for the Spleen to warm and move fluids.
Drooling that appears in older adults, especially with lower back and knee soreness, frequent nighttime urination, and a deep sense of fatigue, points to Kidney Yang Deficiency. The Kidney’s warming and holding power is weakened, so fluids escape upward. The tongue is pale and puffy, and the pulse is deep and thin.
If the drool is thick, sticky, and leaves a bitter or unpleasant taste, with a red tongue and a yellow greasy coating, Damp-Heat in the Stomach and Spleen is the likely cause. This less common pattern arises when heat and dampness brew together in the digestive tract, disrupting fluid distribution and producing a heavy, sticky saliva.
Involuntary drooling accompanied by a pale face, dizziness, and a thin, weak pulse suggests Liver Blood Deficiency. When blood is insufficient to nourish the Liver, internal wind can stir upward, causing uncontrolled drooling. The tongue is usually pale with a thin white coat, and the drool is often thin rather than sticky.

### Spleen Deficiency with Dampness
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-deficiency-with-dampness)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Thin, watery drool that is clear or slightly sticky, Worse after eating, especially heavy or greasy meals, Abdominal bloating and fullness, Fatigue and heavy sensation in the limbs, Poor appetite and loose stools
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, raw foods and iced drinks, Overeating or heavy meals, Damp, humid weather, Mental overwork, worry, or stress, Greasy, fried foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, cooked meals and drinks, Rest after eating, Gentle, consistent movement, Warm drinks and soups
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is responsible for transforming and transporting fluids throughout the body. When Spleen Qi is weak, this function falters, allowing dampness to accumulate internally. That excess dampness then rises and manifests as a steady overflow of thin, watery saliva that the mouth cannot hold back, causing drooling.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks, white greasy coating, moist surface. Pulse: soggy (ru) and slow (huan), possibly slippery (hua).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold and raw foods further weaken the Spleen's digestive fire and directly contribute to dampness, making drooling worse. Warm, cooked meals are easy to digest and support the Spleen's ability to transform fluids, while gentle movement keeps Qi circulating so dampness does not stagnate.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Favor warm, cooked foods like soups, stews, and well-cooked grains. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that burden the Spleen and generate dampness. Gentle daily movement, such as walking or tai chi, supports Qi flow without overtaxing the body.

### Spleen Yang Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-yang-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Clear, thin, watery drool, Cold hands and feet, Poor appetite and loose stools with undigested food, Drooling worsens with cold foods or weather, Dull abdominal pain that feels better with warmth
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, raw foods and iced drinks, Overeating or heavy meals, Cold, damp weather, Prolonged standing or physical overwork
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm drinks and soups, Applying a warm compress to the belly, Small, frequent meals, Rest and avoiding overwork
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen's Yang is like a pilot light that warms and transforms fluids. When it weakens, internal cold develops and fluid metabolism stalls. Instead of being properly transported, clear, watery fluids overflow in the mouth as drool.
This drooling is often chronic and worsens after eating cold foods or during cold weather, because the body lacks the warmth to process moisture.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, and tender with teeth marks and a white slippery coating. Pulse: deep, slow, and weak, especially weak at the right guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Spleen Yang is easily damaged by cold and dampness, which directly weaken its ability to transform fluids. Warmth, both internal from food and external from compresses, rekindles the digestive fire and helps the Spleen hold fluids in. Small meals and rest prevent overburdening the already weak Spleen, while overeating and cold foods further drain its Yang, worsening drooling.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like soups and stews, and avoid raw, cold, or iced items. Sip warm ginger tea throughout the day and keep your abdomen covered and warm. Gentle exercise like walking supports Spleen function without draining energy.

### Kidney Yang Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-yang-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Clear, watery drool that leaks out easily, often worse at night, Cold and aching lower back and knees, Frequent urination, especially waking at night to pee, Feeling cold all over, especially in the lower body, Deep fatigue and low spirits
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold weather or drafts, Prolonged standing or physical overwork, Cold, raw foods and iced drinks
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth and keeping the lower back warm, Rest and adequate sleep, Warm, cooked meals and drinks
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Kidney Yang is the body’s foundational fire-it warms, steams, and holds fluids in place. When that fire dims, the Kidneys lose their grip on water. Saliva, which is a form of body fluid, is no longer properly controlled and can spill out passively, especially when you are relaxed or asleep. This is why the drooling tends to be clear and watery, and why it often appears alongside a deep coldness in the lower back and knees.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, and tender with teeth marks, coated in a wet white fur. Pulse: deep, slow, and weak, especially faint at the Chi (third) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Kidney Yang is like the pilot light of the body-it warms and controls fluids. Cold, overwork, and raw foods smother that fire, making it harder for the body to hold onto saliva. Warmth, rest, and warm nourishment gently feed the flame, restoring the Kidneys’ ability to secure fluids and stop the involuntary drooling.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like soups, stews, and congees. Add a little ginger, cinnamon, or black pepper to meals. Avoid raw, cold, and frozen foods and drinks, which drain the digestive fire. Keep your lower back and feet warm, and rest well-overwork and late nights weaken Kidney Yang further.

### Damp-Heat in Stomach and Spleen
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-stomach-and-spleen)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Thick, sticky drool with bitter or slimy taste, Yellow greasy tongue coating, especially in the centre, Bloating and fullness in the upper abdomen, Heavy feeling in the body and limbs, Thirst with little desire to drink
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Greasy, fried foods, Alcohol or sugary drinks, Spicy, hot-natured foods, Damp, humid weather, Overeating or heavy meals
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Light, bland meals, Bitter leafy greens, Warm, dry environment, Gentle, consistent movement
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When dampness and heat brew together in the stomach and spleen, normal fluid transformation breaks down. The spleen fails to manage moisture, while stomach heat steams fluids upward. This produces a thick, sticky saliva that overflows the mouth, often with a bitter or slimy taste.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, possibly swollen with teeth marks, thick yellow greasy coating especially at centre and root. Pulse: soggy (Ru) and rapid (Shu), often slippery (Hua).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-heat in the stomach and spleen is like a steamy, stagnant kitchen. Greasy, spicy, or sugary foods and alcohol add more fuel and moisture, making the steam thicker and the drool more sticky and bitter. Light, bland, and bitter foods help drain dampness and cool the heat, while gentle movement encourages the spleen to transform and transport fluids properly.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Shift to light, easily digested meals: steamed greens, congee, and small portions of bitter vegetables like dandelion greens. Strictly avoid greasy, fried, or heavily spiced foods, as well as alcohol, which add more dampness and heat. Gentle daily movement like walking helps keep qi and fluids moving.

### Liver Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Thin, clear drool that leaks involuntarily, often at night or when tired, Pale complexion lacking lustre, Dizziness or lightheadedness, Blurred vision, dry eyes, or poor night vision, Brittle, dry, or ridged nails, Numbness or tingling of the limbs, Scanty menstruation with pale blood (in women)
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Mental overwork, worry, or stress, Excessive screen time or reading, Spicy, drying foods, Heavy menstrual bleeding or postpartum
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and adequate sleep, Warm, cooked meals and drinks, Gentle, consistent movement
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Liver stores Blood and governs the sinews. When Liver Blood is insufficient, the sinews - including the muscles around the mouth - lose their nourishment and proper control. At the same time, Blood deficiency can give rise to internal Wind that stirs upward, disrupting the normal containment of saliva and causing involuntary drooling.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body, especially on the sides; thin, slightly dry with a thin white coating. Pulse: fine (xi) and choppy (se), often with a wiry (xian) quality at the left middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Liver Blood is easily depleted by mental strain, late nights, and excessive visual focus, all of which worsen the deficiency and stir internal Wind. Rest, nourishing foods, and gentle movement replenish Blood and calm the Liver, helping muscles regain tone and saliva to be properly contained.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat blood-building foods like dark leafy greens, beets, black sesame, eggs, and small amounts of red meat or liver. Avoid overwork, excessive screen time, and late nights, which drain Liver Blood. Gentle, rhythmic movement like tai chi or walking supports healthy blood flow without depleting reserves.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### What does it mean if I drool during sleep?
Drooling during sleep is often a sign of Spleen or Kidney deficiency in TCM. The Spleen manages fluids, and when it's weak, saliva can overflow as you relax. If the drool is thin and watery and you also feel cold or have lower back pain, it may point to Kidney Yang not holding fluids in. Simple dietary changes and warming herbs can make a big difference for many people.

### Can acupuncture stop drooling?
Yes, acupuncture can help regulate the flow of saliva by strengthening the Spleen and Stomach, warming the Kidneys, or clearing damp-heat. Points like Stomach 36 and Spleen 9 are commonly used to improve fluid metabolism. Most patients notice a gradual reduction in drooling after several weekly sessions, especially when combined with herbal formulas and dietary adjustments.

### Is drooling a sign of a serious health problem?
Not always. In TCM, drooling is often linked to digestive weakness or internal cold, which are very treatable. However, if drooling appears suddenly with facial weakness, slurred speech, or confusion, it could indicate a stroke - seek emergency care immediately. For persistent drooling without those red flags, a TCM pattern diagnosis can uncover the root cause.

### How can I stop drooling naturally?
Start by warming up your diet: eat cooked, warm foods and avoid icy drinks and raw salads, which weaken the Spleen. Ginger tea or cinnamon can help warm the middle. If your drool is thick and sticky, steer clear of greasy, sweet, and dairy-heavy foods that create dampness. Acupuncture and herbs tailored to your specific pattern often speed up the process.

### Will changing my diet help with drooling?
Absolutely. In TCM, diet is a first-line treatment for drooling. Cold and raw foods can damage Spleen Yang, making fluid control worse. Favor soups, stews, and easily digestible grains. For damp-heat patterns, bitter greens and light meals help. Many patients see improvement just by switching to warm, cooked meals and avoiding heavy, damp-producing foods.

### Can TCM help with drooling caused by Parkinson's disease?
TCM can often reduce drooling in people with Parkinson's by addressing underlying patterns like Spleen deficiency or Kidney weakness. While it doesn't cure the neurological condition, many patients find that herbs and acupuncture lessen saliva overflow and improve comfort. Always coordinate with your neurologist, and never stop prescribed medications without medical guidance.
