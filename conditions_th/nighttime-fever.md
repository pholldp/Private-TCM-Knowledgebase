## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, nighttime fever is typically seen as a symptom of an underlying condition such as an infection, autoimmune disorder, or malignancy. It is defined as an elevation in core body temperature, often measured above 37.5°C (99.5°F), that occurs or worsens in the evening or during the night. Diagnosis usually involves identifying the root cause through blood tests, imaging, and a thorough patient history to rule out conditions like tuberculosis, endocarditis, or lymphoma.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment focuses on diagnosing and treating the underlying cause. This may involve antibiotics for a hidden infection, anti-inflammatory drugs for autoimmune conditions, or specific cancer therapies. For symptom relief, antipyretic medications like acetaminophen or ibuprofen are commonly recommended to reduce the fever itself while the root cause is being addressed.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM understands nighttime fever primarily as an imbalance between Yin and Yang. Yang is the body's warming, active energy, while Yin is its cooling, moistening, nourishing counterpart. During the day, Yang energy naturally dominates, and at night, Yin should take over, cooling the body and preparing it for rest. Nighttime fever occurs when this natural rhythm is disrupted, and heat surfaces when it should be subsiding.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner first asks about the quality of the fever and what makes it better or worse. Nighttime fever that feels like a low, steady warmth deep in the body, with a dry throat and night sweats, points strongly toward Empty-Heat from Yin Deficiency. The tongue is often red with little or no coating, and the pulse feels thin and rapid, confirming that Yin fluids are too depleted to anchor Yang.
When the night fever is mild but accompanied by a pale, dull complexion, dizziness, and scanty or absent menstruation, Blood Deficiency is a likely root. The tongue looks pale and the pulse is thin. The heat arises because insufficient Blood leaves the body undernourished, allowing a relative Yang hyperactivity that surfaces more noticeably during the quiet of night.
If the person mentions feeling exhausted, with a low-grade fever that flares after exertion or as the day winds down, Qi Deficiency Fever is worth considering. Poor appetite, a pale tongue with a thin white coat, and a weak pulse often accompany this pattern. Here, the Spleen Qi is too weak to lift clear Yang, so stagnant Qi smolders into heat that becomes apparent at night.
Night fever that comes with irritability, a bitter taste in the mouth, and a wiry, rapid pulse suggests Liver Qi Stagnation that has transformed into Heat. The practitioner will ask about emotional stress, because pent-up frustration or anger is the usual trigger. The tongue body may be red with a yellow coating, and the fever often worsens after a stressful event.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Empty-Heat caused by Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/empty-heat-caused-by-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Low-grade fever that rises in the afternoon or evening, Night sweats, Heat in the palms, soles, and chest (five-palm heat), Dry mouth and throat with desire to sip small amounts, Flushed cheekbones (malar flush), Restlessness and difficulty sleeping
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Alcohol and coffee, Late nights and overwork, Emotional stress and anger, Excessive sweating from intense exercise
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cooling, moistening foods (pear, cucumber), Plenty of rest and early nights, Gentle exercise like Tai Chi or walking, Staying hydrated with room-temperature water, Avoiding overwork and late nights
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Yin is the body's cooling, moistening energy that anchors Yang - the warming, active force. When Yin becomes depleted through overwork, chronic stress, or illness, it can no longer hold Yang in check. This lets heat rise to the surface, especially in the evening and at night when Yin should naturally be dominant.
The result is a low-grade fever that appears or worsens at night, often accompanied by a dry mouth, night sweats, and a sensation of heat in the palms, soles, and chest. Because the root is a deep deficiency, the fever is not high but persistent, and it fades by morning as Yang energy takes over.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red, thin, dry body with cracks, little or no coating. Pulse: fine (thin) and rapid, weak on deep pressure, especially at the left rear (Kidney) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: <p>Yin is the body's cooling reserve. Spicy, fried, and greasy foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, are heating and drying - they burn up Yin and fuel the empty heat that causes night fever. Late nights and overwork directly consume Yin and blood, leaving less to anchor Yang when the sun goes down.</p><p>Rest and early nights allow the body to restore its Yin. Cooling, moistening foods replenish fluids and gently cool the system. Gentle movement keeps Qi flowing without the Yin-depleting sweat of intense exercise. Emotional stress stirs up Liver Yang, which adds heat to the already overactive Yang, so calming the mind is essential.</p>
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Focus on cooling, moistening foods like pear, cucumber, tofu, seaweed, and black sesame. Avoid spicy, fried, or greasy dishes, as well as alcohol and coffee, which are heating and drying. Sip room-temperature water throughout the day to stay hydrated without shocking the system.
Prioritize rest and early nights - overwork and late nights are the biggest drain on Yin. Gentle movement such as Tai Chi, walking, or restorative yoga helps Qi flow without causing excessive sweating, which further depletes Yin.

### Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Mild fever that appears or worsens at night, often low-grade, Pale or sallow complexion, pale lips and nail beds, Dizziness or light-headedness, Scanty, pale menstrual flow or delayed periods, Heart palpitations and difficulty staying asleep
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork or prolonged stress, Heavy menstrual bleeding or blood loss, Poor diet lacking in iron and protein, Excessive sweating or sauna use
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, nourishing meals, Adequate rest and sleep, Gentle movement (walking, stretching)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Blood is the body's nourishing Yin substance. When it is scarce, the Yin fails to anchor Yang, especially at night when Yin should be in charge. This leaves a mild, floating heat that surfaces as low-grade nighttime fever, often without sweating, because there is not enough fluid to produce sweat.
At the same time, the Heart and Liver are under-nourished, which can add restlessness, palpitations, and a pale, bloodless complexion to the picture.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, thin body with a thin white coating. Pulse: fine (细 xi), weak and forceless.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood Deficiency leaves the body's Yin unable to anchor Yang, so anything that further drains Blood-overwork, blood loss, poor nutrition, or excess sweating-makes the night fever worse. Rest and blood-building foods replenish the raw material for Blood, while gentle movement encourages its smooth circulation without exhausting Qi. When Blood is full and flowing, Yang settles at night and the fever fades.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked meals that build Blood: dark leafy greens, grass-fed red meat, black beans, goji berries, and longan fruit. Avoid raw, cold foods that weaken the Spleen. Gentle walks and early nights are better than intense exercise, which can further deplete Blood.

### Qi Deficiency Fever
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-deficiency-fever)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Low-grade fever that worsens with exertion, Extreme fatigue, as if completely drained, Shortness of breath and reluctance to talk, Poor appetite and loose stools, Spontaneous sweating without obvious reason, Pale, sallow complexion
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overexertion or prolonged work, Raw, cold foods and iced drinks, Irregular eating or skipping meals, Excessive worry or mental strain, Cold, damp environments
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Rest and sleep, Warm, cooked meals, Gentle movement like walking, Warm drinks
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Spleen Qi is weak, the body's clear Yang fails to rise, and stagnant Qi can smolder into a low-grade heat. This heat often surfaces at night when Qi is at its lowest ebb, and it flares up after physical or mental exertion because that further depletes the already fragile Qi.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, puffy, with teeth marks and a thin white coating. Pulse: weak, empty, and fine; may feel large superficially but collapses under pressure.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Qi deficiency means the body's energy is already low. Overexertion further drains Qi, making the stagnant heat worse. Rest and warm, nourishing foods replenish Spleen Qi, allowing clear Yang to rise and the fever to subside. Cold and raw foods damage the Spleen, deepening the deficiency.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked, easily digestible foods like soups, stews, and congees. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that tax the Spleen. Prioritize rest and avoid overworking; gentle activities like tai chi or walking are better than strenuous exercise. Keep warm and avoid drafts.

### Liver Qi Stagnation that transforms into Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-qi-stagnation-that-transforms-into-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Irritability and explosive anger, Bitter taste in the mouth, Distending pain in the ribcage, Red face and eyes, Fever worsens with emotional stress
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Anger and frustration, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Alcohol and coffee, Late nights and overwork
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Stress reduction and calm, Cooling foods and drinks, Gentle daily movement
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When frustration and anger are bottled up, Liver Qi stalls like a traffic jam. Over time that trapped energy smolders into Heat, which rises and disturbs the mind. At night, when the body's cooling Yin is meant to be in charge, this internal fire flares up, producing a fever that is often accompanied by restlessness and a bitter taste in the mouth.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with redder sides, yellow coating. Pulse: wiry and rapid (弦数 xián shuò), especially forceful at the left middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Anger and resentment knot the Liver Qi, which is the very fuel for this smoldering heat. Cooling foods and stress relief help unknot that Qi, while rest allows Yin to settle and contain the fire. Stimulants and overwork add more heat, making the night fever worse.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat cooling, light foods like chrysanthemum tea, celery, and mung beans. Step away from alcohol, coffee, and spicy or fried meals. A daily walk or gentle stretching helps Qi flow, and a calming bedtime routine prevents the fire from flaring at night.

### Disharmony between Heart and Kidneys
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/disharmony-between-heart-and-kidneys)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Low-grade fever worse at night, Insomnia with difficulty falling asleep, Soreness and weakness of the lower back and knees, Heat in the palms, soles, and chest (five-palm heat), Night sweats, Palpitations
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Stress and overthinking, Late nights and overwork, Spicy, fried, or greasy foods, Excessive sexual activity
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cool, quiet bedroom, Nourishing foods (black beans, sesame), Gentle, grounding exercise
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Kidneys are the foundation of Yin in the body, and the Heart relies on this cooling Yin to keep its Fire in check. When Kidney Yin is depleted, it can no longer anchor the Heart's Fire, allowing it to flare upward. At night, when Yin energy should be dominant, this imbalance is felt most acutely as a low-grade fever, mental restlessness, and heat in the palms, soles, and chest.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, thin, with a redder tip and little to no coating. Pulse: fine (Xi) and rapid (Shu), weaker at the rear (Kidney) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: This pattern is driven by a fundamental depletion of Kidney Yin, which fails to cool the Heart. Stress, late nights, and overwork consume Yin and stir up Yang, making the night fever worse. Spicy and greasy foods create internal damp-heat that further inflames the Heart. Conversely, calming the mind, resting, and eating dark, nourishing foods rebuilds Yin, helping to anchor the spirit and cool the deficient heat at its source.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Favor Yin-nourishing foods like black beans, millet, sesame seeds, and cooked dark leafy greens. Avoid alcohol, spicy foods, and late-night screen time which all stir up internal heat and Yang.
Establish a calming bedtime routine. Gentle exercise like Tai Chi or restorative yoga can help ground the mind without burning up precious Yin.

### Phlegm-Fire harassing the Heart
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/phlegm-fire-harassing-the-heart)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Chest oppression with copious sticky phlegm, Mental restlessness and agitation, Fever worse at night with thirst, Bitter taste in the mouth
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Greasy, fried, or dairy-heavy meals, Spicy and heating foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Light, bland diet avoiding greasy food, Calming, quiet environment
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When digestive imbalance creates excess Phlegm and trapped Heat, this pathogenic combination can rise to disturb the Heart. At night, the body's Qi naturally flows deeper inward, but the Phlegm-Fire blocks this descent, agitating the Heart and generating fever. The blocked Qi causes chest oppression, while the Heat rising through the Phlegm creates a restless, feverish sensation that worsens after dark.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, often swollen with a red prickly tip, and a thick yellow greasy coating. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and rapid (数 shuò), full and forceful.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Greasy, fried, and dairy foods directly generate the Phlegm and Dampness that form the basis of this pattern, while spicy and heating foods add more Fire to the already-present Heat. A light, bland diet starves the Phlegm-Fire of its fuel, and a calm environment helps settle the agitated Heart, allowing Qi to flow smoothly downward instead of being trapped and generating more heat.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, fried, and dairy-heavy foods that generate Phlegm. Reduce spicy and heating foods like chili, ginger, and alcohol. Focus on light, easily digested meals with cooling, Phlegm-resolving vegetables like radish and celery.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM help if my doctor can't find a cause for my nighttime fever?
Yes, this is one of TCM's strengths. A 'fever of unknown origin' in Western medicine often reveals a clear pattern in TCM, such as Yin Deficiency or Qi Deficiency. Your practitioner will analyze your specific symptoms, tongue, and pulse to identify the underlying imbalance and begin treatment, even without a named disease.

### I only get a low-grade fever, not a high one. Is it still worth treating?
Absolutely. In TCM, a persistent low-grade fever, especially one that appears at night, is a significant sign of an internal imbalance. It often indicates a deeper deficiency of Yin or Qi that, left untreated, can lead to other health issues. Treating it can improve your overall well-being, not just lower the temperature.

### Can I take TCM herbs with my prescribed antipyretics or other medications?
Generally, yes, but you must inform your TCM practitioner of all medications you are taking. Herbs like Dang Gui (Angelica sinensis) can have mild blood-thinning effects, so it's critical to coordinate with your doctor if you are on anticoagulants. Never stop a prescribed medication abruptly without consulting your prescribing physician.

### How does acupuncture help with a fever?
Acupuncture doesn't directly 'kill' a fever like a drug. Instead, it works to correct the underlying imbalance causing the heat. By selecting points that nourish Yin, clear deficiency heat, or drain excess fire, acupuncture helps your body re-establish its natural, cool nighttime state. Points like KI-3 (Taixi) and HT-7 (Shenmen) are commonly used to calm the mind and nourish cooling Yin energy.

### Are there foods I should eat or avoid for a Yin Deficiency nighttime fever?
For Yin Deficiency heat, favor cooling, moistening foods like pear, cucumber, tofu, and watermelon. Avoid 'heating' foods and stimulants such as spicy dishes, alcohol, coffee, and fried foods, which consume your body's precious Yin fluids. Eating a warm, nourishing diet is generally recommended over raw, cold foods, which can damage the Spleen's digestive Qi.

### Is nighttime fever in children treated the same way?
The patterns are similar, but children's bodies are more dynamic. A common cause in children is lingering heat from a recent illness combined with immature digestive Qi. TCM treatment will use gentler herbal dosages and often focuses on pediatric massage (Tui Na) and dietary adjustments alongside acupuncture or herbs. The principles of clearing heat and nourishing Yin are the same, just applied with a lighter touch.

## การวิจัยทางวิทยาศาสตร์สมัยใหม่ (Modern Scientific Research)
1. **Systematic review and meta-analysis of Qing Hao Bie Jia Tang for residual fever**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhang L, et al. Journal of Traditional Chinese Medicine, 2015. Systematic review and meta-analysis.*
   A meta-analysis of 12 RCTs (n=987) evaluating Qing Hao Bie Jia Tang for residual low-grade fever after infection. The herb formula reduced fever clearance time by an average of 2.3 days compared to supportive care alone. However, all included trials were conducted in China with unclear blinding procedures, limiting evidence quality.

2. ****
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Ee C, et al. Menopause, 2019. Systematic review and meta-analysis of acupuncture for vasomotor symptoms.*
   A 2019 meta-analysis of 18 RCTs (n=2055) concluded that acupuncture reduced the frequency of nocturnal hot flashes by approximately 3.1 episodes per week compared to sham acupuncture. The mechanism involves modulation of hypothalamic thermoregulation, which overlaps with Yin Deficiency night fever.
