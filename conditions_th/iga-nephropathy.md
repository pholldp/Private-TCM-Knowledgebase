## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
IgA nephropathy (also known as Berger's disease) is a kidney disorder where deposits of the antibody immunoglobulin A build up in the glomeruli - the tiny filtering units of the kidneys. This triggers inflammation and scarring that can lead to blood and protein in the urine, high blood pressure, and over time, reduced kidney function.
It is the most common form of primary glomerulonephritis worldwide and is diagnosed through a kidney biopsy, often after routine urine tests show persistent microscopic blood or protein.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Standard care focuses on controlling blood pressure and reducing protein loss to slow disease progression. First-line medications usually include ACE inhibitors or ARBs. In cases with persistent proteinuria despite these measures, corticosteroids or other immunosuppressants may be used. Supportive measures like limiting dietary salt and protein are also recommended, and in advanced disease, dialysis or transplantation may become necessary.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM understands IgA nephropathy primarily through the Kidney and Spleen organ systems. The Kidneys store the body's most precious essence (Jīng) and govern water metabolism. When Kidney Qi is weak, it cannot secure this essence, and precious substances - protein and blood - leak into the urine.
The Spleen transforms food into Qi and Blood and manages fluid distribution; when it is deficient, dampness accumulates, causing the puffiness, edema, and heavy fatigue so common in this condition.
Over time, the disease drains both Qi and Yin, creating a state of depletion where the body's cooling, nourishing fluids run low. This can generate Empty Heat and, in more advanced stages, internal Wind - a restless, stirring force that rises upward, causing dizziness, trembling, and further damage to the kidney's filtering network. At the same time, sluggish blood flow from long-standing illness can lead to Blood Stasis, which deepens the damage and creates fixed, stabbing pain.
This is why one Western diagnosis of IgA nephropathy can look so different from person to person. A patient with pale, puffy skin, loose stools, and feeling cold points to Spleen and Kidney Yang Deficiency. Another with a dry mouth, night sweats, and a red tongue with little coating is showing Qi and Yin Deficiency. A third with dark, burning urine and a greasy yellow tongue coating has Damp-Heat. Each pattern reflects a different stage and combination of imbalances, and each calls for a different treatment approach.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
In IgA nephropathy, the most common underlying pattern is Qi and Yin Deficiency, where both energy and fluids are depleted. This often manifests as persistent fatigue, a dry mouth, insomnia, and a pale or red tongue with little coating. The pulse is thin and weak, and night sweats or a low-grade heat sensation may appear.
Fatigue, poor appetite, loose stools, and puffiness point to Spleen and Kidney Qi Deficiency. The Spleen cannot transform food into Qi, and the Kidney loses its grip on essence, leading to protein in the urine. The tongue appears pale and may be slightly swollen, while the pulse is weak and forceless.
When Kidney Yin deficiency deepens, it can fail to anchor Yang, generating internal Wind. Dizziness, ringing in the ears, and foamy urine are key clues, along with a red tongue with scanty coating and a thin, rapid pulse. This pattern is less common but significant when present.
Cold hands and feet, frequent nighttime urination, and noticeable swelling signal Kidney and Spleen Yang Deficiency. Here the warming, transforming function of Yang is too feeble to handle fluids. The tongue is pale and puffy with a thin white coat, and the pulse is deep and slow.
Chronic, fixed low-back pain and a dark, purplish complexion suggest Blood Stagnation. Long-standing illness has created stasis in the kidney collaterals, worsening proteinuria. The tongue shows purple spots or a dusky body, and the pulse may feel choppy or wiry.
During acute flare-ups, nausea, a bitter taste, and a thick yellow greasy tongue coating point to Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner. The urine becomes turbid, and the person may feel heavy or feverish. The pulse is often slippery and rapid, reflecting the heat and dampness disturbing the bladder and kidney.

### Qi and Yin Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-yin-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Persistent fatigue and weakness, Dry mouth and throat with little desire to drink, Night sweats and warm palms/soles, Scanty, dark urine, Shortness of breath on exertion, Insomnia
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Spicy and fried foods, Lack of sleep, Emotional stress and worry
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Adequate rest and sleep, Warm, moistening foods (congee, yams), Gentle exercise (walking, tai chi), Sipping warm water
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  As IgA Nephropathy progresses, it gradually drains the body's Qi and Yin. Qi deficiency weakens the Kidney's ability to secure essence, leading to proteinuria and persistent fatigue. Yin deficiency generates Empty Heat, which can damage the delicate blood vessels in the kidneys, causing microscopic hematuria, while also drying fluids, resulting in a parched mouth and scanty dark urine.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red or pale-red body with a thin or absent coating, dry and possibly cracked. Pulse: fine, weak, and often rapid.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Overwork and stress consume Qi, while late nights and spicy foods burn Yin, directly aggravating this dual deficiency. Rest and nourishing, moistening foods rebuild both Qi and Yin, while gentle exercise helps move Qi without depleting it.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Prioritize rest and avoid overwork, which depletes Qi. Eat warm, easy-to-digest foods like congee, yams, and goji berries to nourish Yin without burdening digestion. Sip warm water throughout the day to ease dry mouth, and avoid spicy, fried, or overly heating foods that can further consume Yin fluids.

### Empty-Wind agitating in the Interior
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/empty-wind-agitating-in-the-interior)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Trembling or twitching of hands and feet, Persistent fatigue and weakness, Crimson, mirror-like tongue with little coating, Dry mouth and throat, night sweats, Dizziness, blurred vision, Foamy or turbid urine (proteinuria)
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Spicy and fried foods, Emotional stress and worry, Lack of sleep, Alcohol and coffee
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Adequate rest and sleep, Cooling, moistening foods, Quiet, dark environment, Gentle stretching
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  In IgA nephropathy, severe Kidney Yin deficiency fails to anchor Yang, generating deficiency heat and internal Wind. This Wind stirs upward, disturbing the kidney's ability to secure essence, leading to protein and blood leaking into the urine. The heat dries body fluids, causing the crimson, mirror-like tongue, night sweats, and dry throat.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: crimson, thin, dry, cracked, little or no coating (mirror-like), may tremble. Pulse: fine, rapid, may be wiry at left Guan, lacks force.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: This pattern arises from deep Yin deficiency and deficiency heat stirring Wind. Rest and cooling, moistening foods replenish Yin and calm the heat, while overwork, stress, and heating foods further deplete Yin and fan the flames, making the Wind more active. Quiet and darkness help the body conserve Yin and settle the spirit.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Focus on deeply nourishing Yin with cooling, moistening foods like pear, black sesame, tofu, and millet. Avoid spicy, fried, and heating foods that dry up fluids. Prioritize rest and avoid overwork, as exhaustion depletes Yin further. Gentle movement like Tai Chi or restorative yoga can help without stirring Wind.

### Spleen and Kidney Qi Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-and-kidney-qi-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Persistent fatigue and weakness, Poor appetite and loose stools, Lower back soreness and weak knees, Pale, puffy face and mild edema, Frequent urination, especially at night, Feeling of heaviness in the body
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overwork and exhaustion, Raw, cold, or iced foods, Exposure to cold weather or drafts, Prolonged standing, Emotional stress and worry
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, easily digested meals, Adequate rest and sleep, Gentle exercise (walking, tai chi), Warmth on the lower back (heating pad, warm compress)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  In IgA Nephropathy, the Spleen fails to transform and transport fluids and food essence, leading to dampness that spills into the urine as foam and causes puffiness and edema. At the same time, Kidney Qi is too weak to secure the body's vital essence, which escapes as protein in the urine.
The result is a cycle of depletion: poor appetite and loose stools further weaken the Spleen, while lower back soreness and frequent urination reflect the Kidneys’ loss of holding power. Fatigue becomes overwhelming because the body's core energy supply-Spleen and Kidney Qi-is running low.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Pale, swollen body with teeth marks and a white, moist or slippery coating. Pulse: Deep, weak, and fine, especially at the right Guan and both Chi positions.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: The Spleen and Kidneys are the foundation of postnatal and prenatal Qi. Overwork, cold foods, and damp environments further weaken these organs, while warm, restful habits and gentle movement help preserve and restore Qi, allowing the body to better manage fluids and essence.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like soups, congee, and steamed vegetables, and avoid raw, cold, or greasy items that burden the Spleen. A pinch of ginger in cooking can help warm the digestive fire.
Gentle daily movement such as walking or Tai Chi supports Qi without depleting it. Prioritize rest, keep the lower back warm, and avoid standing for long periods.

### Kidney and Spleen Yang Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/kidney-and-spleen-yang-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Cold hands and feet, especially below the knees, Early-morning diarrhea with undigested food, Sore, cold sensation in the lower back and knees, Pale, swollen tongue with teeth marks and white coating, Deep, weak, slow pulse
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Exposure to cold weather or drafts, Raw, cold, or iced foods, Overwork and exhaustion, Damp living conditions, Prolonged standing
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warmth on the lower back (heating pad, warm compress), Rest and lying down, Warm, cooked meals and soups, Moxibustion on the low back and belly, Gentle exercise (walking, tai chi)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Kidney Yang is the body's pilot light, warming every part of you and driving water metabolism. In IgA nephropathy, when that fire dims together with Spleen Yang, the body grows cold from the inside. The Spleen can no longer cook food properly, so loose stools and early-morning diarrhea appear, while the Kidneys lose their grip on essence, spilling protein into the urine as foam. At the same time, water piles up because there is not enough heat to move it, leading to puffy ankles and legs.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Pale, swollen, puffy tongue body with teeth marks, white slippery coating. Pulse is deep, weak, and often slow; both Chi positions feeble, right Guan weak.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold and dampness act like throwing water on a small fire, further depleting the body's dwindling Yang. Warmth, rest, and warm, cooked foods protect and gently stoke that internal flame, helping the Spleen and Kidneys transform fluids and hold onto vital essence. Overexertion and cold exposure drain the little Yang that remains, which is why they quickly worsen edema, diarrhea, and coldness.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like soups, stews, and congees. Add gentle warming spices such as ginger, cinnamon, and black pepper. Strictly avoid raw, cold, and frozen foods. Keep the lower back and abdomen warm; a hot water bottle or belly warmer helps. Gentle daily movement like walking or tai chi is ideal, but stop before you break a sweat to avoid draining Yang.

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing low-back pain, Pain worse at night and with pressure, Dark or dusky facial complexion, Dark circles under the eyes, Purplish lips and nails, Rough, dry, scaly skin
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Exposure to cold weather or drafts, Anger and frustration, Raw, cold, or iced foods
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle exercise (walking, tai chi), Warmth on the lower back (heating pad, warm compress), Turmeric and ginger in food, Stress management (meditation)
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Long-standing IgA Nephropathy damages the tiny blood vessels of the kidneys, leading to sluggish blood flow and stasis. This stasis blocks the kidney collaterals, causing fixed, stabbing low-back pain and a dark, purplish tongue. The impaired circulation also worsens protein leakage into the urine, making foamy urine more persistent.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body with stasis spots, distended dark sublingual veins, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 Sè) or wiry (弦 Xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Blood stasis is worsened by cold, which congeals the blood, and by inactivity, which allows stasis to settle. Emotional stress causes Liver Qi stagnation, which in turn leads to blood stasis. Gentle movement and warmth invigorate Yang and promote blood flow. Spices like turmeric move blood and dispel stasis, while stress management helps keep Qi moving smoothly.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Incorporate gentle movement like walking or tai chi to keep blood flowing. Avoid cold, raw foods and icy drinks, which can congeal blood. Add warming spices such as turmeric and ginger to meals. Manage stress to prevent Qi stagnation that worsens blood stasis.

### Damp-Heat in the Lower Burner
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-the-lower-burner)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Burning or stinging pain during urination, Heavy, dragging sensation in the legs and lower back, Cloudy, dark yellow, or turbid urine, sometimes with blood, Bitter taste in the mouth and nausea, Yellow greasy tongue coating thickest at the root
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Alcohol and rich, greasy meals, Hot, humid weather, Spicy and fried foods, Overwork and exhaustion
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Eating light, bland foods, Drinking plenty of water, Cool, dry environment, Herbal teas like dandelion
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Dampness and Heat accumulate in the Lower Burner, they disturb the Bladder and Kidney's ability to separate clear from turbid. This forces turbid fluids, blood, and protein to leak into the urine, causing dark, cloudy, or bloody urine. The Damp-Heat also creates a heavy, burning sensation in the lower body and can trigger acute flare-ups of IgA Nephropathy, often after dietary or lifestyle excess.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, slightly swollen, with a yellow greasy coating that is thickest at the root. Pulse: slippery (Hua) and rapid (Shu), more pronounced at the rear (Chi) position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Heat is a combination of pathological moisture and heat. Greasy, spicy, or alcoholic foods directly feed both Dampness and Heat, while hot, humid environments worsen the external Dampness. Cooling, bland foods and plenty of water help drain Dampness and clear Heat, easing the burden on the kidneys and bladder.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid alcohol, fried, greasy, and spicy foods that create more Dampness and Heat. Eat light, cooling foods like mung beans, barley, cucumber, and watermelon. Drink plenty of plain water or dandelion tea to help flush the system. Rest in a cool, dry environment and avoid overexertion during flare-ups.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM cure IgA nephropathy?
In TCM, the goal is not a simple 'cure' but to restore the body's balance so that the kidneys can function as well as possible and disease progression halts or slows. Many patients see significant reductions in proteinuria and stabilization of kidney function over months of consistent treatment, especially when the underlying pattern is correctly identified and addressed. However, advanced scarring cannot be reversed, so early intervention offers the best chance for a deep response.

### How long until I see improvement in my urine tests?
Improvement in symptoms like fatigue and edema often comes first, within the first 4 to 6 weeks. Changes in urine protein and blood levels are slower; meaningful reductions typically appear after 3 to 6 months of consistent herbal and acupuncture treatment. Patience is crucial - the kidneys heal slowly, and the treatment is rebuilding your body's foundational energy, not just suppressing a lab value.

### Is acupuncture safe if I have kidney disease?
Yes, acupuncture is generally safe and well-tolerated in kidney disease when performed by a qualified practitioner. Points are chosen to strengthen the Kidney and Spleen, promote fluid metabolism, and calm internal Wind. Needles are sterile and single-use. If you have severe edema or are on blood thinners, inform your acupuncturist so they can adjust their technique.

### Do I need to avoid certain foods?
Yes, dietary adjustments can powerfully support your treatment. In general, avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods that weaken the Spleen, and limit salt to reduce fluid retention. Specific foods that nourish the Kidney and Spleen include cooked yam, black beans, goji berries, and lotus seeds. Your practitioner will give you more tailored advice based on your pattern - for instance, Damp-Heat patterns require avoiding spicy and rich foods, while Yang deficiency benefits from warming spices like ginger and cinnamon.

### Can I take Chinese herbs with my blood pressure medication?
Yes, TCM herbs can often be used safely alongside ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and other blood pressure medications, but this must be coordinated carefully. Some herbs have diuretic or blood-pressure-lowering effects of their own, which could amplify your medication's effect. Always bring a complete list of your medications to your TCM practitioner, and keep both your doctor and herbalist informed of any changes. Never stop or adjust your prescription medication on your own.

### What does 'internal Wind' mean in IgA nephropathy?
In TCM, 'Wind' is a pathogenic force that causes sudden, moving, or shaking symptoms. Internal Wind arises when Kidney Yin is so depleted that it cannot anchor the body's Yang energy, which then rises erratically. In IgA nephropathy, this can manifest as dizziness, tinnitus, trembling, and a deep crimson tongue with little coating. It signals a more advanced stage of Yin deficiency and requires nourishing and settling treatment, not just draining or cooling.
