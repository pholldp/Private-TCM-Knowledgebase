## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, a coated tongue is typically considered a benign variation, often attributed to poor oral hygiene, dehydration, or the buildup of bacteria, dead cells, and food debris on the tongue's surface. It can also be associated with conditions like oral thrush (Candida), geographic tongue, or systemic issues such as digestive disorders or immune deficiencies.
Diagnosis usually involves visual examination and, if infection is suspected, a swab culture. While a coated tongue is rarely a primary medical concern, it can be a clue to underlying health problems, particularly when accompanied by bad breath or changes in taste.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment focuses on improving oral hygiene - regular brushing of the tongue, using a tongue scraper, staying hydrated, and sometimes antiseptic mouthwashes. If an underlying condition like thrush is identified, antifungal medications may be prescribed.
When the coating is linked to digestive issues, treatment targets the root cause, such as acid reflux or gastritis, but there is no specific medication to clear a tongue coating directly.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the tongue coating is called 'tai' (苔), which literally means 'moss' - and like moss on a damp stone, it grows when there is excess moisture or stagnation in the body. The coating is formed by the 'steam' of digestion rising from the Stomach. When the Stomach and Spleen are healthy, a thin, white, moist coating is normal.
But when digestion is impaired, that steam becomes turbid and condenses into a thick, visible fur. The colour, thickness, and texture of the coating tell the practitioner exactly what kind of imbalance is brewing.
The Spleen and Stomach are the central organs involved. The Spleen transforms food and fluids into Qi and Blood; the Stomach receives and ripens food. If the Spleen is weak, it cannot manage fluids properly, and dampness accumulates - producing a thick, white, greasy coating. If that dampness combines with heat (often from rich foods or stress), the coating becomes yellow and greasy.
When food sits undigested in the Stomach and ferments, it generates turbid dampness and heat, creating a thick, curd-like coating with a foul smell. And when heat blazes directly in the Stomach, it scorches the coating dry and yellow, like a pot boiling over.
Because the tongue coating is a direct reflection of the digestive tract's lining, TCM sees it not as a local problem but as a window into the entire gut environment. The same pattern that causes a coated tongue will also cause bloating, nausea, heavy limbs, or bad breath - depending on which pattern is dominant.
That's why TCM doesn't just scrape off the coating; it treats the underlying Spleen weakness, dampness, heat, or stagnation so that the coating clears naturally as the digestive system regains balance.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner reads the tongue coating like a map of the digestive system. The coating's color, thickness, moisture, and texture reveal which pattern is causing the symptom. They also ask about appetite, digestion, thirst, and energy levels to confirm the diagnosis, because the tongue never lies but the full story comes from the whole person.
If the coating is thick, white, and greasy like cream, and the person feels heavy, bloated, and tired after eating, that points to Spleen Deficiency with Dampness. The Spleen is too weak to manage fluids, so dampness accumulates and rises. The tongue body is often pale and puffy with teeth marks, and the pulse feels slippery.
A thick, yellow, and greasy coating suggests Damp-Heat in the Stomach and Spleen. The person may complain of a sticky taste, bad breath, nausea, and a heavy sensation in the body. The tongue body is redder than normal, and the pulse is rapid and slippery. This pattern combines the sluggishness of dampness with the agitation of heat.
When the coating is thick, sticky, and greasy regardless of whether it is white or yellow, Damp-Phlegm is the likely culprit. The key is the glue-like texture and accompanying symptoms of chest fullness, phlegm in the throat, and dizziness. The tongue may be swollen, and the pulse is slippery. This pattern reflects a deeper stagnation of fluids.
If the coating is thick, greasy, and has a foul smell, and the person suffers from acid reflux, belching, and a distended abdomen, Food Stagnation is at play. Undigested food ferments and creates turbid dampness. The coating may turn yellow if heat builds up. The pulse is often full and slippery.
A thick, dry, and yellow coating without the greasy feel points to Stomach Fire. Here heat is dominant, scorching fluids and leaving the coating parched. The person feels intense thirst, craves cold drinks, and may have burning stomach pain or mouth ulcers. The tongue body is red, and the pulse is rapid and forceful.

### Spleen Deficiency with Dampness
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/spleen-deficiency-with-dampness)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Pale, swollen tongue with teeth marks and a white greasy coating, Feeling of heaviness in the body and limbs, Loose stools or unformed bowel movements, Abdominal bloating, especially after eating, Fatigue and lack of energy, Drowsiness after meals
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Raw, cold, or greasy foods, Damp or humid weather, Overeating or heavy meals, Sedentary lifestyle, Worry and overthinking
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm easily digestible foods, Gentle walking, Dry, warm environment, Small, frequent meals, Ginger tea
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen is responsible for transforming food and fluids into usable energy and moisture. When Spleen Qi is weak, it cannot manage this process properly, causing fluids to pool into internal dampness. That dampness rises and coats the tongue as a thick, white, greasy fur, while the tongue body becomes pale and swollen from the underlying Qi deficiency, often showing teeth marks along the edges.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale, swollen body with teeth marks and a white greasy coating that is moist. Pulse: soggy (Ru) and slow (Huan), possibly with a slippery (Hua) quality.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: The Spleen struggles to transform fluids when Qi is weak. Cold and raw foods further damage Spleen Yang, worsening dampness. Warm, cooked foods and moderate exercise support Spleen Qi and help move dampness out. Worry knots the Spleen Qi, so emotional calm aids recovery.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like soups, stews, and congee. Avoid raw, cold, and greasy foods that burden the Spleen. Gentle daily movement such as walking helps Qi flow and drains dampness. Eat smaller, more frequent meals rather than large heavy ones.

### Damp-Heat in Stomach and Spleen
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-heat-in-stomach-and-spleen)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Thick yellow greasy tongue coating, Bloating and fullness in the upper abdomen, Sticky or incomplete bowel movements, Bitter or sticky taste in the mouth, Heavy feeling in the body and limbs, Thirst with little desire to drink
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Greasy, spicy, or fried food, Alcohol, Damp or humid weather, Overeating or late-night meals, Prolonged stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Light, plain diet, Moderate exercise, Dry, warm environment, Bitter greens and barley
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the Spleen is overwhelmed by heavy, rich foods or a damp environment, it can no longer transform and transport fluids properly. Dampness accumulates in the middle burner, and heat from the Stomach steams this dampness upward. The result is a thick, yellow, greasy coating on the tongue, especially in the centre and back.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, possibly swollen with teeth marks, thick yellow greasy coating especially at centre and root. Pulse: soggy (Ru) and rapid (Shu), often slippery (Hua).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Greasy, rich, and spicy foods directly create dampness and heat in the body, overwhelming the Spleen and Stomach and making the coating thicker and yellower. A light, simple diet and gentle exercise support the Spleen's ability to transform and transport fluids, draining dampness and clearing heat naturally. Humid weather adds external dampness, which compounds the internal problem, so a dry, warm environment helps.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid greasy, fried, spicy, and very sweet foods, as well as alcohol and dairy, which all generate dampness and heat. Eat light, easily digested meals like congee, steamed vegetables, barley, and mung beans. Gentle daily movement such as walking helps the Spleen move Qi and drain dampness.

### Damp-Phlegm
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/damp-phlegm)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Thick, white, greasy tongue coating that is hard to scrape off, Feeling of heaviness in the body and limbs, Chest stuffiness and upper abdominal bloating, Brain fog, dizziness, or muzzy-headedness, Poor appetite, nausea, or loose sticky stools
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overeating or heavy meals, Greasy, fried, or dairy foods, Cold drinks and raw foods, Damp or humid weather, Sedentary lifestyle
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, cooked meals, Gentle daily exercise, Dry, warm environment, Small, frequent meals
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Spleen Qi is weak, it fails to transform and transport body fluids. These fluids stagnate, thicken into dampness, and then condense into phlegm. This turbid, sticky substance rises with Stomach Qi and settles on the tongue, producing the characteristic thick, greasy coating that is difficult to scrape off.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Pale, swollen body with teeth marks; thick, white, greasy, wet coating. Pulse: Slippery (Hua) and possibly soggy (Ru), especially at the right middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Damp-Phlegm forms when the Spleen is too weak to transform fluids, so anything that further burdens the Spleen or adds dampness worsens the coating. Cold, raw, and greasy foods are hardest to digest and promote dampness, while warm, cooked meals support the Spleen's function. Gentle movement keeps Qi flowing and helps the body process fluids, while a sedentary lifestyle allows dampness to accumulate.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw, greasy, and sugary foods that create dampness. Favor warm, cooked meals like soups and stews with ginger and a little pepper. Gentle daily movement like walking helps the Spleen transform fluids.

### Food Stagnation in the Stomach
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/food-stagnation-in-the-stomach)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Thick, greasy, curd-like coating thickest in the centre, Sour, rotten-smelling belching, Epigastric bloating and pain worse after eating, Nausea or vomiting of sour, undigested food, Aversion to food and its smell, Foul-smelling flatulence like rotten eggs
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Overeating, Greasy, fried, or sweet foods, Alcohol, Lying down after meals, Emotional stress
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Light, easily digestible meals, Skipping a meal when bloated, Gentle walking after meals, Warm water or peppermint tea
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When food sits undigested in the stomach, it ferments and produces turbid dampness and heat. These pathological byproducts rise upward on the inner vapors of digestion, coating the tongue with a thick, greasy layer that is often foul-smelling. The coating is thickest in the centre of the tongue, which mirrors the stomach area. Because this is a pattern of excess obstruction rather than organ damage, the tongue body itself typically looks normal.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: normal body with a thick, greasy, curd-like coating thickest in the centre. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and full, especially at the right middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Food stagnation is an excess condition where undigested food ferments, producing turbid dampness and heat that steam upward to form the coating. Overeating and heavy foods directly overload the stomach, worsening the stagnation and thickening the fur. Light eating and skipping meals reduce the digestive burden, while gentle movement helps Qi descend, carrying the turbid matter downward. Emotional stress tightens the Qi mechanism, impairing the stomach's ability to move food along, so calm and rest are essential.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat small, light meals and chew food thoroughly. Avoid heavy, greasy, sweet, or cold-raw foods that burden digestion. A skipped meal or a day of light congee can give the stomach a chance to clear itself. Gentle walking after eating helps Qi descend and prevents stagnation.

### Stomach Fire (Stomach Heat)
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/stomach-fire-stomach-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Burning sensation in the stomach area, Constant hunger or large appetite, Foul breath, Swollen, painful, or bleeding gums, Thirst with desire for cold drinks, Red tongue body with central prickles
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy and greasy food, Alcohol and coffee, Anger and frustration, Overeating, Hot weather
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cold drinks, Cooling foods like cucumber, Rest and relaxation, Gentle walking, Deep breathing
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The stomach is the origin of the tongue coating. When excessive heat blazes in the stomach, it consumes fluids and steams upward, scorching the coating like a pot boiling dry. This produces the characteristic thick, dry, yellow fur, often most pronounced in the center of the tongue where the stomach is reflected.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: Red body with possible central prickles, thick dry yellow coat. Pulse: Rapid (数 shuò), slippery (滑 huá), and forceful, especially in the right Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Stomach Fire is an excess heat pattern, so anything that adds heat-spicy foods, alcohol, anger-will stoke the fire and worsen the coating. Cooling foods and drinks directly counteract the heat and soothe the stomach. Overeating burdens the stomach and generates more heat, while rest and moderate movement help Qi flow smoothly without overheating.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which add heat. Eat cooling, moistening foods like cucumber, watermelon, pear, and mung bean soup. Drink plenty of room-temperature water and eat at regular times without rushing. Gentle daily movement helps Qi flow and prevents stagnation.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Why does my tongue have a thick white coating?
In TCM, a thick white coating usually points to dampness or phlegm in the digestive system. When the Spleen is weak, it fails to transform fluids, and they accumulate as a white, greasy fur. This often comes with bloating, fatigue, and a heavy feeling in the body. It can also be a sign of food stagnation if the coating is curd-like and foul-smelling. Your practitioner will look at the texture and accompanying symptoms to distinguish between Spleen deficiency, damp-phlegm, or food stagnation.

### What does a yellow tongue coating mean in TCM?
A yellow coating indicates heat - either damp-heat or Stomach fire. If the coating is thick, greasy, and yellow, it's likely damp-heat, often from rich, spicy foods or a hot, humid environment. If it's dry, thick, and yellow, especially in the centre, it points to Stomach fire, which burns fluids and causes thirst, bad breath, and a burning sensation in the stomach. The treatment will clear heat and dry dampness, or cool the Stomach, depending on the exact pattern.

### Can a coated tongue be a sign of something serious?
Most coated tongues reflect temporary digestive imbalances and are not dangerous. However, a coating that turns black, is accompanied by severe pain or difficulty swallowing, or appears with unexplained weight loss or high fever should be evaluated by a doctor. For more on warning signs, see the Safety section below.

### Will scraping my tongue get rid of the coating permanently?
Tongue scraping removes the surface coating but does not address the internal imbalance that produces it. The coating will usually return within hours or days. TCM aims to resolve the root cause - whether it's Spleen weakness, dampness, heat, or food stagnation - so that the coating clears naturally and stays away. Scraping can be a helpful hygiene practice, but it's not a cure.

### How long does it take for TCM to clear a coated tongue?
Most people notice the coating thinning within 2-3 weeks of consistent herbal treatment and dietary changes. Full resolution often takes 4-8 weeks for excess patterns like Damp-Heat or Stomach Fire. Spleen deficiency patterns, which involve rebuilding the organ's function, may require 6-12 weeks. Acupuncture can speed up the process, but diet is key - if you go back to eating foods that generate dampness or heat, the coating may reappear.

### Do I need to change my diet to get rid of a coated tongue?
Yes, diet is essential. Foods that create dampness and heat - dairy, sugar, fried and greasy foods, alcohol, and excessive raw or cold items - must be minimized or avoided. Instead, eat warm, cooked meals like soups, congee, and steamed vegetables. Bitter greens can help clear heat, and small, frequent meals support weak digestion. Without dietary adjustments, herbal treatment will be less effective and the coating may return.

### Is a coated tongue related to bad breath?
Often, yes. In TCM, bad breath (halitosis) frequently comes from the same patterns that cause a coated tongue - particularly Stomach fire or food stagnation. When heat or undigested food ferments in the stomach, the turbid vapors rise and coat the tongue, causing a foul smell. Treating the underlying pattern usually improves both the coating and the breath.

### Can acupuncture help with a coated tongue?
Acupuncture is a valuable support. Points like Zusanli (ST-36), Yinlingquan (SP-9), and Zhongwan (REN-12) strengthen the Spleen and Stomach, drain dampness, and regulate Qi. It won't clear the coating overnight, but regular sessions alongside herbs can accelerate healing and improve overall digestion. Many patients report feeling lighter and more energetic as the coating clears.

1. **Tongue coating microbiome in patients with chronic gastritis**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Sun J, et al. Journal of Traditional Chinese Medicine, 2019*
   This study found that patients with chronic gastritis and a thick greasy coating had distinct tongue microbiome profiles compared to those with a thin coating, supporting TCM's use of tongue inspection for digestive assessment.

   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhang L, et al. Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine, 2020*
   The study showed that a thick yellow coating was significantly associated with epigastric burning and acid reflux, while a thick white coating correlated with bloating and early satiety.

3. **Acupuncture for functional dyspepsia: a systematic review and meta-analysis**
   *วารสารวิชาการ: Wang Y, et al. Frontiers in Medicine, 2021*
   A 2021 systematic review found that acupuncture combined with herbal medicine improved digestive symptoms and quality of life in patients with dyspepsia, indirectly supporting the treatment of coated tongue patterns.
