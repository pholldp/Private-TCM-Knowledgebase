## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
In Western medicine, retention of a dead fetus - often called a missed miscarriage or retained products of conception - is diagnosed when fetal demise has occurred but the body does not spontaneously pass the tissue. It is typically confirmed by ultrasound showing an absence of cardiac activity or an empty gestational sac. Symptoms may include a loss of pregnancy symptoms, vaginal bleeding, or cramping, though sometimes no outward signs appear until a scan reveals the loss.
Standard management includes expectant waiting, medication (misoprostol) to induce uterine contractions, or surgical evacuation via dilation and curettage (D&C). The choice depends on gestational age, maternal health, and risk of infection or hemorrhage. While these methods are effective at clearing the uterus, they do not address the constitutional factors that may have contributed to the fetal demise or the body’s inability to complete the miscarriage on its own.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment offers three main paths: watchful waiting to see if the body expels the tissue naturally over days to weeks; medical management with prostaglandins like misoprostol to stimulate uterine contractions; and surgical evacuation (D&C) to remove the contents directly. Antibiotics may be prescribed if infection is suspected, and Rh immunoglobulin is given to Rh-negative women. These interventions are highly effective at resolving the immediate physical retention, but they do not address the underlying maternal depletion or circulatory imbalance that may have led to the loss and the body’s failure to expel it.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
In TCM, the uterus’s ability to expel its contents depends on two things: sufficient Qi to push downward and adequate Blood to lubricate the passage. When the fetus dies, the body should recognize it as a foreign object and initiate a natural expulsion. If that does not happen, it signals a deeper imbalance - either the mother’s vital forces are too depleted to generate the necessary uterine contractions, or some form of obstruction (stagnant Blood, dampness, or Heat) is blocking the way.
Qi and Blood Deficiency is the most straightforward pattern: the mother is so exhausted and undernourished that her uterus lacks the energy to contract effectively. The pain is dull and persistent rather than sharp, the bleeding is pale and watery, and the woman may feel cold and utterly drained. Blood Stagnation, on the other hand, presents with fixed, stabbing pain and dark, clotted discharge - here, congealed blood physically locks the dead tissue in place, often following trauma, emotional shock, or prolonged Qi constraint.
Two other patterns involve the interplay of the Spleen and Liver. When the Spleen is weak, dampness accumulates in the pelvis, creating a heavy, sticky obstruction that prevents expulsion; the woman feels bloated and heavy, with a thick greasy tongue coating.
When intense emotional distress - especially suppressed anger - causes Liver Qi to stagnate and generate Heat, that Heat enters the Blood, congealing it and creating a toxic, inflammatory blockage. In all cases, the TCM practitioner reads the type of pain, the quality of discharge, and the tongue and pulse to identify which mechanism is dominant and to select a treatment that will gently but effectively resolve it.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A TCM practitioner first assesses the mother's overall vitality and the quality of her pain. If she appears very pale, exhausted, and describes a dull, persistent ache rather than sharp pain, the practitioner suspects a Qi and Blood Deficiency pattern. The tongue is usually pale with a thin white coating, and the pulse feels weak and thready - signs that the body lacks the energy to push the dead fetus out.
When the pain is severe, stabbing, and fixed in one spot, and the vaginal discharge is dark purple with clots, the focus shifts to Blood Stagnation. The complexion may look bluish or dark, and the tongue often appears purple with possible dark spots. The pulse feels choppy, like a river hitting rocks, indicating that congealed blood is physically blocking the uterus.
If the woman complains more of a heavy, distended sensation in the lower abdomen and chest, along with a sticky, yellowish or turbid discharge, the practitioner thinks of Spleen Dampness with Liver Qi Stagnation. The tongue coating is thick and greasy, and the pulse may feel slippery. This pattern often comes with poor appetite and a sensation of fullness after eating, revealing that dampness and stagnant Qi are gumming up the works.
Emotional signs like pronounced irritability, a bitter taste in the mouth, and a feeling of heat point toward Liver Qi Stagnation transforming into Heat. Here the tongue is red with a yellow coating, and the pulse is wiry and rapid. The discharge may be bright red. This pattern suggests that pent-up anger or frustration has generated fire, which then scorches the blood and creates a toxic blockage that keeps the fetus trapped.

## กลุ่มอาการการแพทย์แผนจีนและการรักษา (TCM Patterns and Treatment)

### Qi and Blood Deficiency
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/qi-and-blood-deficiency)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dull, lingering lower abdominal pain, Scant, pale, watery vaginal discharge, Extreme fatigue and weakness, Pale or sallow complexion
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Physical exertion or overwork, Cold food and drink, Anxiety and worry
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm, cooked meals, Complete bed rest, Gentle warmth on the abdomen
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When the mother's Qi and Blood are severely depleted, the uterus-relying on Qi for pushing and Blood for nourishing-loses the strength and lubrication needed to expel the dead fetus. Qi deficiency means there is no force to drive it out, while Blood deficiency leaves the uterus dry and unable to pass its contents smoothly. This is why the fetus remains retained, often for many days, with only a scant, pale discharge.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale body, thin white coating. Pulse: thin and weak, lacking force.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: This pattern is defined by a profound lack of Qi and Blood. Physical exertion depletes Qi, while cold foods damage the Spleen's ability to generate more. Rest and warm, nourishing foods do the opposite, conserving precious resources and gently supporting the Spleen to rebuild the energy and blood needed to complete the expulsion.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Focus on warm, easily digestible, nutrient-dense foods like congee, bone broth, and cooked vegetables. Avoid raw, cold, or greasy foods that further deplete Spleen Qi. Absolute rest is critical to conserve energy.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing lower abdominal pain, Dark or purplish vaginal discharge with clots, Pain worse with pressure on the abdomen, Pain and bleeding worse at night, Dark or dusky facial complexion and lips
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold food and drink, Stress, anger, or frustration, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Cold weather exposure
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle warmth on the abdomen, Gentle movement, Emotional calm and stress reduction
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  Blood Stagnation obstructs the uterus after the fetus dies. This pattern often follows physical trauma, emotional shock, or prolonged Qi constraint that congeals into Blood stasis. The stagnant blood forms a palpable mass that locks the dead fetus in place, making it impossible for the uterus to contract and expel it. The obstruction causes severe, fixed pain and forces blood out through any available channel, producing the characteristic dark, clotted discharge.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: dark purple body with possible stasis spots, thin white coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè), rough and uneven like a knife scraping bamboo.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold constricts blood vessels and congeals Blood, directly worsening the underlying Stasis that traps the dead fetus. Anger and frustration cause Liver Qi to stagnate, which is the root of Blood Stasis; the choppy pulse reflects this rough, interrupted flow. Warmth and gentle movement counteract this by promoting the smooth flow of Qi and Blood, helping the uterus regain the strength to expel its contents.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid cold, raw foods and cold drinks, which can constrict blood vessels and worsen stasis. Gentle warmth, such as a warm compress on the lower abdomen, can help move stagnant blood. Emotional calm is essential, as anger and frustration worsen Qi and Blood stagnation.

### Obstruction Of the Spleen By Dampness with Liver Qi Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/obstruction-of-the-spleen-by-dampness-with-liver-qi-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Lower abdominal cold pain or distension, Sticky yellow vaginal discharge, Chest and abdominal fullness, Bad breath, Emotional frustration or low mood, Heaviness of body and limbs
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Cold, damp weather, Greasy or heavy foods, Stress, anger, or frustration, Prolonged sitting or inactivity
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle warmth on the abdomen, Light, easily digestible meals, Gentle movement, Emotional calm and stress reduction
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  The Spleen transforms fluids; when it is weak, dampness builds up and congeals in the uterus, creating a heavy, obstructed environment. At the same time, Liver Qi stagnation adds to the blockage, preventing the uterus from contracting effectively to expel the dead fetus. The result is a sensation of fullness and cold pain, with a sticky discharge that reflects the dampness clogging the system.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: pale and swollen with teeth marks, white greasy coating, excessively moist. Pulse: slippery (滑 huá) and wiry (弦 xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Dampness is a heavy, cold pathogen that thrives on greasy foods and cold environments, so warmth and light meals help dry it out. Stress tightens Liver Qi, which further obstructs the uterus, so emotional calm and gentle movement keep Qi flowing and support expulsion.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat warm, cooked foods like congee with ginger and avoid cold, raw, or greasy items that create dampness. Gentle walking helps Qi flow, but rest when tired. Emotional calm is essential; frustration worsens stagnation.

### Liver Qi Stagnation that transforms into Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/liver-qi-stagnation-that-transforms-into-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Irritability and explosive anger, Bitter taste in the mouth, Distending pain in the ribcage, Fresh red vaginal bleeding, Red face and eyes, Dry mouth and throat
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Stress, anger, or frustration, Spicy or greasy food, Alcohol, Overwork and heat exposure
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cooling foods and drinks, Emotional calm and stress reduction, Gentle movement, Hydration with water
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When intense emotional distress-especially anger and frustration-is suppressed, the Liver Qi stagnates and eventually generates Heat. This Heat enters the Blood, congealing it and creating a hot, toxic obstruction in the uterus. As a result, the dead fetus becomes trapped and cannot be expelled, while symptoms like irritability, bitter taste, and fresh red bleeding appear.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body with redder sides, yellow coating. Pulse: wiry and rapid (弦数, xián shuò), especially at the left middle position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Emotional stress directly fuels Liver Qi stagnation and generates heat, which congeals blood and traps the dead fetus. Cooling foods and calming practices clear heat and smooth Qi, helping to resolve the obstruction and allow the body to expel the fetus naturally.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid spicy, greasy, and fried foods, as well as alcohol and coffee, which add heat. Focus on cooling, light meals like mung bean soup, celery, and chrysanthemum tea. Gentle stress management, such as walking or meditation, helps smooth Liver Qi.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Can TCM help me pass a missed miscarriage naturally?
Yes, in many cases TCM herbs and acupuncture can encourage the body to expel a dead fetus when the underlying pattern is correctly identified. However, this must be done under the close supervision of both a qualified TCM practitioner and a medical doctor. If the tissue does not pass within a few days, or if there is any sign of heavy bleeding or infection, surgical management should be pursued immediately. TCM is not a substitute for emergency care.

### Is it safe to use Chinese herbs for retained products of conception?
When prescribed by a licensed TCM practitioner who is aware of your full medical history, the herbs used for this condition are generally safe and are selected to gently stimulate uterine contractions and move blood without causing excessive bleeding. However, some herbs can interact with medications like misoprostol or anticoagulants, so it is critical that your doctor and TCM practitioner communicate. Never take over-the-counter herbal products for this purpose without professional guidance.

### How long does it take for TCM to work in a missed miscarriage?
Herbal formulas aimed at expelling the dead tissue are usually given for 3 to 7 days. If there is no progress within that window, a follow-up ultrasound and possible surgical intervention are recommended. Acupuncture may be used alongside herbs to help stimulate uterine contractions and relieve pain. The subsequent recovery phase to rebuild the mother’s strength can last several weeks to months.

### What if I have already had a D&C - can TCM still help?
Absolutely. After a surgical evacuation, TCM focuses on restoring the Qi and Blood that are inevitably depleted by the procedure and the emotional loss. Herbal formulas and acupuncture can help regulate the menstrual cycle, improve energy, and prepare the uterus for a future healthy pregnancy. Many women find that TCM provides a much-needed sense of holistic recovery during the grieving process.

### Will acupuncture cause more bleeding or pain?
No, when performed by a skilled practitioner, acupuncture for this condition is gentle and aims to regulate uterine contractions and relieve pain, not to cause trauma. Points are chosen to move Qi and Blood in the lower abdomen while also calming the mind. It is not typically painful, and any spotting afterward is usually mild and expected as part of the expulsion process.

### Are there any foods I should eat or avoid during this time?
Warm, cooked, and easily digestible foods are best - think congee, bone broth, and stewed fruits. Avoid cold, raw, and greasy foods, which can weaken the Spleen and worsen dampness. Spicy and heating foods should be avoided if there are signs of Heat. After the tissue has passed, focus on nourishing foods like organic meats, eggs, and dark leafy greens to rebuild Blood. Your TCM practitioner can give you personalized dietary advice based on your pattern.

### Can TCM prevent a missed miscarriage in the future?
TCM cannot guarantee prevention, but it can address the constitutional imbalances that may have contributed to the loss. By strengthening Qi and Blood, regulating the Liver, and resolving any chronic dampness or stasis, TCM aims to create a more hospitable environment for a future pregnancy. Many women choose to work with a TCM practitioner for several months before trying to conceive again.
