## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนปัจจุบัน (Conventional Medicine View)
### คำอธิบาย (Description)
Western medicine generally considers menstrual blood colour a normal variation influenced by how quickly blood leaves the body and how long it has been exposed to air. Bright red blood usually means fresh, rapid shedding, while dark red or brown blood is older blood that has oxidised. It is not a primary diagnostic marker, but unusually heavy bleeding, large clots, or severe pain may prompt investigation for fibroids, endometriosis, or hormonal imbalances.

### การรักษาหลัก (Treatments)
Conventional treatment focuses on managing the underlying cause of heavy or painful periods rather than the colour itself. Options may include nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) for pain, tranexamic acid to reduce heavy flow, hormonal contraceptives to regulate the cycle, or surgical interventions for structural problems like fibroids. If no pathology is found, reassurance is often the main approach.

## มุมมองทางการแพทย์แผนจีน (Traditional Chinese Medicine View)
### ความเข้าใจเกี่ยวกับการแพทย์แผนจีน (TCM Understanding)
TCM views menstrual blood as a direct reflection of the Uterus (Bāo Gōng) and the state of the Chōng and Rèn meridians - the two extraordinary vessels that govern menstrual health. When these vessels are balanced, the blood flows smoothly, with a healthy red colour and without pain. But when Heat enters the Uterus, it stirs the blood like boiling water, forcing it out early and giving it a bright red or dark red, sticky appearance. This is the pattern called Heat in Uterus Blood, and it often comes with a heavy flow, thirst, and irritability.
When Qi becomes stuck - often from emotional stress, prolonged sitting, or a history of cold exposure - the blood cannot move freely through the uterine vessels. It congeals and stagnates, turning the menstrual blood a dark red or purplish colour with clots. This is Blood Stagnation, and its hallmark is sharp, fixed cramping that eases once a clot passes. The tongue often looks dusky or has purple spots, and the pulse feels choppy, as if something is blocking the flow.
In some women, Heat and Stagnation combine. The Heat thickens the blood, making it more likely to stagnate, while the Stagnation traps the Heat, creating a cycle. The period blood is dark red with clots, yet there are also signs of Heat like a dry mouth, a feeling of warmth, and a rapid pulse. Recognising this mixed pattern is essential because treatment must both cool the Heat and move the Stagnation - just doing one or the other will not fully resolve the problem.

### การวินิจฉัยโดยแพทย์แผนจีน (Practitioner Diagnosis)
A practitioner begins by looking at the colour itself. Bright red, almost fresh-looking blood is the strongest clue for Heat in the Uterus Blood. This pattern often comes with a heavier flow, and the blood may feel thicker or stickier than usual. The person might also feel unusually thirsty, warm, or irritable around their period, and their tongue will typically appear red with a yellow coating.
When the blood is dark red, purplish, or contains many dark clots, the picture shifts toward Blood Stagnation. Here the flow is often scanty or starts and stops, and cramping pain that feels better once a clot passes is a classic sign. The tongue may look dusky or have small purple spots, and the pulse often feels choppy or wiry, as if something is blocking the smooth movement of blood.
Sometimes the two pictures blend together into Blood Stagnation with Heat. In this mixed pattern the blood is dark red with clots, yet there are also signs of heat like a dry mouth, a feeling of warmth, or a tongue that is red with a yellow coat. The pain tends to be sharper and more fixed, and the pulse can feel rapid and wiry, reflecting both heat and stagnation at the same time.

### Heat in Uterus Blood
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/heat-in-uterus-blood)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Bright red or dark red sticky blood, Periods arrive early (7&#x2B; days ahead), Heavy flow with feeling of heat, Thirst for cold drinks, dry mouth, Irritability and restlessness
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Emotional stress, frustration, or anger, Hot weather or overheated rooms, Alcohol and coffee
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Cooling foods and drinks, Rest and calm environment, Cool showers or compresses
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When excess heat accumulates in the uterus, it stirs the blood and forces it to move recklessly. This makes the menstrual flow arrive early and appear bright red or dark red, because heat gives blood a vivid color and can thicken it into a sticky consistency. The heat also irritates the mind and body, causing restlessness, thirst, and a sensation of internal warmth.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: red body, especially at the tip, with possible prickles; thin yellow coating, tends to be dry. Pulse: rapid (Shu) and wiry (Xian) or slippery (Hua).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: <p>Heat in the Uterus is an excess heat condition. Spicy foods, alcohol, and emotional stress all generate more internal heat, which further stirs the blood and worsens the bright red, heavy flow. Cooling foods, rest, and a calm environment help drain that excess heat and settle the blood, bringing the menstrual cycle back into balance.</p>
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Avoid heating foods like spicy dishes, alcohol, coffee, and greasy fried foods. Instead, enjoy cooling, hydrating foods such as cucumber, watermelon, mung bean soup, and leafy greens. Keep stress in check with gentle movement like walking or yoga, and ensure you stay well hydrated.

### Blood Stagnation
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Dark or purplish menstrual blood, Clots in menstrual blood, Sharp, stabbing cramps that are worse with pressure, Pain improves after clots pass, Dusky or dark facial complexion
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Emotional stress, frustration, or anger, Cold foods and drinks, Exposure to cold, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Pressure on the abdomen
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Warm compress on lower abdomen, Gentle walking or stretching, Passing clots, Warm ginger tea, Resting in a comfortable position
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When Qi flow is obstructed, blood cannot move freely through the uterine vessels. It congeals and stagnates, turning the menstrual blood a dark red or purplish colour. The clotted blood then causes sharp, fixed cramps as the uterus tries to expel the obstructions.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: purple or dusky body with stasis spots, distended dark veins underneath. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè) or wiry (弦 xián).
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Cold makes blood congeal and stagnate, so warmth and movement are the natural antidotes. Stress knots the Liver Qi, which is the engine of blood circulation; when Qi stalls, blood clots form. Gentle exercise and warm drinks promote smooth flow, while passing clots physically removes the obstruction, giving immediate relief.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Keep the lower abdomen warm, especially during your period, and avoid cold foods and drinks. Gentle daily exercise like walking or yoga helps keep Qi and blood moving. Include warming, blood-nourishing foods such as turmeric, ginger, dark leafy greens, and small amounts of lean red meat.

### Blood Stagnation with Heat
*ลิงก์รายละเอียดกลุ่มอาการ: [หน้าหลักข้อมูลกลุ่มอาการ (ภาษาอังกฤษ)](https://www.meandqi.com/knowledge-base/patterns/blood-stagnation-with-heat)*

- **สัญญาณและอาการทางคลินิก (Clinical Signs)**: Fixed, stabbing lower abdominal pain, Pain worse at night and with pressure, Dark red or purplish menstrual blood with clots, Sensation of internal heat, especially at night, Irritability and restlessness, Reddish-purple tongue with stasis spots
- **สิ่งกระตุ้นให้อาการแย่ลง (Worse with)**: Spicy, greasy, or fried foods, Emotional stress, frustration, or anger, Prolonged sitting or inactivity, Hot weather or overheated rooms
- **สิ่งช่วยให้อาการดีขึ้น (Better with)**: Gentle walking or stretching, Cooling foods and drinks, Stress reduction
- **กลไกการเกิดอาการ (Why this happens)**:
  When heat lingers in the body, it can thicken and congeal the blood, creating stasis. This stagnant blood obstructs the uterine vessels, so the menstrual flow becomes dark red or purplish with clots. The heat adds restlessness and a sensation of internal warmth, while the stasis causes sharp, fixed pain that often worsens at night.
- **ลักษณะลิ้นและชีพจร (Tongue & Pulse)**: Tongue: reddish-purple body with stasis spots and distended sublingual veins; thin dry yellow coat. Pulse: choppy (涩 sè) and rapid (数 shuò), often wiry (弦 xián) at the left Guan position.
- **อธิบายสิ่งกระตุ้นและการทุเลาอาการ (Triggers & Relief Explanation)**: Spicy and greasy foods add heat to the body, which thickens the blood and aggravates stasis. Frustration and anger stagnate Liver Qi, making blood stasis worse. Gentle movement keeps Qi and blood circulating, preventing stasis from settling, while cooling foods and stress reduction clear heat and calm the mind, reducing the restlessness and night-time pain.
- **คำแนะนำด้านอาหารและการดำเนินชีวิต (Diet & Lifestyle)**: Eat cooling, blood-moving foods like celery, eggplant, and a small amount of hawthorn. Avoid spicy, greasy, and overly heating foods. Gentle walking or yoga helps keep Qi and blood flowing without overheating the body. Manage stress with breathing exercises, as emotional tension worsens stasis.

## คำถามที่พบบ่อย (Frequently Asked Questions)
### Does bright red blood always mean I have Heat?
Not always, but it is the most common TCM interpretation. Bright red, fresh-looking blood that comes with a heavy flow, feeling hot, and thirst is a strong Heat sign. However, if your flow is scanty and you have no other Heat symptoms, the colour could simply reflect a rapid shedding that day. A TCM practitioner will look at your tongue, pulse, and full symptom picture before confirming the pattern.

### Can acupuncture and herbs really change my period blood colour?
Yes. Blood colour is a reflection of the internal environment, and as that environment shifts with treatment, the colour often changes - sometimes within a single cycle. For example, when Heat is cleared, the blood may shift from bright red to a more normal deep red. When Stagnation is moved, clots diminish and the colour lightens. These changes are among the most reliable signs that the treatment is working.

### How long until I see a difference in my period?
Many women notice some change after the first full cycle on herbs, but meaningful, stable improvement usually takes two to three cycles. Acupuncture once or twice a week can speed this process. Patience is important, especially with long-standing Stagnation or mixed patterns - the body needs time to re-establish a healthy rhythm.

### Can I take Chinese herbs while on birth control?
In most cases, yes, but always tell both your TCM practitioner and your prescribing doctor. There are no known direct interactions between common menstrual-regulating herbs and hormonal contraceptives, but herbs that strongly move Blood (such as Táo Rén or Hóng Huā) could theoretically affect bleeding patterns. Your practitioner may adjust the formula to ensure safety and harmony with your medication.

### Is it safe to treat period colour changes during pregnancy?
No. If you are pregnant or could be pregnant, do not take any herbs or have certain acupuncture points stimulated without explicit guidance from a qualified TCM practitioner experienced in pregnancy care. Many Blood-moving herbs are contraindicated in pregnancy. Any bleeding during pregnancy should be evaluated urgently by your doctor. See our Safety section for red flags.

### Do I need to change my diet to see results?
Diet supports treatment but does not replace it. If you have a Heat pattern, avoiding spicy, greasy, and fried foods while eating more cooling foods like cucumber and watermelon can help. For Stagnation, warming, cooked foods and gentle spices like ginger are beneficial. Your practitioner will give you specific guidance, but even small shifts can make a noticeable difference.

   *วารสารวิชาการ: Zhu X, Proctor M, Bensoussan A, Wu E, Smith CA. Chinese herbal medicine for primary dysmenorrhoea. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews 2007, Issue 4. Art. No.: CD005288.*
   This Cochrane systematic review assessed the effectiveness of Chinese herbal medicine for period pain.
