# Ding Jing Tang (定经汤)
**English Name**: Settling the Menses Decoction  
**Category**: Menstruation-regulating formulas  
**Dynasty/Source**: Likely from a work by Chen Shiduo (陈士铎), possibly the Bian Zheng Lu (辨证录) or Shi Shi Mi Lu (石室秘录), Qing dynasty — exact source text could not be definitively verified.  
**Temperature**: Neutral | **Form**: Decoction (Tang)

---

## Summary
A classical gynecological formula from Fu Qingzhu's Gynecology designed to regulate irregular menstrual cycles. It works by soothing the Liver's free flow of Qi and nourishing the Kidney's essence, addressing the root cause when periods come sometimes early, sometimes late, with no predictable pattern.

- **Typical Duration**: Typically prescribed for 2–4 weeks with reassessment, as spirit-calming formulas often require sustained use. Exact duration depends on the specific condition being treated.
